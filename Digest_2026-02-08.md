# TYPICON: SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 8th, 2026.
## AFTERFEAST OF THE ENCOUNTER & SUNDAY OF THE LAST JUDGEMENT - TONE II
Great Martyr Theodore Stratelates. Prophet Zachariah.  
Sunday service combined with the triodion.  
The service to St. Great Martyr Theodore Stratelates. Prophet Zachariah. is transferred to the previous Friday at Compline, or another convenient time, whenever the ecclesiarch so wishes.  
**Vestment colour:** Bright [Gold or blue for the feast].  
## GREAT VESPERS
**At O Lord, I have cried…** we sing 3 resurrectional stichera from the Octoechos, 4 Stichera from the Triodion, 3 Feast from the Menaion; Glory... doxasticon from the Triodion; Both now... Dogmatic Theotokion in the tone of the week.  
**If Litiya is performed:** The sticheron of the temple.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 1 Aposticha Resurrection 1 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 2 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 3 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 4 from the Octoechos; Glory... doxasticon from the Triodion; Both now... doxasticon of the feast.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:**
We sing We sing the Sunday (resurrectional) troparion in the tone of the week; Glory, both now... Theotokion Dismissal in Tone 2.  
Or, if Vigil is served: Rejoice, O Virgin Theotokos... twice; troparion of the feast, once.  
## MATINS
After each cathisma we sing the sessional hymns from the Octoechos.  
We sing the Polyeleos, and we add Psalm 136 (By the waters of Babylon). Magnification: sung if prescribed.  
After, the Evlogitaria: the Hypakoë (Tone II), Hymns of Ascents, Prokeimenon, Let everything that has breath: in the tone of the week.  
**Matins Gospel II**: Mk. 16: 1-8.  
After Psalm 50: instead of the usual hymns, we sing: Glory: Open to me the doors of repentance..., Both now: On the paths of salvation... and after the refrain Have mercy on me, O God, the sticheron: When I think of the many evil things I have done.  
**At the Canon (abbreviated for parish use):**  
  At each ode, the irmos of the resurrection from the Octoechos.  
  1 troparion of the resurrection.  ℟. Glory to Your holy resurrection, O Lord!  
  2 troparia from the Triodion.  ℟.  Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me!  
  Glory... both now... Triodion.  
  Catabasia Tone VI "When Isreael passed on foot...".  
Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Resurrection - 4 (including the irmos), Theotokos - 2, Triodion - 4, Menaion - 4.  
After Ode III: Sessional hymns from the Triodion (found after Ode III).  
After Ode VI: kontakion and ikos from the Triodion are sung.  
After Ode VIII: we sing the Magnificat.  
Holy is the Lord... Tone II.  
**Exapostilarion** - Eothinon 2; Eothinon 2 Theotokion.  
**At the Praises:** We sing 9 stichera: 5 from the Octoechos, and 4 from the Triodion; Glory... Glory to God; Other: Now and Ever.  
After the Dismissal of Matins: Glory... both now... Gospel Sticheron II.  
## HOURS
At all the hours: Resurrectional troparion, Glory... troparion of the feast.
Kontakia: at the First and Sixth – Triodion; at the Third and Ninth – Feast.
## DIVINE LITURGY
**Troparia and Kontakia:**
Troparion of the Resurrection in Tone 2.
Troparion of the Feast.
Glory... Kontakion of the Triodion.
Both now... Kontakion of the Feast.

**Alleluia:**		Tone II
**Gospel:**		Mt 25:31-46

**Alleluia:**		Tone VIII
