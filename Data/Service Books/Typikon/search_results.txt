153: Midnight Office
154: "The Midnight Office has three forms: Daily, Saturday and Sunday. The Daily and Saturday have one form, although different prayers, and their order is placed in the Chasoslovets i Molitvoslov (lit. "Horo- and Prayer Book"). The Order of the Sunday [Midnight Office] is also given in the Horologion and Prayer Book; however, the Triadic Canon must be taken from the Octoechos of the current tone because the Canon placed in the Chasoslovets i Molitvoslov, of which each of the eight odes is taken from one of the eight tones of the Octoechos, is said only in private.
155: According to the Typikon the time of the Midnight Office is midnight itself; but according to our current custom we celebrate it in the evening before, or in the morning after midnight."

Therefore, both the former and the latter are good, for it is possible to ask the Lord God for a blessing at the conclusion of the Divine Service, just as is asked at Vespers with Vigil, at the beginning of which the Deacon, or the Priest himself, exclaims: Κύριε ευλογησον /"Lord, bless"/.
