HYMNS TO THE TRINITY AND HYMNS OF LIGHT (SVETILEN) 
AT MATINS IN THE EIGHT TONES FROM THE OKTOECHOS 
Note: At Matins on Weekdays during Lent, in place: God is the Lord ..., we 
chant Alleluia with the appointed verses, and then the following Hymns to the 
Holy Trinity in the given Tone of the week. At the end of the canon, we 
likewise sing the appointed Hymn of light (Svetilen/Exapostilarion) in the given 
Tone of the week, repeating it thrice. The ending of these Hymns varies 
according to the day of the week as follows:  
In the Hymns to the Trinity, after: Holy, holy, holy art Thou, our God, we 
end the first
On Monday: Through the protection of Thine angels, have mercy upon 
us. 
 Hymn thus: 
On Tuesday: By the prayers of Thy Forerunner, have mercy upon us. 
On Wednesday and Friday: By the power of Thy Cross, O Lord, save us. 
On Thursday: By the prayers of Thine apostles and St. Nicolas, have 
mercy upon us. 
Note: In the Hymn of Light (Exapostilarion or Exapostilarion), when it is said 
the first time
On Monday: Through the protection of Thine angels, O Lord, and save 
me. 
 we end the hymn thus: 
On Tuesday: By the prayers of Thy Forerunner, O Lord, and save me.  
On Wednesday and Friday: By the power of Thy Cross, O Lord, and 
save me.  
On Thursday: By the prayers of Thine apostles and St. Nicolas, O Lord, 
and save me. .

 time we end:

As Thou art God who hast dominion over both the living and the dead, *

TONE ONE 
Hymns to the Trinity: 
By means of material imagery * let us ascend to the noetic and immaterial 
understanding of the bodiless powers; * singing the Thrice-Holy Hymn, * 
awaiting the illumination of the Divinity in three Hypostases, * let us cry aloud 
as do the cherubim * unto the one and only God: * Holy, holy, holy art Thou, 
our God; ** (ending above) 
Glory ...,  
With all the hosts of heaven, * let us offer the Thrice-Holy Hymn of praise * 
and cry aloud as do the cherubim * unto Him who dwelleth on high: * Holy, 
holy, holy art Thou, our God; * by the prayers of all Thy saints, ** have mercy 
upon us. 
 Both now ..., 
Arising from sleep, we fall down before Thee, * O Lover of mankind, * and 
with the angels’ hymn * we cry aloud unto Thee, O All-powerful One: * Holy, 
holy, holy art Thou, our God; ** through the Theotokos have mercy upon us. 
Hymn of light (Svetilen/Exapostilarion): 
O Christ who doth make the light to shine, * cleanse Thou my soul from 
every sin..., ** (ending above) 
 
TONE TWO 
Hymns to the Trinity 
Let us on earth do as do the powers on high, * and offer to Thee, O Lover 
of mankind, * a hymn of victory: * Holy, holy, holy art Thou our God; ** 
(ending above)

Glory ...,  
O uncreated Essence, and Fashioner of all things, * do Thou open our lips 
that we may proclaim Thy praises, crying: * Holy, holy, holy, art Thou, our 
God; ** by the prayers of all Thy saints, have mercy upon us. 
 Both now ..., 
Having raised me from my bed and sleep O Lord; * enlighten my mind, and 
my heart and lips, that I may praise Thee, O Holy Trinity: * Holy, holy, holy art 
Thou, our God; ** through the Theotokos have mercy upon us. 
Hymn of light (Svetilen/Exapostilarion): 
Send forth Thine eternal light, O Christ my God, * and illumine the hidden 
eyes of my heart, ** (ending above) 
TONE THREE 
Hymns to the Trinity 
O Trinity, one in Essence and undivided, * a unity in three coeternal 
Hypostases, * to Thee as God we sing the angels’ hymn: * Holy, holy, holy art 
Thou, our God; ** (ending above) 
Glory ...,  
Daring to render glory to the one Godhead, * to the eternal Father, the 
coeternal Son, and beginningless Spirit, * we, like the cherubim, cry aloud: * 
Holy, holy, holy art Thou, our God; ** by the prayers of all Thy saints, have 
mercy upon us. 
 Both now ..., 
Suddenly the judge will come, * and the deeds of every person will be laid 
bare. * But at midnight let us cry with fear: * Holy, holy, holy art Thou, our 
God; ** through the Theotokos have mercy on us. 
Hymn of light (Svetilen/Exapostilarion): 
Send forth Thy light, O Christ my God, * and illumine my heart, ** (ending 
above)

TONE FOUR 
Hymns to the Trinity 
Daring, though we be mortal, * to render unto Thee the hymn of Thine 
angelic ministers, * we cry aloud saying: * Holy, holy, holy art Thou, our God; 
** (ending above) 
Glory ...,  
As do the angelic hosts in heaven, * we on earth, standing now with fear, 
offer unto Thee, O Lover of mankind, * a hymn of victory saying: * Holy, holy, 
holy art Thou, our God; * by the prayers of all Thy saints, have mercy upon us. 
 Both now ..., 
Daring to render glory to Thine eternal Father, * and to Thee, O Christ 
God, * and to Thy Most Holy Spirit, * like the cherubim we cry aloud saying: * 
Holy, holy, holy art Thou, our God; ** through the Theotokos have mercy 
upon us. 
Hymn of light (Svetilen/Exapostilarion): 
O Thou who dost make light shine forth upon Thy world, * cleanse my 
darkened soul from every sin, ** (ending above) 
 
TONE FIVE 
Hymns to the Trinity 
Now is the hour for praise and prayer; * let us fervently cry aloud to the one 
and only God: * Holy, holy, holy art Thou, our God; ** (ending above) 
Glory ...,  
We dare to be reflections of Thy noetic hosts, * O beginningless Trinity, * 
and with our unworthy mouths, we cry to Thee: * Holy, holy, holy art Thou, 
our God; ** by the prayers of all Thy saints, have mercy upon us. 
 Both now ..., 
Though Thou wast contained within a virgin womb O Christ God, * Thou 
wast not separated from the Father, * accept us who call upon Thee with the 
angelic hosts: * Holy, holy, holy art Thou, our God; ** through the Theotokos, 
have mercy upon us. 
Hymn of light (Svetilen/Exapostilarion): 
O Lord the Giver of Light, * send down Thy light and illumine my heart, ** 
(ending above)

Hymns to the Trinity 
With fear and trembling * standing before Thy face, * the cherubim and 
seraphim offer the Thrice-Holy Hymn * with never-silent voices. * And with 
them we sinners also cry aloud: * Holy, holy, holy art Thou, our God; ** 
(ending above) 
Glory ...,  
With never-silent hymns of glory, * the six-winged seraphim sing to Thee O 
God, * with their noetic voices, * offering Thee the Thrice-Holy Hymn; * and 
we on earth also render praise to Thee * from our unworthy lips: * Holy, holy, 
holy art Thou, our God; ** by the prayers of all Thy saints, have mercy upon us. 
 Both now ..., 
Let us glorify the Godhead, * three in one, united and yet unconfused in 
attributes, and let us sing the angels’ hymn: Holy, holy, holy art Thou, our God; 
** through the Theotokos have mercy upon us. 
Hymn of light (Svetilen/Exapostilarion): 
Send down upon our souls * Thine everlasting light, ** (ending above) 
 
TONE SEVEN 
Hymns to the Trinity 
Praised by the cherubim in Thine almighty power, * and worshipped by the 
angelic hosts in Thy divine glory, * do Thou also accept us sinners, who though 
unworthy, * dare to cry to Thee: * Holy, holy, holy art Thou, our God; ** 
(ending above) 
Glory ...,  
O my soul, casting aside sleep and slothfulness, * bring to the Judge a more 
fervent hymn of praise, * and cry aloud with fear and trembling: * Holy, holy, 
holy art Thou, our God; ** by the prayers of all Thy saints, have mercy upon us. 
 Both now ..., 
Unto the Godhead whom none can approach, * to the Trinity in Unity, let 
us offer the Thrice-Holy Hymn of the seraphim, * and cry aloud with fear and 
trembling: * Holy, holy, holy art Thou, our God; ** through the Theotokos 
have mercy upon us. 
Hymn of light (Svetilen/Exapostilarion): 

--- PAGE 6 ---
Stir me up to sing Thy praises, O Lord, * and teach me to do Thy will, O 
Holy One, ** (ending above) 
 
 TONE EIGHT 
Hymns to the Trinity 
Lifting up our hearts unto the heavens, * let us imitate the ranks of angels. * 
Let us fall down with fear before the righteous judge, * singing a triumphal 
hymn of praise: * Holy, holy, holy art Thou, our God; ** (ending above) 
Glory ...,  
Not daring gaze upon Thee, * but inspired by God, the six-winged 
cherubim cry out to Thee * with the words of the Thrice-Holy Hymn; * and 
with them we sinners also cry to Thee: * Holy, holy, holy art Thou, our God; ** 
by the prayers of all Thy saints, have mercy upon us. 
 Both now ..., 
Bowed down by the weight of our many iniquities, * we dare not look up at 
the height of the heavens; * but, obeisant in both soul and body, * with the 
angels we sing unto Thee: * Holy, holy, holy art Thou, our God; ** through the 
Theotokos have mercy upon us. 
Hymn of light (Svetilen/Exapostilarion): 
Thou art Light, O Christ, * fill me with Thine effulgent radiance, ** (ending 
above)
