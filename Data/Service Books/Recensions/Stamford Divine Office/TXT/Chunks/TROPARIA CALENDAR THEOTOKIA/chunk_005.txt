THEOTOKIA

(After the troparia of the saints during Vespers and Matins)

Tone One

Resurrectional – Saturday Vespers and Sunday Matins, and for a Polyeleos:

O holy tabernacle, when Gabriel cried out to you:* Rejoice O Virgin, full of grace,* the Lord of all became incarnate of you,* as the righteous David had foretold.* In bearing your Creator, you have shown yourself to surpass the vastness of the heavens.* We, therefore, cry out: Glory to Him Who dwelt in you!* Glory to Him Who came forth from you!* Glory to Him Who has set us free through your life-giving birth!

Sunday Vespers and Monday Matins after God the Lord...:

The universe recognizes in you, O Woman Full of Grace,* the marvel of marvels, and it rejoices.* You have borne without human seed* the One Whom even the highest ranks of angels cannot look upon,* and you have given Him birth in a manner beyond understanding.* Intercede with Him for the salvation of our souls.

Monday at the end of Matins:

Rejoice, O pure Mother of God,* blessed in heaven and glorified on earth!* Rejoice, O bride and Virgin forever!

Monday and Wednesday Vespers; and Tuesday and Thursday Matins after God the Lord...:

You have borne the fire of the Godhead without being burned;* you have given birth to the Lord, the Source life,* O Mother of God and woman full of grace,* save all those who exalt you.

Tuesday and Thursday at the end of Matins:

Without the assistance of a father on earth,* you have given birth in a manner beyond understanding* to the One Who was born without a mother in heaven.* O Mother of God, intercede with Him for our souls!

Tuesday and Thursday Vespers; Wednesday and Friday Matins after God the Lord...:

O pure One, we have obtained your protection.* Through your intercession, we have been kept from harm* and surrounded at all times with the grace of your Son’s cross.* Therefore, we exalt you with great devotion.

Wednesday and Friday at the end of Matins:

Rejoice, O pure Mother of God... (see above).

Friday Vespers and Saturday Matins after God the Lord...:

O holy Tabernacle... (see above).

Saturday at the end of Matins:

Without the assistance of a father... (see above).

Tone Two

Resurrectional – Saturday Vespers and Sunday Matins, and for a Polyeleos:

O Mother of God, the mysteries which surround you are exceedingly glorious* and beyond the power of understanding.* For you retained the seal of purity,* and your virginity remained inviolate;* yet you are acknowledged, without doubt,* to be the Mother who gave birth to the true God.* We beg you, therefore, to entreat Him to save our souls.

Sunday Vespers and Monday Matins after God the Lord...:

O Mother of God, you are a fountain of mercy;* count us worthy of your compassion;* look upon a people who have sinned;* manifest your power as you always do.* Because of our trust in you, we cry out to you* as once did Gabriel, the commander of the angels.

Monday at the end of Matins:

O holy Mother, Mother of the inexpressible Light, we honor you with angelic hymns, and we all exalt you with great devotion.

Monday and Wednesday Vespers; Tuesday and Thursday Matins after God the Lord...:

O Mother of God and virgin forever,* through you we have been made to share the divine nature;* you have given birth for us to the incarnate God.* Therefore, we all exalt you with great devotion.

Tuesday and Thursday at the end of Matins:

O Mother of God, we glorify you and cry out:* Rejoice, O cloud of the never-fading Light!* You have borne in your womb the very Lord of glory!

Tuesday and Thursday Vespers; Wednesday and Friday Matins after God the Lord...:

O Virgin and all-glorious Mother of God,* we sing to you a hymn of praise,* for Hades has been destroyed by your Son’s cross;* Death has been put to death,* and we, the dead, have been made to rise and become worthy of life.* We have obtained paradise, the original bliss.* Therefore, we glorify you by giving thanks to Christ our God,* for He alone is almighty and all-merciful.

Wednesday and Friday at the end of Matins:

O Mother of God, we glorify you and cry out:* Rejoice, O branch who budded forth God without human seed!* He has crushed Death on the cross.

Friday Vespers and Saturday Matins after God the Lord...:

O Mother of God, the mysteries..., (see above).

Saturday at the end of Matins:

O holy Mother, Mother of the inexpressible Light... (see above).

Tone Three

Resurrectional – Saturday Vespers and Sunday Matins, and for a Polyeleos:

We sing your praises, O Virgin,* for; as the Mother of God, you always intercede for the salvation of the human race.* It is from you that our God and your Son took flesh.* Then, by suffering the passion on the cross,* and out of love for mankind, He delivered us from corruption.

Sunday Vespers and Monday Matins after God the Lord...:

Gabriel was rapt in amazement as he beheld your virginity* and the splendor of your purity, O Mother of God,* and he cried out to You:* By what name shall I call you? I am bewildered, I am lost.* I shall greet you as I was commanded to do:* Rejoice, O Woman Full of Grace!

Monday at the end of Matins:

Indeed, everyone runs to the place of salvation.* O Mother of God, what other shelter can protect our souls as well as you?

Monday Vespers and Tuesday Matins after God the Lord...:

O Mother of God, powerful protectress of the world,* you are our shelter and you are our strength.* Through your intercession, protect your servants against all dangers,* for you are the only blessed One!

Tuesday at the end of Matins:

Indeed, everyone runs... (see above).

Tuesday and Thursday Vespers; Wednesday and Friday Matins after God the Lord...:

O Mother of God, we have obtained your Son’s cross as a staff of power* with which to strike down the boastings of the enemy.* Therefore, we exalt you without ceasing.

Wednesday and Friday at the end of Matins:

O Mother of God, powerful protectress... (see above).

Wednesday Vespers and Thursday Matins after God the Lord:

The prophets foretold, the apostles taught, the martyrs professed,* and we have believed that you are, indeed, the Mother of God.* Therefore, we exalt your inexpressible birthgiving.

Thursday at the end of Matins:

Indeed, everyone runs... (see above).

Friday Vespers and Saturday Matins after God the Lord...:

We sing your praises... (see above).

Saturday at the end of Matins:

The prophets foretold... (see above).

Tone Four

Resurrectional – Saturday Vespers and Sunday Matins, and for a Polyeleos:

O Mother of God, the mystery hidden from all eternity* and unknown even to the angels,* was revealed through you to those on earth:* God took on our human nature* and united it to His divine nature in a perfect but unconfused union.* Then, He willingly accepted the cross for our sake* and thereby raised again the first created,* and saved our souls from death.

Sunday Vespers and Monday Matins after God the Lord...:

To the One who was reared in the Temple,* close to the Holy of Holies,* and who was full of faith, wisdom, and perfect virginity,* Gabriel, the captain and leader,* offered esteem and greetings from heaven when he said:* Rejoice, O blessed One! Rejoice, O glorified One!* The Lord is with you.

Monday at the end of Matins:

Since you are placed above all creatures, O Mother of God,* we cannot sing a hymn of praise that is worthy of you.* Therefore, we ask you as a gracious favor:* Have mercy on us!

Monday Vespers and Tuesday Matins after God the Lord...:

Let us poor sinners hasten with fervor to the Mother of God;* let us humbly kneel before her and say from the depth of our hearts:* O Lady, be kind to us and help us.* Hasten, for we are perishing under a multitude of sins.* Do not turn away your servants empty-handed,* for you are their only hope and protection.

Tuesday at the end of Matins:

We exalt you, O Mother of God, crying out:* You are the bush in which Moses saw the fire of the Godhead in the form of flames,* and it was not consumed.

Tuesday and Thursday Vespers; Wednesday and Friday Matins after God the Lord...:

O Virgin, all immaculate Mother of Christ God,* a sword pierced your all-holy soul* when you saw your Son and God willingly crucified.* Therefore, O blessed One,* never cease to pray to Him* that He may grant us forgiveness of our sins.

Wednesday and Friday at the end of Matins:

We exalt you, O Mother of God, crying out:* You are the mountain out of whom, in a wondrous way,* a stone was hewn that crushed the gates of Hades.

Wednesday Vespers and Thursday Matins after God the Lord:

O Virgin Mother of God,* we acknowledge that the Word of the Father, Christ our God,* was incarnate of you, the only pure one and the only blessed one.* Therefore, we sing to you a hymn of praise and we exalt you.

Thursday at the end of Matins:

Since you are placed above all creatures... (see above).

Friday Vespers and Saturday Matins after God the Lord...:

O Mother of God, the mystery... (see above).

Saturday at the end of Matins:

We exalt you, O Mother of God... (see above).

Tone Five

Resurrectional – Saturday Vespers, Sunday Matins, and for a Polyeleos:

Rejoice, Virgin, impassable gateway of the Lord!* Rejoice, protective wall of those who take refuge in you!* Rejoice, peaceful haven untroubled by storms!* Rejoice, O Virgin who has not known wedlock* and yet has given birth in the flesh to your Creator and God!* O Mother of God, never cease to intercede* for those who praise and worship your Son.

Sunday Vespers and Monday Matins after God the Lord...:

With the angels of heaven and with those upon earth,* O Mother of God, we cry out to you exultingly:* Rejoice, O gate that is wider than heaven!* Rejoice, only salvation of the human race!* Rejoice, O honorable One, full of grace,* who gave birth to the incarnate God!

Monday at the end of Matins

O Christ God, You revealed yourself to the world through a Virgin,* and through her made us to be children of light,* have mercy upon all of us.

Monday Vespers and Tuesday Matins after God the Lord...:

O Mother of God, show forth to your servants* your ready protection, your help, and your mercy;* in your purity, calm the storms of my vain thoughts* and lift up my poor soul;* for I know, O Virgin, that you can do whatever you will.

Tuesday at the end of Matins:

All-holy Mother of God, rampart of Christians,* be the constant salvation of your people,* for they earnestly cry out to you:* Strike down disgraceful thoughts and defeat false reasonings,* for we sing to you: Rejoice, O Virgin forever!

Tuesday and Thursday Vespers; Wednesday and Friday Matins after God the Lord...:

Since you are all-pleasing to God,* may the error of idol worship be destroyed,* and may the power of demons be trodden underfoot* by the might of your Son’s cross.* Therefore, we the faithful always sing to you in humility;* we bless you and we exalt you, professing that you are truly the Mother of God.

Wednesday and Friday at the end of Matins:

O Christ God... (see above).

Wednesday Vespers and Thursday Matins after God the Lord...:

The Virgin’s wondrous mystery was the salvation revealed to the world,* for You, O Lord, were born of her without human seed,* and You appeared in the flesh without corruption, O Joy of us all!* O Lord, glory to You!

Thursday at the end of Matins:

All-holy Mother of God... (see above).

Friday Vespers and Saturday Matins after God the Lord...:

Rejoice, O Virgin, Impassable gateway... (see above).

Saturday at the end of Matins:

O Christ God... (see above).

Tone Six

Resurrectional – Saturday Vespers, Sunday Matins, and for a Polyeleos:

Christ, Who called you Mother and the Blessed One,* accepted the passion of His own free will.* In His desire to find the fallen Adam,* He caused light to shine from His cross in order to search him out.* Then He said to the angels:* Rejoice with Me, for I have found the lost piece of silver.* Glory to You, O God,* who accomplished all things in such wisdom!

Sunday Vespers and Monday Matins after God the Lord...:

The announcement of Gabriel to the Virgin* was the beginning of salvation,* for she heard the salutation and did not flee from the greeting,* nor did she doubt as did Sarah in the tent.* She proclaimed instead: Behold the handmaid of the Lord.* Be it done to me according to your word.

Monday at the end of Matins:

O Mother of God, by accepting the word of the archangel,* you became a cherubic throne,* and received in your arms* the hope of our souls.

Monday Vespers and Tuesday Matins after God the Lord...:

O virgin Mother of God, good hope of the world,* we seek no other help than your power.* Have compassion for your people who have no other protector* and intercede with the merciful God* that He may save our souls from all dangers,* for you are the only blessed one.

Tuesday at the end of Matins:

O most pure Virgin Theotokos,* no one fleeing to you departs in shame,* but pleads for peace and receives your gifts through supplication.

Tuesday and Thursday Vespers; Wednesday and Friday Matins after God the Lord...:

O virgin Mother of God,* pray to your Son, Christ our God, Who delivered the world from error when He accepted willingly to be crucified, that He may have mercy on our souls.

Wednesday and Friday at the end of Matins:

You bore God the Son and the Word of God;* He was born of the Father without a mother before all ages,* and was incarnate in time of your pure blood without a man.* Intercede with Him that He may grant us* the forgiveness of our sins before the end.

Wednesday Vespers and Thursday Matins after God the Lord:

Holy and pure Lady, virgin Mother of our God,* who gave birth to the Creator of all in a manner beyond description;* together with the apostles pray without ceasing to his goodness,* that He may save us from our passions* and grant us remission of our sins.

Thursday at the end of Matins:

You deserved great spiritual favors, O pure virgin Mother of God,* for you gave birth out of your flesh* to one of the persons of the Trinity, Christ the Life-giver,* for the salvation of our souls.

Friday Vespers and Saturday Matins after God the Lord...:

Christ, Who called you Mother... (see above).

Saturday at the end of Matins:

You bore God the Son... (see above).

Tone Seven

Resurrectional – Saturday Vespers, Sunday Matins, and for a Polyeleos:

O Mother of God, we sing your praises,* for you were a Virgin before conceiving* and remained a virgin after giving birth.* And since you have given birth to our Salvation, Jesus Christ,* you have become a treasury of assistance* for all who seek resurrection and eternal life.* Therefore, lift us up from the depth of our transgressions,* for we place our hope in you:

Sunday Vespers and Monday Matins after God the Lord...:

O Mother of God, most pure Virgin,* with the heavenly Powers pray to your Son,* that, before our death, He grant forgiveness of sins* to us, who faithfully extol you.

Monday at the end of Matins:

O blessed Mother of God,* you appeared higher than the powers of heaven,* for you became a divine temple* by giving birth to Christ, the Savior of our souls.

Monday Vespers and Tuesday Matins after God the Lord...:

O Mother of God,* we honor you,* for you were seen to be higher than the angels* when you carried God in your womb.

Tuesday at the end of Matins:

Through the prayers of the Mother of God,* establish peace in our lives,* for we cry out to You:* O merciful Lord, glory to You!

Tuesday and Thursday Vespers; Wednesday and Friday Matins after God the Lord...:

O virgin Mother of God,* unceasingly intercede with Christ our God* who was crucified for us and destroyed the power of Death,* that He may save our souls.

Wednesday and Friday at the end of Matins:

O Mother of God,* deliver us from our deep-rooted sins,* for we, the faithful, have no hope but in you* and in God Who was born of you.

Wednesday Vespers and Thursday Matins after God the Lord:...

O pure One, the fruit of your womb* is the perfect fulfillment of the prophets and of the Law.* Therefore, O Mother of God,* we glorify you with thanks and we exalt you with great devotion.

Thursday at the end of Matins:

O bride of God, the fruit of your womb* was seen as the cause of the salvation of all.* Therefore, we the faithful glorify you in our minds and through our words,* and we exalt you, O Mother of God!

Friday Vespers and Saturday Matins after God the Lord...:

O Mother of God, we sing your praises... (see above).

Saturday at the end of Matins:

Rejoice, O woman who contained within your womb* the One Whom the heavens cannot hold!* Rejoice, O Virgin foretold by the prophets, from whom Immanuel arose!* Rejoice, O Mother of Christ our God!

Tone Eight

Resurrectional Saturday Vespers, Sunday Matins, and for a Polyeleos:

O gracious Lord, for the sake of all You were born of a virgin;* through Your death on the cross You conquered Death;* and through Your resurrection You revealed Yourself as God.* O merciful Lord, do not disdain those whom You have created with Your own hands,* but show forth Your love for us.* Accept the intercession which the Mother who bore You makes in our behalf.* O Savior, save Your despairing people!
