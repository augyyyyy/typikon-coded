# TYPICON: SUNDAY, APRIL 19th, 2026.
## SUNDAY OF THE MYRRH-BEARING WOMEN - TONE II
Ven. John of the Ancient Cave.  
Sunday service combined with the triodion, and that of st. ven. john of the ancient cave..  
**Vestment colour:** Bright [blue for the forefeast or gold].  
## GREAT VESPERS
**At O Lord, I have cried…** we sing 7 resurrectional stichera from the Octoechos, 3 Saint from the Menaion; Glory... Glory; Both now... Dogmatic Theotokion in the tone of the week.  
**If Litiya is performed:** The sticheron of the temple.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 1 Aposticha Resurrection in Tone 2 from the Octoechos; Glory, both now... Aposticha Theotokion in Tone 2.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:**
We sing We sing the Sunday (resurrectional) troparion in the tone of the week; Glory... Troparion; Both now... Theotokion Dismissal in Tone 2.  
## MATINS
After each cathisma we sing the sessional hymns from the Octoechos.  
We sing the 17th Kathisma (Psalm 118).  
After, the Evlogitaria: the Hypakoë (Tone II), Hymns of Ascents, Prokeimenon, Let everything that has breath: in the tone of the week.  
**Matins Gospel II**: Mk. 16: 1-8.  
**At the Canon (abbreviated for parish use):**  
  At each ode, the irmos of the resurrection from the Octoechos.  
  1 troparion of the resurrection.  ℟. Glory to Your holy resurrection, O Lord!  
  1 troparion of the Saint.  ℟. Holy Ven. John of the Ancient Cave., pray to God for us!  
  Glory... both now... Theotokion.  
  Catabasia: Katavasia Pascha (Tone 1).  
Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Resurrection - 4 (including the irmos), Cross Res - 3, Theotokos - 3, Menaion - 4.  Catabasia Tone I "The Resurrection Day . . . ".  
After Ode III: Hypakoe in Tone II; Glory... both now... Theotokion.  
After Ode VI: Resurrection Kontakion and Ikos in Tone II.  
After Ode VIII: we sing the Magnificat.  
Holy is the Lord... Tone II.  
**Exapostilarion** - Eothinon 2; Eothinon 2 Theotokion.  
**At the Praises:** We sing 44 stichera: 44 from the Octoechos; Glory... Praises Glory Gospel 2; Both now... Praises Both Now in Tone 2.  
After the Dismissal of Matins: Glory... both now... Gospel Sticheron II.  
## HOURS
At all the hours: Resurrectional troparion, Glory... troparion of John of the Ancient Cave.
Kontakia: at the First and Sixth – Resurrection; at the Third and Ninth – feast.
