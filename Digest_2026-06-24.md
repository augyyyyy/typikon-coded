# TYPICON: WEDNESDAY, JUNE 24th, 2026.

NATIVITY OF ST. JOHN THE BAPTIST - TONE III.  
Nativity of St. John the Baptist  
Wednesday service combined with that of Nativity of St. John the Baptist.  
Vestment colour: Bright (Green).  

## SMALL VESPERS

*At Lord, I Call…* we sing 4 Stichera of the Saint from the Menaion; Glory, Both now: Theotokion.  
Prokeimenon: Psalm Lord Is King 92  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 4 Aposticha Saint from the Menaion; Glory... Saint; Both now: Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing the Troparion of Nativity John Baptist; Glory, Both now: Theotokion.  

## GREAT VESPERS

Vestments (priest): epitrachelion, phelonion. The phelonion is blessed and kissed before the Entrance.  
Fasting Rule: Fish and wine permitted for the feast.  
Censing: Priest censes entire church during Psalm 103.  
Censing: Deacon performs Great censing of the entire church during 'Lord, I Call'.  
*At Lord, I Call…* we sing 8 Stichera of the Saint from the Menaion; Glory... Doxastikon of Nativity John Baptist; Both now: Dogmatic Theotokion in the tone of the week.  
Censing: Small censing at the entrance.  
The Royal Doors are opened.  
Prokeimenon: The righteous man shall rejoice in the Lord, and shall hope in Him. (Tone VII).  
We sing the Litiya stichera: 5 stichera of the Saint from the Menaion; Glory... Doxastikon of Litiya; Both now: Theotokion of Litiya.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing the Aposticha; Glory, Both now: Aposticha Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing the Troparion of Nativity John Baptist; Glory, Both now: Theotokion.  
At the Blessing of Loaves (Artoklasia):  
- Rubric: Priest blesses five loaves, wheat, wine, and oil.  
- Troparia to sing: Rejoice O Virgin (theotokos) x3.  
- Priest action: Stand before the table with loaves. Read the Artoklasia prayer. Sign the loaves crosswise with one loaf.  
- Deacon action: Cense the loaves during the prayer.  
- Choir action: Sing troparia as prescribed (see troparia array).  

## FESTAL MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing: Troparion of Nativity of St. John the Baptist, twice; Glory, Both now. Theotokion, once.  

We read Kathismata 8, 9 and 10.  

After the Kathismata (8, 9, and 10): We sing the Sessional Hymns of the Saint.  
We sing the Polyeleos.  
Anabathmoi (Hymns of Ascents): Anabathmoi Tone Antiphon 4:1.  
Gradual (Anabathmoi): Antiphon Tone 1:4.  
Prokeimenon: Say among the nations: The Lord is King! (Tone III).  
And that we may be accounted worthy of hearing the holy Gospel, let us pray to the Lord God. Wisdom, stand upright! Let us hear the holy Gospel. **Matins Gospel:** Gospel of the Feast (None): Luke 1:24-25, 57-68. Response: Glory to Thee, O Lord, glory to Thee.  
At the Canon: Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Menaion - 12 (including the heirmos).  

**Katavasia:** "Heirmos of the last canon" (Tone IV).  
At Ode 8 (Glory): "Let us bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, the Lord."  
At Ode IX, we sing the Magnification ('My soul magnifies the Lord...') and the refrains ('More honorable than the Cherubim...').  
It is truly meet (Axion Estin).  
Exapostilarion: Exapostilarion of Nativity John Baptist.  
Doxology: Great Doxology (sung).  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing: Troparion of Nativity John Baptist in Tone I; Both now: Theotokion.  

## HOURS

**At the Hours:** At all the hours: Troparion of Nativity of St. John the Baptist.  
Kontakia: at all the hours – Kontakion of **Nativity of St. John the Baptist.**.

Psalms of Typica; Beatitudes on 4: Menaion - 4.  
**Troparia and Kontakia:**  
Troparion of the Cross.  
Troparion of Nativity of St. John the Baptist.  
Glory... Kontakion of Nativity of St. John the Baptist.  
Both now: Kontakion of the Cross.  
**Prokeimenon:** *of Nativity of St. John the Baptist*  
**Epistle:** *Romans 13:11-14:4*

**Post-Communion Hymn:** "We have seen the true light, we have received the heavenly Spirit..."
