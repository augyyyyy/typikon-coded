TYPICON: WEDNESDAY, JUNE 10TH, 2026. HIEROMARTYR TIMOTHY - TONE I.  
Wednesday service combined with that of Hieromartyr Timothy.  
Vestment colour: Bright (Red).  

## DAILY VESPERS

At Vespers, Kathisma 18 is read.  
*At O Lord, I have cried…* we sing 3 Stichera from the Octoechos, and 3 Stichera of the Saint from the Menaion; Glory... Doxastikon of the Saint; Both now... Theotokion from the Horologion or Octoechos.  
Prokeimenon: of the day, Tone VII.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 daily Aposticha from the Octoechos; Glory, both now... Aposticha Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** Troparion of Hieromartyr Timothy; Glory, both now... Dismissal Theotokion in Tone I.  

## DAILY MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing the Troparion of the Cross, twice; Glory, both now... Dismissal Theotokion in Tone I.  

We read Kathismata 8 and 9 and 10 (Wednesday Matins).  
After the Kathismata (8 & 9 & 10): We sing the Sessional Hymns from the Octoechos.  

At the Canon: Order of the Canon: First Canon of the Octoechos with the Heirmos on 6; second Canon of the Octoechos on 4; Canon of the Saint on 4. Katavasia: Heirmos of the last canon (of the Saint) after Odes 3, 6, 8, and 9.  

**After Ode III:** Sessional hymns; Glory... both now... Theotokion.  

**After Ode VI:** Kontakion and Ikos.  

**After Ode VIII:** We sing the Magnificat.  

Note: At the 9th Ode, the priest censes as at Great Matins.  

**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 daily Aposticha from the Octoechos; Glory, both now... Aposticha Theotokion.  

**At the Dismissal Troparia:** Troparion of Hieromartyr Timothy; Glory, both now... Dismissal Theotokion for Wednesday in Tone I.  

## HOURS
Troparia: First Hour – Troparion of the Cross; Third Hour – Troparion of Hieromartyr Timothy; Sixth Hour – Troparion of the Temple; Ninth Hour – Troparion of Hieromartyr Timothy. Kontakia: First Hour – Kontakion of the Cross; Third Hour – Kontakion of Hieromartyr Timothy; Sixth Hour – Kontakion of the Temple; Ninth Hour – Kontakion of Hieromartyr Timothy.  

## DIVINE LITURGY
Psalms of Typica; Beatitudes.  
**Troparia and Kontakia:**  
Troparion of the Cross.  
Troparion of Hieromartyr Timothy.  
Glory... Kontakion of Hieromartyr Timothy.  
Both now... Kontakion of the Cross.  
**Prokeimenon:** of the day, Tone VII.

**Alleluia:** Tone VII, with verses of the day.
