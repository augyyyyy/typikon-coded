Wednesday service.

Glory... Doxastikon of the Saint; Both now: Theotokion of the Feast.

## SMALL COMPLINE

Kontakia: Kontakion of the Saint; Glory... Both now: Kontakion of the Feast (Eucharist).  

## MIDNIGHT OFFICE

After the 1st Trisagion — Troparion of the Feast (Eucharist). After the 2nd Trisagion — Kontakion of the Feast. We do not say the prayer “Remember”. Instead: “Lord, have mercy” (12) and the dismissal.  

## FESTAL MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing: Troparion of the Saint, twice; Glory, Both now. Theotokion, once.  

We read Kathismata 8, 9 and 10.  

After the Kathismata (8, 9, and 10): We sing the Sessional Hymns of the Venerable Father.  
**Katavasia:** "Christ is born" (Tone I).  
At Ode IX, we sing the Magnification ('My soul magnifies the Lord...') and the refrains ('More honorable than the Cherubim...').  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 daily Aposticha from the Octoechos; Glory, Both now: Aposticha Theotokion.  
Dismissal Stavrotheotokion (Tone 4, Wednesday): Dismissal Theotokion for Wednesday in Tone 4.  

## HOURS

**At the Hours:** At all the hours: Troparion of the feast, Glory... Troparion of the Saint.  
Kontakia: at the First and Sixth Hours – Kontakion of the (Ven. Melania). Apodosis of the Nativity of Christ; at the Third and Ninth Hours – Kontakion of the Saint.
