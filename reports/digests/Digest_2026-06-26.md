# TYPICON: FRIDAY, JUNE 26th, 2026.

VEN. DAVID OF THESSALONICA - TONE III.  
St. Ven. David of Thessalonica  
Friday service combined with that of St. Ven. David of Thessalonica.  
Vestment colour: Bright (Green).  

## DAILY VESPERS

At Vespers, Kathisma 18 is read.  
Glory... Doxastikon of the Saint; Both now: Theotokion from the Horologion or Octoechos.  
Prokeimenon: Tone VII (Friday of the Octoechos): "O God, Thou art my strength; Haste Thee to help me"  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 daily Aposticha from the Octoechos; Glory, Both now: Aposticha Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** Troparion of St. Ven. David of Thessalonica; Glory, Both now: Dismissal Theotokion in Tone III.  

## SMALL COMPLINE

Canon: Departed from the Octoechos.  
Troparia: Troparion of the Cross (first place, Wednesday/Friday), *"O God of our fathers..."* and the following three troparia.  

## MIDNIGHT OFFICE (DAILY)

Midnight Troparia: Troparion of the weekday, Troparion of the Saint (if any), daily Theotokion.  
Prayer of St. Mardarius.  

## DAILY MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing the Troparion of St. Ven. David of Thessalonica, twice; Glory, Both now: Dismissal Theotokion in the tone of the Saint's troparion and of the day of the week.  

We read Kathismata 13 and 14.  

After the Kathismata (13 & 14): We sing the Sessional Hymns from the Octoechos.  
At the Canon: Order of the Canon: First Canon of the Octoechos with the Heirmos on 6; second Canon of the Octoechos on 4; Canon of the Saint on 4. Katavasia: Heirmos of the last canon (of the Saint) after Odes 3, 6, 8, and 9.  

Note: At the 9th Ode, the priest censes as at Great Matins.  
At Ode IX, we sing the Magnification ('My soul magnifies the Lord...') and the refrains ('More honorable than the Cherubim...').  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 daily Aposticha from the Octoechos; Glory, Both now: Aposticha Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** Troparion of St. Ven. David of Thessalonica; Glory, Both now: Dismissal Theotokion for Friday in Tone I.  

## HOURS

Troparia: First Hour – Troparion of the Cross; Third Hour – Troparion of St. Ven. David of Thessalonica; Sixth Hour – Troparion of the Temple; Ninth Hour – Troparion of St. Ven. David of Thessalonica. Kontakia: First Hour – Kontakion of the Cross; Third Hour – Kontakion of St. Ven. David of Thessalonica; Sixth Hour – Kontakion of the Temple; Ninth Hour – Kontakion of St. Ven. David of Thessalonica.  

## DIVINE LITURGY OF ST. JOHN CHRYSOSTOM

Psalms of Typica; Beatitudes on 6: 3 from the Octoechos, 3 from Ode III of the Saint.  
**Troparia and Kontakia:**  
Troparion of the Cross.  
Troparion of St. Ven. David of Thessalonica.  
Glory... Kontakion of St. Ven. David of Thessalonica.  
Both now: Kontakion of the Cross.  
**Prokeimenon:** Tone VII: "The righteous shall rejoice in the Lord"  
**Epistle:** of the day (Romans 15:17-29)  
**Alleluia:** Tone VII, with verses of the day.  
**Gospel:** of the day (Matthew 12:46-13:9)  
**Communion Hymn:** of the day: "O Lord, save Thy people."  
**Post-Communion Hymn:** "We have seen the true light, we have received the heavenly Spirit..."  
Dismissal: May Christ our true God...
