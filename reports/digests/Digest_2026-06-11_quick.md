TYPICON: THURSDAY, JUNE 11TH, 2026. APODOSIS OF THE EUCHARIST; APOSTLES BARTHOLOMEW AND BARNABAS - TONE I.  
Thursday service combined with that of the Triodion, and that of Apostles Bartholomew and Barnabas.  
Vestment colour: Bright (White).  

## GREAT VESPERS

*At O Lord, I have cried…* we sing 6 Feast Stichera from the Triodion, and 4 Stichera of the Saint from the Menaion; Glory... Doxastikon of Apostles Bartholomew and Barnabas; Both now: Sticheron of the Feast (Eucharist).  
Prokeimenon: of the day, Tone VII.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 Aposticha Feast from the Triodion; Glory... Doxastikon of the Saint; Both now: Theotokion of the Feast.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing the Troparion of Apostles Bartholomew and Barnabas; Glory, Both now: Troparion of the Feast.  

## FESTAL MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing: Troparion of the feast, twice; Glory. Troparion of Apostles Bartholomew and Barnabas, once; Both now. Troparion of the feast, once.  

After each kathisma: Small Litany, then we sing the Sessional Hymns from the Triodion.  

At the Canon: We sing the canon of the Triodion on 10, then the Saint on 4. At each ode: Heirmos from the Feast; troparia with refrains; Glory... Both now.  

**After Ode III:** Sessional hymns; Glory... Both now: Theotokion.  

**After Ode VI:** Kontakion and Ikos.  

**At Ode IX:** We sing the Magnification.  

**Katavasia:** "The bread of heaven He gave them" (Tone V).  

**Exaposteilarion** - Saint; Theotokion.  

**At the Praises:** We sing 6 Stichera: 3 from the Triodion, and 3 from the Menaion; Glory... Doxastikon of the Saint; Both now: Theotokion of the Feast.  

**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing: Troparion of Bartholomew Barnabas in Tone I; Glory, Both now: Troparion of the Feast in Tone VIII.  

## HOURS
**At the Hours:** At the First and Sixth Hours: Troparion of the feast; At the Third and Ninth Hours: Troparion of Apostles Bartholomew and Barnabas.  
Kontakia: at the First and Sixth Hours – Kontakion of the Eucharist; at the Third and Ninth Hours – Kontakion of Apostles Bartholomew and Barnabas.  

## DIVINE LITURGY
Psalms of Typica; Beatitudes on 8: Triodion - 4, Menaion - 4.  
**Troparia and Kontakia:**  
Troparion of the Feast.  
Troparion of Apostles Bartholomew and Barnabas.  
Glory... Kontakion of Apostles Bartholomew and Barnabas.  
Both now: Kontakion of the Feast.  
**Prokeimenon (Feast - sung twice):** Tone VI: "O Lord, save Your people and bless Your inheritance"  
**Prokeimenon (Saint - sung once, without verse):** Tone VIII: "Their proclamation has gone out into all the earth, and their words to the ends of the world"  
**Epistle:** Romans 8:22-27  
**Epistle:** Acts 11:19-26, 29-30  
**Alleluia:** Tone VI, with verses: "He who eats My flesh and drinks My blood abides in Me, and I in him; The bread that I will give is My flesh for the life of the world".  
**Alleluia:** Tone I, with verses: "The heavens shall confess Your wonders, O Lord, and Your truth in the congregation of the Saints".  
**Gospel:** Matthew 10:23-31  
**Gospel:** Luke 10:16-21  
**Communion Hymn:** "Receive the Body of Christ; taste the fountain of immortality. And of the Saint: Their sound hath gone forth into all the earth, and their words unto the ends of the world."
