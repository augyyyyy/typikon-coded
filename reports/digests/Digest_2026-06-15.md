# TYPICON: MONDAY, JUNE 15th, 2026.

PROP. AMOS - TONE II.  
St. Prop. Amos  
Monday service combined with that of St. Prop. Amos.  
Vestment colour: Bright (Green).  

## DAILY VESPERS

No kathisma is appointed.  
Glory... Doxastikon of the Saint; Both now: Theotokion from the Horologion or Octoechos.  
Prokeimenon: Tone I (Monday of the Octoechos): "Thy mercy, O Lord, shall follow me all the days of my life"  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 daily Aposticha from the Octoechos; Glory, Both now: Aposticha Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** Troparion of St. Prop. Amos; Glory, Both now: Dismissal Theotokion in Tone II.  

## SMALL COMPLINE

Canon: Theotokos from the Octoechos.  
Troparia: Monday Troparion from the Octoechos, Troparion of the Temple, *"O God of our fathers..."* and the following three troparia.  

## MIDNIGHT OFFICE (DAILY)

Midnight Troparia: Troparion of the weekday, Troparion of the Temple, Troparion of the Saint (if any), daily Theotokion.  
Prayer of St. Mardarius.  

## DAILY MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing the Troparion of St. Prop. Amos, twice; Glory, Both now: Dismissal Theotokion in the tone of the Saint's troparion and of the day of the week.  

We read Kathismata 4 and 5.  

After the Kathismata (4 & 5): We sing the Sessional Hymns from the Octoechos.  
At the Canon: Order of the Canon: First Canon of the Octoechos with the Heirmos on 6; second Canon of the Octoechos on 4; Canon of the Saint on 4. Katavasia: Heirmos of the last canon (of the Saint) after Odes 3, 6, 8, and 9.  

Note: At the 9th Ode, the priest censes as at Great Matins.  
At Ode IX, we sing the Magnification ('My soul magnifies the Lord...') and the refrains ('More honorable than the Cherubim...').  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 daily Aposticha from the Octoechos; Glory, Both now: Aposticha Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** Troparion of St. Prop. Amos; Glory, Both now: Dismissal Theotokion for Monday in Tone I.  

## HOURS

Troparia: First Hour – Day; Third & Ninth Hours – St. Prop. Amos; Sixth Hour – Temple. Kontakia: First Hour – Day; Third & Ninth Hours – St. Prop. Amos; Sixth Hour – Temple.
