# TYPICON: SUNDAY, JUNE 28th, 2026.

TRANSLATION OF THE RELICS OF SAINTS UNMERCENARIES CYRUS AND JOHN - TONE IV.  
Translation of the relics of Saints Unmercenaries Cyrus and John  
Sunday service combined with that of Translation of the Relics of Saints Unmercenaries Cyrus and John.  
Vestment colour: Bright (Gold).  

## GREAT VESPERS

Vestments (priest): epitrachelion, phelonion. The phelonion is blessed and kissed before the Entrance.  
Fasting Rule: No fasting restrictions.  
Censing: Priest censes entire church during Psalm 103.  
Censing: Deacon performs Great censing of the entire church during 'Lord, I Call'.  
Glory... Doxastikon of Translation Relics Unmercenaries Cyrus John; Both now: Dogmatic Theotokion in the tone of the week.  
Censing: Small censing at the entrance.  
The Royal Doors are opened.  
Prokeimenon: The Lord is King (Tone VI).  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing the resurrectional aposticha in the tone of the week, from the Octoechos; Glory, Both now: Aposticha Theotokion Tone 4.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing the Sunday (resurrectional) troparion in the tone of the week; Glory... Troparion of Translation Relics Unmercenaries Cyrus John; Both now: Theotokion.  

## SMALL COMPLINE

Canon: Theotokos from the Octoechos.  
Troparia: Sunday Troparion from the Octoechos, Troparion of the Temple, *"O God of our fathers..."* and the following three troparia.  

## MIDNIGHT OFFICE (SUNDAY)

Triadic Canon: Canon Trinity in Tone 4.  
Midnight Troparia: Sunday Hypakoe in Tone 4.  
Prayer: Prayer Holy Trinity All Creating.  

## SUNDAY MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing: the Sunday (resurrectional) troparion, twice; Glory. Troparion of Translation of the relics of Saints Unmercenaries Cyrus and John, once; Both now. Theotokion, once.  

After the Kathismata (2 & 3): We sing: after the 1st (2): Small Litany, then the Sessional Resurrection Tone Set 4:1; after the 2nd (3): Small Litany, then the Sessional Resurrection Tone Set 4:2.  
We sing the Polyeleos.  
Hypakoe: Hypakoe Tone 4.  
Anabathmoi (Hymns of Ascents): Anabathmoi Tone 4.  
Gradual (Anabathmoi): Anabathmoi Tone 4.  
Prokeimenon: Arise, O Lord, help us, and deliver us for Thy name's sake (Tone IV).  
Matins Gospel: Matins Gospel 1 (Eothinon).  
Psalm 50 and Post-Gospel Stichera: Glory Apostles, Both Now Theotokos, Have Mercy, Jesus Having Risen.  
**Katavasia:** "Heirmos of the last canon" (Tone IV).  
At Ode 8 (Glory): "Let us bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, the Lord."  
At Ode IX, we sing the Magnification ('My soul magnifies the Lord...') and the refrains ('More honorable than the Cherubim...').  
Holy is the Lord our God (3x).  
Exapostilarion: Holy Is the Lord in Tone 4; Gospel Exapostilarion I; Theotokion of Gospel Exapostilarion I.  
At the Praises, we sing 8 Stichera: 4 from the Octoechos, and 4 from the Menaion; Glory... ; Other: Now and ever....  
Doxology: Sunday Resurrection (Sung).  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing: Sunday Dismissal Troparion 'Today salvation has come to the world' in Tone I; Glory, Both now: Today Salvation.  
We sing: Glory... Gospel Sticheron I in Tone 5; Both now: Most Blessed Art Thou.  

## HOURS

**At the Hours:** At the First Hour: Resurrectional troparion; At the Third and Ninth Hours: Resurrectional troparion, Glory... Troparion of Translation of the relics of Saints Unmercenaries Cyrus and John; At the Sixth Hour: Resurrectional troparion, Glory... Troparion of the Temple.  
Kontakia: at the First and Ninth Hours – Resurrection; at the Third Hour – Kontakion of Translation of the relics of Saints Unmercenaries Cyrus and John; at the Sixth Hour – Temple.

Psalms of Typica; Beatitudes on 4: Octoechos - 4.
