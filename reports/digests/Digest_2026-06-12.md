# TYPICON: FRIDAY, JUNE 12th, 2026.

FRIDAY OF THE CO-SUFFERING OF THE MOST HOLY THEOTOKOS - TONE I.  
Friday service combined with that of the Triodion.  
Vestment colour: Bright (Blue or light blue).  

## GREAT VESPERS

At Vespers, Kathisma 18 is read.  
*At Lord, I Call…* we sing 8 Feast Stichera from the Menaion; Glory... Feast Doxastikon; Both now: Theotokion of the Feast.  
Prokeimenon: Tone VII (Friday of the Octoechos): "O God, Thou art my strength; Haste Thee to help me"  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 Aposticha Feast from the Menaion; Glory... Feast Doxastikon; Both now: Theotokion of the Feast.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing the Troparion of the Feast; Glory, Both now: Theotokion.  

## SMALL COMPLINE

Canon: Feast from the Triodion.  
Troparia: Troparion of the Cross (first place, Wednesday/Friday), *"O God of our fathers..."* and the following three troparia.  

## MIDNIGHT OFFICE (DAILY)

Midnight Troparia: Troparion of the weekday, Troparion of the Saint (if any), daily Theotokion.  
Prayer of St. Mardarius.  

## FESTAL MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing: Troparion of the feast, twice; Glory, Both now. Troparion of the feast, once.  

We read Kathismata 13 and 14.  

After the Kathismata (13 & 14): We sing the Sessional Hymns from the Triodion.  
At the Canon: Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Feast - 12 (including the heirmos).  

**Katavasia:** "I will open" (Tone IV).  
At Ode IX, we sing the Magnification ('My soul magnifies the Lord...') and the refrains ('More honorable than the Cherubim...').  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 Aposticha Feast from the Menaion; Glory... Feast Doxastikon; Both now: Theotokion of the Feast.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing: Troparion of the Feast in Tone I; Glory, Both now: Troparion of the Feast.  
Resurrectional Theotokion in Tone I.  

## HOURS

**At the Hours:** At all the hours: Troparion of the feast.  
Kontakia: at all the hours – Kontakion of the Co-Suffering of the Most Holy Theotokos.  

## DIVINE LITURGY OF ST. JOHN CHRYSOSTOM

Psalms of Typica; Beatitudes on 4: Menaion - 4.  
**Troparia and Kontakia:**  
Troparion of the Feast.  
Kontakion of the Feast.  
**Prokeimenon:** *of the day* Tone VII  
**Epistle:** Romans 9:6-19  
**Alleluia:** sung in Tone VII.  
**Gospel:** Matthew 10:32-40; 11:1  
**Communion Hymn:** "O Lord, save Thy people."  
**Post-Communion Hymn:** "We have seen the true light, we have received the heavenly Spirit..."  
Dismissal: May Christ our true God...
