# TYPICON: TUESDAY, FEBRUARY 24th, 2026.

FIRST AND SECOND FINDING OF THE PRECIOUS HEAD OF THE FORERUNNER - TONE IV.  
First and Second Finding of the Precious Head of the Forerunner  
Tuesday service combined with that of the Triodion, and that of First and Second Finding of the Precious Head of the Forerunner.  
Vestment colour: Dark (Dark_purple or black).  

## GREAT COMPLINE

Canon: Triodion Compline Canon from the Triodion.  
Troparia: Tuesday Troparion from the Octoechos, Troparion of the Temple, *"O God of our fathers..."* and the following three troparia.  

## MIDNIGHT OFFICE (DAILY)

Midnight Troparia: Troparion 'Behold, the Bridegroom', Glory... 'Behold, the Bridegroom' (Glory), Both now: 'Behold, the Bridegroom' (Theotokion).  
Prayer of St. Mardarius.  

## FESTAL MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing: Troparion of First and Second Finding of the Precious Head of the Forerunner, twice; Glory, Both now. Theotokion, once.  
Trinity Hymns (Tone 1):  
- Hymn 1 (Weekday): Trinity Hymn 1 in Tone 1  
- Hymn 2 (All Saints): Trinity Hymn 2 in Tone 1  
- Hymn 3 (Theotokos): Trinity Hymn 3 in Tone 1  
(Trinity Hymns of Tone 1 with tuesday commemorations)  

We read Kathismata 6 and 7.  

After the Kathismata (6 & 7): We sing: after the 1st (6): the Tuesday 1 (Sessional Hymn 1 from Octoechos (Tone 1)); after the 2nd (7): the Tuesday 3 (Sessional Hymn 3 from Triodion (Week 2)).  
After Psalm 50, we sing the Intercession Hymns:  
- Glory: 'Open to me the doors of repentance, O Giver of Life...' (Repentance Doors)  
- Both now: 'Lead me on the paths of salvation, O Theotokos...' (Salvation Paths)  
- Sticheron: 'When I think of the multitude of evil things I have done...' (Multitude Evil)  
Lenten Triodion Canon: Triodion Odes 2, 8, 9.  
Lenten Exapostilarion (Trinity Light Hymn, Tone 1): Exapostilarion Trinity in Tone 1 (Trinity Exapostilarion (Tone 1), sung 3x with commemorations)  
Lenten Aposticha: Tuesday 1; Tuesday 2; Tuesday 3. Glory: Tuesday Glory. Both now: Tuesday Theotokion. (Lenten Aposticha from Triodion (Week 2, tuesday))  
Resurrectional Theotokion in Tone I.  

## HOURS

**At the Hours:** At the First Hour: Lenten troparion (O order my steps); At the Third Hour: Lenten troparion (O Lord, Who at the third hour); At the Sixth Hour: Lenten troparion (O Thou Who on the sixth day); At the Ninth Hour: Lenten troparion (O Thou Who at the ninth hour).  
Kontakia: at all the hours – Kontakion of First and Second Finding of the Precious Head of the Forerunner.  

## LITURGY OF THE PRESANCTIFIED GIFTS

At the Kathisma: All stand in silence. No singing during transfer.; During the Kathisma: Transfer Gifts silently from Prothesis to Holy Table; The deacon: Precede with candle (no censing during transfer); After placement: Cover with Aer after placement.  
*At Lord, I Call…* we sing 3 Stichera from the Triodion, and 3 Stichera from the Menaion; Glory... Doxastikon of First and Second Finding of the Precious Head of the Forerunner; Both now: Dogmatic Theotokion in the tone of the week.
