# TYPICON: TUESDAY, JUNE 30th, 2026.

SYNAXIS OF THE 12 APOSTLES - TONE IV.  
Synaxis of the 12 Apostles  
Tuesday service combined with that of Synaxis of the 12 Apostles.  
Vestment colour: Bright (Green).  

## GREAT VESPERS

Vestments (priest): epitrachelion, phelonion. The phelonion is blessed and kissed before the Entrance.  
Fasting Rule: No fasting restrictions.  
Censing: Priest censes entire church during Psalm 103.  
Censing: Deacon performs Great censing of the entire church during 'Lord, I Call'.  
Glory... Doxastikon of the Saint; Both now: Theotokion from the Horologion or Octoechos.  
Censing: Small censing at the entrance.  
The Royal Doors are opened.  
Prokeimenon: Daily Prokeimenon for Tuesday evening, Tone V.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 daily Aposticha from the Octoechos; Glory, Both now: Aposticha Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing the Troparion of Synaxis 12 Apostles; Glory, Both now: Theotokion.  

## SMALL COMPLINE

Canon: Theotokos from the Octoechos.  
Troparia: Tuesday Troparion from the Octoechos, Troparion of the Temple, *"O God of our fathers..."* and the following three troparia.  

## MIDNIGHT OFFICE (DAILY)

Midnight Troparia: Troparion of the weekday, Troparion of the Temple, Troparion of the Saint (if any), daily Theotokion.  
Prayer of St. Mardarius.  

## FESTAL MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing: Troparion of Synaxis of the 12 Apostles, twice; Glory, Both now. Theotokion, once.  

We read Kathismata 6 and 7.  

After the Kathismata (6 & 7): We sing the Sessional Hymns of the Saint.  
We sing the Polyeleos.  
Anabathmoi (Hymns of Ascents): Anabathmoi Tone Antiphon 4:1.  
Gradual (Anabathmoi): Antiphon Tone 1:4.  
Prokeimenon: Arise, O Lord, help us, and deliver us for Thy name's sake (Tone IV).  
And that we may be accounted worthy of hearing the holy Gospel, let us pray to the Lord God. Wisdom, stand upright! Let us hear the holy Gospel. **Matins Gospel:** Gospel to Synaxis of the 12 Apostles: Luke 10:16-21. Response: Glory to Thee, O Lord, glory to Thee.  
At the Canon: Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Octoechos - 6 (including the heirmos), Octoechos - 4, Menaion - 4.  

**Katavasia:** "Heirmos of the last canon" (Tone IV).  
At Ode 8 (Glory): "Let us bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, the Lord."  
At Ode IX, we sing the Magnification ('My soul magnifies the Lord...') and the refrains ('More honorable than the Cherubim...').  
It is truly meet (Axion Estin).  
Exapostilarion: Exapostilarion of Synaxis 12 Apostles.  
At the Praises, we sing 4 Stichera: 4 from the Menaion; Glory... Doxastikon of the Saint; Other: Now and ever....  
Doxology: Great Doxology (sung).  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing: Troparion of Synaxis 12 Apostles in Tone I; Both now: Theotokion.  

## HOURS

Kontakia: at all the hours – Kontakion of Synaxis of the 12 Apostles.
