TYPICON: FRIDAY, JUNE 12TH, 2026. FRIDAY OF THE CO-SUFFERING OF THE MOST HOLY THEOTOKOS - TONE I.  
Friday service combined with that of the Triodion.  
Vestment colour: Bright (Blue or light blue).  

## GREAT VESPERS

*At O Lord, I have cried…* we sing 8 Feast Stichera from the Menaion; Glory... Feast Doxastikon; Both now: Theotokion of the Feast.  
Prokeimenon: of the day, Tone VII.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 Aposticha Feast from the Menaion; Glory... Feast Doxastikon; Both now: Theotokion of the Feast.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing the Troparion of the Feast; Glory, Both now: Theotokion.  

## FESTAL MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing: Troparion of the feast, twice; Glory, Both now. Troparion of the feast, once.  

After each kathisma: we sing the Sessional Hymns from the Triodion.  

We sing the Polyeleos. Magnification: sung if prescribed.  
At the Canon: We sing the canon of the Feast on 12. At each ode: Heirmos from the Feast; troparia with refrains; Glory... Both now.  

**After Ode III:** Sessional hymns; Glory... Both now: Theotokion.  

**After Ode VI:** Kontakion and Ikos.  

**At Ode IX:** We sing the Magnification.  

**Katavasia:** "I will open" (Tone IV).  

**At the Praises:** We sing 4 Stichera: 4 from the Menaion; Glory... ; Both now: Theotokion of the Feast.  

**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing: Troparion of the Feast in Tone I; Glory, Both now: Troparion of the Feast.  

## HOURS
**At the Hours:** At all the hours: Troparion of the feast.  
Kontakia: at all the hours – Kontakion of the Co-Suffering of the Most Holy Theotokos.
