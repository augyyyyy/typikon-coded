## SUNDAY MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing: the Sunday (resurrectional) troparion, twice; Glory. Troparion of Cyril, once; Both now. Theotokion, once.  

After the Kathismata (2 & 3): We sing: after the 1st (2): Small Litany, then we sing the resurrectional Sessional Hymns in the tone of the week, from the Octoechos; after the 2nd (3): Small Litany, then we sing the resurrectional Sessional Hymns in the tone of the week, from the Octoechos.  
We sing the Polyeleos.  
Hypakoe: Hypakoe Tone V.  
Gradual (Hymns of Ascents): Gradual Tone V.  
Prokeimenon: Arise, O Lord my God, let Thy hand be raised, for Thou reignest forever (Tone V).  
Matins Gospel VI: (114) Luke 24:36-53.  
Psalm 50 and Post-Gospel Stichera: Glory Apostles, Both Now Theotokos, Have Mercy, Jesus Having Risen.  
At the Canon: Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Octoechos - 4 (including the heirmos), Octoechos - 2, Menaion - 8.  

**Katavasia:** "Heirmos of the last canon" (Tone IV).  
At Ode 8 (Glory): "Let us bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, the Lord."  
At Ode IX, we sing the Magnification ('My soul magnifies the Lord...') and the refrains ('More honorable than the Cherubim...').  
Holy is the Lord our God (3x).  
Exapostilarion: Holy Is the Lord in Tone V; Gospel Exapostilarion VI; Exapostilarion of Hieromartyr Mocius; Theotokion of Gospel Exapostilarion VI.  
At the Praises, we sing 8 Stichera: 4 from the Octoechos, and 4 from the Menaion.  
Doxology: Sunday Resurrection (Sung).  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing: Sunday Dismissal Troparion 'Today salvation has come to the world' in Tone I; Troparion of Cyril and Methodius; Both now: Theotokion.  
We sing: Glory... Gospel Sticheron I in Tone V; Both now: Most Blessed Art Thou.

Kontakia: at the First and Ninth Hours – Resurrectional kontakion; at the Third Hour – Kontakion of St. Cyril; at the Sixth Hour – Kontakion of Temple.
