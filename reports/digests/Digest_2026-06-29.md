# TYPICON: MONDAY, JUNE 29th, 2026.

CHIEF APOSTLES PETER AND PAUL - TONE IV.  
Chief Apostles Peter and Paul  
Monday service combined with that of Chief Apostles Peter and Paul.  
Vestment colour: Bright (Green).  

## SMALL VESPERS

*At Lord, I Call…* we sing 4 Stichera of the Saint from the Menaion; Glory, Both now: Theotokion.  
Prokeimenon: Psalm Lord Is King 92  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 4 Aposticha Saint from the Menaion; Glory... Saint; Both now: Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing the Troparion of Peter and Paul; Glory, Both now: Theotokion.  

## GREAT VESPERS

Vestments (priest): epitrachelion, phelonion. The phelonion is blessed and kissed before the Entrance.  
Fasting Rule: No fasting restrictions.  
Censing: Priest censes entire church during Psalm 103.  
Censing: Deacon performs Great censing of the entire church during 'Lord, I Call'.  
*At Lord, I Call…* we sing 8 Stichera of the Saint from the Menaion; Glory... Doxastikon of Peter and Paul; Both now: Dogmatic Theotokion in the tone of the week.  
Censing: Small censing at the entrance.  
The Royal Doors are opened.  
Prokeimenon: Their sound hath gone forth into all the earth, and their words unto the ends of the world. (Tone VIII).  
We sing the Litiya stichera: 5 stichera of the Saint from the Menaion; Glory... Doxastikon of Litiya; Both now: Theotokion of Litiya.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing the Aposticha; Glory, Both now: Aposticha Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing the Troparion of Peter and Paul; Glory, Both now: Theotokion.  
At the Blessing of Loaves (Artoklasia):  
- Rubric: Priest blesses five loaves, wheat, wine, and oil.  
- Troparia to sing: Rejoice O Virgin (theotokos) x3.  
- Priest action: Stand before the table with loaves. Read the Artoklasia prayer. Sign the loaves crosswise with one loaf.  
- Deacon action: Cense the loaves during the prayer.  
- Choir action: Sing troparia as prescribed (see troparia array).  

## FESTAL MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing: Troparion of Chief Apostles Peter and Paul, twice; Glory, Both now. Theotokion, once.  

We read Kathismata 4 and 5.  

After the Kathismata (4 & 5): We sing the Sessional Hymns of the Saint.  
We sing the Polyeleos.  
Anabathmoi (Hymns of Ascents): Anabathmoi Tone Antiphon 4:1.  
Gradual (Anabathmoi): Antiphon Tone 1:4.  
Prokeimenon: Arise, O Lord, help us, and deliver us for Thy name's sake (Tone IV).  
And that we may be accounted worthy of hearing the holy Gospel, let us pray to the Lord God. Wisdom, stand upright! Let us hear the holy Gospel. **Matins Gospel:** Gospel of the Feast (None): John 21:15-25. Response: Glory to Thee, O Lord, glory to Thee.  
At the Canon: Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Menaion - 12 (including the heirmos).  

**Katavasia:** "Heirmos of the last canon" (Tone IV).  
At Ode 8 (Glory): "Let us bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, the Lord."  
At Ode IX, we sing the Magnification ('My soul magnifies the Lord...') and the refrains ('More honorable than the Cherubim...').  
It is truly meet (Axion Estin).  
Exapostilarion: Exapostilarion of Peter and Paul.  
Doxology: Great Doxology (sung).  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing: Troparion of Peter and Paul in Tone I; Both now: Theotokion.  

## HOURS

**At the Hours:** At all the hours: Troparion of Chief Apostles Peter and Paul.  
Kontakia: at all the hours – Kontakion of **Chief Apostles Peter and Paul.**.
