# TYPICON: FRIDAY, JUNE 19th, 2026.

AP. JUDE, BROTHER OF THE LORD - TONE II.  
St. Ap. Jude, brother of the Lord  
Friday service combined with that of St. Ap. Jude, Brother of the Lord.  
Vestment colour: Bright (Green).  

## GREAT VESPERS

Vestments (priest): epitrachelion, phelonion. The phelonion is blessed and kissed before the Entrance.  
Fasting Rule: Fish and wine permitted for the feast.  
Censing: Priest censes entire church during Psalm 103.  
Censing: Deacon performs Great censing of the entire church during 'Lord, I Call'.  
*At Lord, I Call…* we sing 8 Stichera of the Saint from the Menaion; Glory... Doxastikon of Jude Apostle; Both now: Dogmatic Theotokion in the tone of the week.  
Censing: Small censing at the entrance.  
The Royal Doors are opened.  
Prokeimenon: Daily Prokeimenon for Friday evening, Tone VII.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 daily Aposticha from the Octoechos; Glory, Both now: Aposticha Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing the Troparion of Jude Apostle; Glory, Both now: Theotokion.  

## SMALL COMPLINE

Canon: Departed from the Octoechos.  
Troparia: Troparion of the Cross (first place, Wednesday/Friday), *"O God of our fathers..."* and the following three troparia.  

## MIDNIGHT OFFICE (DAILY)

Midnight Troparia: Troparion of the weekday, Troparion of the Saint (if any), daily Theotokion.  
Prayer of St. Mardarius.  

## FESTAL MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing: Troparion of Ap. Jude, brother of the Lord, twice; Glory, Both now. Theotokion, once.  

We read Kathismata 13 and 14.  

After the Kathismata (13 & 14): We sing the Sessional Hymns of the Saint.  
We sing the Polyeleos.  
Anabathmoi (Hymns of Ascents): Anabathmoi Tone Antiphon 4:1.  
Gradual (Anabathmoi): Antiphon Tone 1:4.  
Prokeimenon: Arise, O Lord my God, in the precept which Thou hast commanded (Tone II).  
And that we may be accounted worthy of hearing the holy Gospel, let us pray to the Lord God. Wisdom, stand upright! Let us hear the holy Gospel. **Matins Gospel:** Gospel to Saint Ap. Jude, brother of the Lord: Luke 10:16-21. Response: Glory to Thee, O Lord, glory to Thee.  
At the Canon: Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Octoechos - 6 (including the heirmos), Menaion - 8.  

**Katavasia:** "Heirmos of the last canon" (Tone IV).  
At Ode 8 (Glory): "Let us bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, the Lord."  
At Ode IX, we sing the Magnification ('My soul magnifies the Lord...') and the refrains ('More honorable than the Cherubim...').  
It is truly meet (Axion Estin).  
Exapostilarion: Exapostilarion of Jude Apostle.  
At the Praises, we sing 4 Stichera: 4 from the Menaion; Glory... Doxastikon of the Saint; Other: Now and ever....  
Doxology: Great Doxology (sung).  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing: Troparion of Jude Apostle in Tone I; Both now: Theotokion.  

## HOURS

**At the Hours:** At all the hours: Troparion of Ap. Jude, brother of the Lord.  
Kontakia: at all the hours – Kontakion of Ap. Jude, brother of the Lord.  

## DIVINE LITURGY OF ST. JOHN CHRYSOSTOM

Psalms of Typica; Beatitudes on 4: Menaion - 4.  
**Troparia and Kontakia:**  
Troparion of the Cross.  
Troparion of Ap. Jude, brother of the Lord.  
Glory... Kontakion of Ap. Jude, brother of the Lord.  
Both now: Kontakion of the Cross.  
**Prokeimenon (Feast - sung twice):** *of the day* Tone VII  
**Prokeimenon (Saint - sung once, without verse):** *of Ap. Jude, brother of the Lord*  
**Epistle:** Romans 11:25-36  
**Epistle:** *of Ap. Jude, brother of the Lord*  
**Alleluia:** sung in Tone VII.  
**Alleluia:** *of Ap. Jude, brother of the Lord*  
**Gospel:** Matthew 12:1-8  
**Gospel:** *of Ap. Jude, brother of the Lord*  
**Communion Hymn:** "O Lord, save Thy people."  
**Post-Communion Hymn:** "We have seen the true light, we have received the heavenly Spirit..."  
Dismissal: May Christ our true God...
