# TYPICON: SATURDAY, JUNE 27th, 2026.

VEN. SAMPSON, HOST OF STRANGERS - TONE III.  
St. Ven. Sampson, Host of Strangers  
Saturday service combined with that of St. Ven. Sampson, Host of Strangers.  
Vestment colour: Bright (Green).  

## DAILY VESPERS

At Vespers, Kathisma 18 is read.  
Glory... Doxastikon of Ven Sampson Host Strangers; Both now: Dogmatic Theotokion in the tone of the week.  
Prokeimenon: Tone I (Saturday of the Octoechos): "Thy mercy, O Lord, shall follow me all the days of my life"  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 daily Aposticha from the Octoechos; Glory, Both now: Aposticha Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing the Troparion of Ven Sampson Host Strangers; Glory, Both now: Theotokion.  

## SMALL COMPLINE

Canon: Theotokos from the Octoechos.  
Troparia: Resurrectional Troparion in Tone 2, Troparion of the Temple, *"O God of our fathers..."* and the following three troparia.  

## MIDNIGHT OFFICE (SATURDAY)

Midnight Troparia: Troparion 'Uncreated Nature'.  
Prayer: Prayer Eustratius.  

## DAILY MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing: Troparion of Ven. Sampson, Host of Strangers, twice; Glory, Both now. Theotokion, once.  

We read Kathismata 16 and 17.  

After the Kathismata (16 & 17): We sing the Sessional Hymns from the Octoechos.  
At the Canon: Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Temple/menaion - 6 (including the heirmos), Menaion - 4, Octoechos - 4.  

**Katavasia:** "Heirmos of the last canon" (Tone IV).  
At Ode IX, we sing the Magnification ('My soul magnifies the Lord...') and the refrains ('More honorable than the Cherubim...').  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 daily Aposticha from the Octoechos; Glory, Both now: Aposticha Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing: Troparion of Ven Sampson Host Strangers in Tone I; Both now: Theotokion.  
Dismissal Theotokion (Tone 1, Saturday): Dismissal Theotokion for Saturday in Tone 1.  

## HOURS

**At the Hours:** At the First Hour: Troparion of the Day; At the Third and Ninth Hours: Troparion of Ven. Sampson, Host of Strangers; At the Sixth Hour: Troparion of the Temple.  
Kontakia: at the First Hour – Day; at the Third and Ninth Hours – Kontakion of Ven. Sampson, Host of Strangers; at the Sixth Hour – Temple.  

## DIVINE LITURGY OF ST. JOHN CHRYSOSTOM

Psalms of Typica; Beatitudes.  
**Troparia and Kontakia:**  
Troparion of Ven. Sampson, Host of Strangers.  
Troparion of the Temple.  
Glory... Kontakion of the Temple.  
Both now: Kontakion of Ven. Sampson, Host of Strangers.  
**Prokeimenon:** *of the day* Tone I  
**Epistle:** Romans 8:14-21  
**Alleluia:** sung in Tone I.  
**Gospel:** Matthew 9:9-13  
**Communion Hymn:** "Blessed are they whom Thou hast chosen."  
**Post-Communion Hymn:** "We have seen the true light, we have received the heavenly Spirit..."  
Dismissal: May Christ our true God...
