# TYPICON: MONDAY, JUNE 22nd, 2026.

HIEROMARTYR EUSEBIUS, BISHOP OF SAMOSATA - TONE III.  
Hieromartyr Eusebius, Bishop of Samosata  
Monday service combined with that of Hieromartyr Eusebius, Bishop of Samosata.  
Vestment colour: Bright (Red).  

## DAILY VESPERS

No kathisma is appointed.  
Glory... Doxastikon of the Saint; Both now: Theotokion from the Horologion or Octoechos.  
Prokeimenon: Tone I (Monday of the Octoechos): "Thy mercy, O Lord, shall follow me all the days of my life"  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 daily Aposticha from the Octoechos; Glory, Both now: Aposticha Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** Troparion of Hieromartyr Eusebius, Bishop of Samosata; Glory, Both now: Dismissal Theotokion in Tone III.  

## SMALL COMPLINE

Canon: Theotokos from the Octoechos.  
Troparia: Monday Troparion from the Octoechos, Troparion of the Temple, *"O God of our fathers..."* and the following three troparia.  

## MIDNIGHT OFFICE (DAILY)

Midnight Troparia: Troparion of the weekday, Troparion of the Temple, Troparion of the Saint (if any), daily Theotokion.  
Prayer of St. Mardarius.  

## DAILY MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing the Troparion of the Archangels, twice; Glory, Both now: Dismissal Theotokion in Tone VIII.  

We read Kathismata 4 and 5.  

After the Kathismata (4 & 5): We sing the Sessional Hymns from the Octoechos.  
At the Canon: Order of the Canon: First Canon of the Octoechos with the Heirmos on 6; second Canon of the Octoechos on 4; Canon of the Saint on 4. Katavasia: Heirmos of the last canon (of the Saint) after Odes 3, 6, 8, and 9.  

Note: At the 9th Ode, the priest censes as at Great Matins.  
At Ode IX, we sing the Magnification ('My soul magnifies the Lord...') and the refrains ('More honorable than the Cherubim...').  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 daily Aposticha from the Octoechos; Glory, Both now: Aposticha Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** Troparion of Hieromartyr Eusebius, Bishop of Samosata; Glory, Both now: Dismissal Theotokion for Monday in Tone I.  

## HOURS

Troparia: First Hour – Day; Third & Ninth Hours – Hieromartyr Eusebius, Bishop of Samosata; Sixth Hour – Temple. Kontakia: First Hour – Day; Third & Ninth Hours – Hieromartyr Eusebius, Bishop of Samosata; Sixth Hour – Temple.  

## DIVINE LITURGY OF ST. JOHN CHRYSOSTOM

Psalms of Typica; Beatitudes on 6: 3 from the Octoechos, 3 from Ode III of the Saint.  
**Troparia and Kontakia:**  
Troparion of the Angels.  
Troparion of Hieromartyr Eusebius, Bishop of Samosata.  
Kontakion of the Angels.  
Glory... Kontakion of Hieromartyr Eusebius, Bishop of Samosata.  
Both now: Kontakion of the Temple.  
**Prokeimenon:** Tone I: "The righteous shall rejoice in the Lord"  
**Epistle:** of the day (Romans 12:4-15)  
**Alleluia:** Tone I, with verses of the day.  
**Gospel:** of the day (Matthew 12:9-13)  
**Communion Hymn:** of the day: "He maketh His angels spirits."  
**Post-Communion Hymn:** "We have seen the true light, we have received the heavenly Spirit..."  
Dismissal: May Christ our true God...
