### 2. Scriptural Readings Accuracy
#### 2.1 Matins Gospel Response
**Gap:** **Incorrect response after the Gospel at Matins.**  
- **Digest output:** “**Matins Gospel:** Gospel to Apostles Bartholomew and Barnabas: Luke 10:16-21. Response: **Glory to Thy Passion, O Lord**.”  
- **Canonical rule:** The response “Glory to Thy Passion, O Lord” is used **only** for the Passion Gospels of Great Friday (and similarly on Great Thursday/Matins of Great Saturday). For a saint’s Gospel, the standard response is **“Glory to Thee, O Lord, glory to Thee”** (or simply “Glory to Thee, O Lord”).  
- **Source:** The Dolnytsky Glossary does not contain a specific note for this, but the rubrics for Matins of Great Friday explicitly limit that Passion response (see *Part 4 Triodion*). All other Gospels (saints, feasts, daily) use the normal response.  
- **Correction:** Change to **“Glory to Thee, O Lord, glory to Thee.”** Remove the Passion acclamation.

#### 2.2 Katavasia
**Gap:** **Katavasia does not match the Feast of the Eucharist.**  
- **Digest output:** “**Katavasia:** ‘Come, O ye people, let us sing a song to Christ our God’ (Tone II).”  
- **Actual Feast Katavasia:** The Eucharist (Feast of the Body of Christ) uses its **own Katavasia** from the Triodion, usually **“The bread of heaven He gave them”** (Tone 5) or the heirmoi of the feast’s own canon. “Come, O ye people” is the Katavasia for the Dormition of the Theotokos and other Theotokos feasts.  
- **Correction:** Replace with the **Katavasia of the Eucharist** as given in the **Pentecostarion** for the Apodosis of that feast (e.g., “Let us raise our hearts to God” or the specified heirmoi).

#### 2.3 Communion Hymn
**Gap:** **Communion Hymn for the Feast is incorrect.**  
- **Digest output:** “**Communion Hymn:** ‘Praise the Lord from the heavens, praise Him in the highest. And of the Saint: Their sound hath gone forth …’”  
- **Actual Communion Hymn for the Eucharist:** The proper Communion Hymn for the Feast of the Body of Christ is **“Receive the Body of Christ; taste the fountain of immortality”** (from the Liturgy). “Praise the Lord” is the ordinary Sunday Communion Hymn, not the feast’s proper.  
- **Correction:** Use the combined formula: **“Receive the Body of Christ; taste the fountain of immortality”** for the Feast, followed by the saint’s **“Their sound hath gone forth …”** (as the digest already lists for the saint). Keep the sequencing (Feast first, then Saint).

### 3. Precedence / Combination Rules
#### 3.1 Stichera at “Lord, I have cried” – Doxastikon of Saint Possibly Omitted
**Gap:** **The Doxastikon of the saint is not given, but substituted by a feast sticheron at “Glory.”**  
- **Digest output:** “4 Feast stichera … and 4 stichera of the Saint … **Glory, Both now: sticheron of the Feast (Eucharist)**.”  
- **Canonical rule for a Polyeleos saint on an Afterfeast/Apodosis:** If the saint has a Doxastikon (and Apostles Bartholomew and Barnabas certainly have one), the order is: **Glory: Doxastikon of the saint; Both now: sticheron of the Feast** (or the Theotokion of the Feast). The feast’s Doxastikon does not replace the saint’s own.  
- **Source:** The Menaion note for 11 June sends us to the rule of Constantine and Helen (p. 300-301), which is part of the general *typikon* for a Polyeleos saint on the Apodosis of a Lord’s feast. That rule consistently gives the saint’s Doxastikon at “Glory” when present.  
- **Correction:** Change to: “**Glory: Doxastikon of Apostles Bartholomew and Barnabas; Both now: sticheron of the Feast.**”

#### 3.2 Small Litany Placement at Kathismata

This is correct for Great Matins; no violation against the rule in the Menaion for a Polyeleos saint.
