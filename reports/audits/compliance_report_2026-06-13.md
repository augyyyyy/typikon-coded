## 3. Precedence / Combination Rules

### 3.1 Small Compline – Resurrectional Troparion Tone
**Gap:** The Resurrectional Troparion at Small Compline is given as “Tone 2.” This is erroneous.  
**Rule:** At Small Compline, the Resurrectional Troparion follows the **tone of the current week**, which for Friday evening (June 12) is the tone that began the previous Sunday. The new tone (Tone 1) starts only at Saturday Vespers, not before. Therefore the Friday‑evening Small Compline should use the tone of the preceding Sunday (Tone 8, given the normal descending sequence of tones).  
**Citation:** From the Horologion and standard Octoechos rubrics (implied in the Glossary, compline-and-midnight-office, but not explicit); nonetheless the principle that the Octoechos tone does not change until Saturday Vespers is universal.  
**Correction:** Replace “Resurrectional Troparion in Tone 2” with the Resurrectional Troparion in the **tone of the week that ended on Friday evening** (Tone 8).  

### 3.2 Divine Liturgy – Troparia & Kontakia Order
**Gap:** The digests omit the **Troparion of the Day** and **Kontakion of the Day**, and they place the Kontakion of the Saint as “Both now” instead of the proper Theotokion. This violates the Slavonic typikon’s rule for weekday Liturgy when a simple saint occurs.  
**Rule:**  
- On weekdays with a simple saint, the Slavonic typikon prescribes the *entire service of the day* (i.e., the Octoechos troparion and kontakion) alongside the saint’s and temple’s.  
- The kontakia end with the **Kontakion for the Dead** (“With the saints give rest”) on *Saturday* (or the day’s kontakion) and the **Theotokion**, not with the saint’s kontakion as the final “Both now.”  
- See Glossary, weekday-liturgy-commemorations, fn. 91: “Slavonic typikons prescribe the whole service to the day and to the saint” and the note about the Saturday kontakion for the dead. Also troparia-of-the-day-temple-and-saints, fn. 20.  

**Correction:** Reorder the Divine Liturgy troparia and kontakia as follows:  
- **Troparia:** Troparion of the Day (Saturday Octoechos, e.g., “Apostles, Martyrs, Prophets…”), Troparion of the Saint, Troparion of the Temple.  
- **Kondakia after the Entrance:** Glory… Kontakion for the Dead (or Kontakion of the Day), then Kontakion of the Temple; Both now: Theotokion (of the day, e.g., “O undisputed intercessor…”), **not** the saint’s kontakion.  
- **No** “Both now: Kontakion of Martyr Aquilina…”.
