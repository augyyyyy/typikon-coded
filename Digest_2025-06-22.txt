TYPICON: SUNDAY, JUNE 22nd, 2025.
SAINT OF THE DAY (6-22) - TONE 1

GREAT VESPERS
At O Lord, I have cried... we sing 7 resurrectional stichera from the Octoechos, 3 saint from the Menaion; Glory... (no saint doxastikon); Both now... Dogmatic Theotokion.
If Litiya is performed: The sticheron of the temple.
At the Aposticha: We sing the aposticha.

MATINS
At The Lord is God ... we sing the troparion.
After each cathisma we sing the sessional hymns.
After, the evlogitaria: the hypakoe, Hymns of Ascents, Prokeimenon, Let everything that has breath: in the tone of the week.
At the Canon:
Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Octoechos - 4 (including the irmos), Octoechos - 3, Octoechos - 3, Menaion - 4.
After the Dismissal of Matins: Glory... both now... Gospel Sticheron.

HOURS
At all the hours: Resurrectional troparion, Glory... forefeast/saint.
Kontakia: according to the Typikon.

DIVINE LITURGY
Psalms of Typica; Beatitudes.
