# TYPICON: SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 22nd, 2026.
## SUNDAY OF ORTHODOXY: RESTORATION OF THE HOLY ICONS - TONE IV
Finding of the relics of the holy martyrs in Eugenius.  
Sunday service combined with the triodion.  
The service to St. Finding of the relics of the holy martyrs in Eugenius. is transferred to the previous Friday at Compline, or another convenient time, whenever the ecclesiarch so wishes.  
**Vestment colour:** Bright [deep gold, or, in some places, gold with purple].  
## GREAT VESPERS
**At O Lord, I have cried…** we sing 6 resurrectional stichera from the Octoechos, 4 Stichera from the Triodion; Glory... doxasticon from the Triodion; Both now... Dogmatic Theotokion in the tone of the week.  
**If Litiya is performed:** The sticheron of the temple.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 1 Aposticha Resurrection 1 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 2 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 3 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 4 from the Octoechos; Glory... doxasticon from the Triodion; Both now... Theotokion Aposticha Exceeding Wonder in Tone 6.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:**
We sing We sing the Sunday (resurrectional) troparion in the tone of the week; Glory, both now... Theotokion Dismissal in Tone 4.  
Or, if Vigil is served: Rejoice, O Virgin Theotokos... twice, troparion from the Triodion, once.  
## MATINS
After each cathisma we sing the sessional hymns from the Octoechos.  
We sing the 17th Kathisma (Psalm 118).  
After, the Evlogitaria: the Hypakoë (Tone IV), Hymns of Ascents, Prokeimenon, Let everything that has breath: in the tone of the week.  
**Matins Gospel IV**: Lk. 24: 1-12.  
After Psalm 50: instead of the usual hymns, we sing: Glory: Open to me the doors of repentance..., Both now: On the paths of salvation... and after the refrain Have mercy on me, O God, the sticheron: When I think of the many evil things I have done.  
**At the Canon (abbreviated for parish use):**  
  At each ode, the irmos of the resurrection from the Octoechos.  
  2 troparia from canon I in the Triodion.  ℟.  Glory to You, O God, glory to You!  
  1 troparia from canon II in the Triodion.  ℟.  Holy hierarch Gregory pray to God for us!  
  Glory... both now... Triodion.  
  Catabasia Tone IV "I shall open my mouth . . . ".  
Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Resurrection - 4 (including the irmos), Cross Resurrection - 2, Theotokos - 2, Triodion - 6.  
After Ode III: Sessional hymns from the Triodion (found after Ode III).  
After Ode VI: kontakion and ikos from the Triodion are sung.  
After Ode VIII: we sing the Magnificat.  
Holy is the Lord... Tone IV.  
**Exapostilarion** - Eothinon 4; Eothinon 4 Theotokion.  
**At the Praises:** We sing 9 stichera: 5 from the Octoechos, and 4 from the Triodion; Glory... Glory to God; Other: Now and Ever.  
After the Dismissal of Matins: Glory... both now... Gospel Sticheron IV.  
## HOURS
At all the hours: Resurrectional troparion, Glory... Trop Triodion.
Kontakia: at the First, Third, Sixth and Ninth – Triodion.
## DIVINE LITURGY OF SAINT BASIL THE GREAT
**Troparia and Kontakia:**
Troparion of the Resurrection in Tone 4.
Troparion of the Triodion.
Glory... Kontakion of the Triodion.
Both now... Theotokion 'To you, O Mother of God...'.  
**Prokimenon:**	Tone IV: "Blessed Are You, O Lord, God of Our Fathers..."
**Epistle:**		Heb 11:24-26, 32-40; 12:1-2
**Alleluia:**		Tone VIII
**Gospel:**		Jn 1:43-51
**Kinonicon:**	"Praise the Lord from the heavens, praise Him in the highest."
## NOTES & FOOTNOTES
1. In the refectory we eat food prepared with oil, but no fish. If it is the custom, we are permitted to drink two cups of wine to the Glory of God; and at the evening meal, two cups. (This instruction is given in the Lviv Triodion of 1906).  
2. This, according to the Lenten Triodion of Mother Maria and Metropolitan Kallistos (Ware). The Lviv Triodion of 1906 prescribes 10 troparia: six from the Octoechos and 4 from the Triodion. Since the custom of taking the troparia at the Beatitudes has fallen out of practice in most places, it is prudent to adhere to the minimum, until such time as it is fully restored and appreciated.  
3. The Muscovite Typicon, the Antholody for Worship, and the Triodion of Mother Maria and Metropolitan Kallistos (Ware), all conclude with the kontakion of from the Triodion after the Resurrectional troparion, as if it were [a feastal] one of the Lord; however, our service books prescribe the Theotokion, because the kontakion of the Triodion is penitential, not festal. (This instruction given in Dol'nytsky).
