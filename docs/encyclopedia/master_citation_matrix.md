 207: 7.	If there be several Prokimena, Alleluias, Apostle-Gospels and Communion Hymns, then one must know how to take them. Know first, that in church typikons more than two are not given, with the exception of Apostle-Gospels, of which only in Slavonic typikons three are given, for the Slavs are richer in supplications than the Greeks. For where three readings coincide, the Greek typikons do not give the sequential one, but the Slavonic ones take it under the refrain of the first reading, as if one with it[^94]. When this is impossible, on the occasion of some great feast, then they order to take it on the day before under the refrain with the sequential one[^95]. However, this rule has been preserved among us perhaps only in cathedral churches, for in others usually only one Apostle-Gospel is taken, as also one service either of the day, or of the saint, or one for need. If, therefore, there be two Prokimena and two Alleluias, then both are taken under one heading, that is both Prokimena - before the refrain of the first reading, both Alleluias - after the completion of the second reading. However, the first Prokimenon is taken twice, namely: once - before and once - after the verse, the second - only once and without a verse, for in this case the Typikon does not give a verse. Similarly also "Alleluia": both of its verses are taken only of the first service, and of the second - only one first verse[^96]. The Heading, that is from whom: from Paul, from Matthew or from another, is taken only of the first reading, and the others usually are not taken, although it would be better to announce the headings of other readings as well, for it is not indifferent to the listeners to know the reading of which holy author is being proclaimed; and for this reason only would the rubric make certain sense, which orders to take the sequential reading under the refrain, for taking one reading under the refrain of another means merging it with the previous one because its heading is not announced. If the heading of the following third reading, which is not taken under the refrain, is not named, then it should have been said that this third reading is also taken under the refrain. "Let us be attentive" is announced only at the beginning of the first reading. If there be two Communion Hymns, then "Alleluia" is repeated only upon the completion of the second[^97].
```
