# TYPICON: WEDNESDAY, JUNE 10th, 2026.

HIEROMARTYR TIMOTHY - TONE I.  
Wednesday service combined with that of the Triodion.  
Vestment colour: Bright (White).  

## DAILY VESPERS

At Vespers, Kathisma 18 is read.  
*At O Lord, I have cried…* we sing 8 Feast Stichera from the Triodion; Glory... Feast Doxastikon; Both now: Sticheron of the Feast (Eucharist).  
Prokeimenon: Tone VII (Wednesday of the Octoechos): "My help cometh from the Lord, Who hath made heaven and earth"  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 Aposticha Feast from the Menaion; Glory... Feast Doxastikon; Both now: Theotokion of the Feast.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing the Troparion of the Feast; Glory, Both now: Theotokion.  

## SMALL COMPLINE

Canon: Theotokos from the Octoechos.  
Troparia: Troparion of the Cross (first place, Wednesday/Friday), *"O God of our fathers..."* and the following three troparia.  

## MIDNIGHT OFFICE (DAILY)

Midnight Troparia: Troparion of the weekday, Troparion of the Saint (if any), daily Theotokion.  
Prayer of St. Mardarius.  

## DAILY MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing: Troparion of the feast, twice; Glory, Both now. Troparion of the feast, once.  

We read Kathismata 8, 9 and 10.  

After the Kathismata (8, 9, and 10): We sing the Sessional Hymns from the Octoechos.  
At the Canon: Order of the Canon: First Canon of the Octoechos with the Heirmos on 6; second Canon of the Octoechos on 4; Canon of the Saint on 4. Katavasia: Heirmos of the last canon (of the Saint) after Odes 3, 6, 8, and 9.  

Note: At the 9th Ode, the priest censes as at Great Matins.  
At Ode IX, we sing the Festal magnification and the Heirmos of Ode IX of the Feast.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 Aposticha Feast from the Menaion; Glory... Feast Doxastikon; Both now: Theotokion of the Feast.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing: Troparion of the Feast in Tone VIII; Glory, Both now: Troparion of the Feast.  

## HOURS

**At the Hours:** At all the hours: Troparion of the feast.  
Kontakia: at all the hours – Kontakion of the Eucharist.  

## DIVINE LITURGY OF ST. JOHN CHRYSOSTOM

Psalms of Typica; Beatitudes on 6: Triodion - 6.  
**Troparia and Kontakia:**  
Troparion of the Feast.  
Kontakion of the Feast.  
**Prokeimenon:** Tone VI: "O Lord, save Your people and bless Your inheritance"  
**Epistle:** of the day (Romans 8:2-13)  
**Alleluia:** Tone VI, with verses of the day.  
**Gospel:** of the day (Matthew 10:16-22)  
**Communion Hymn:** of the day: "Receive the Body of Christ; taste the fountain of immortality."  
**Post-Communion Hymn:** "We have seen the true light, we have received the heavenly Spirit..."  
Dismissal: May Christ our true God...
