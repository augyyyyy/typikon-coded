# TYPICON: SUNDAY, MARCH 15th, 2026.
## SUNDAY IV OF THE GREAT FAST: VEN. JOHN OF THE LADDER - TONE VII
Martyr Agapius and those with him.  
Sunday service combined with the triodion.  
The service to St. Martyr Agapius and those with him. is transferred to the previous Friday at Compline, or another convenient time, whenever the ecclesiarch so wishes.  
**Vestment colour:** Bright [Gold or green for ascetics].  
## GREAT VESPERS
**At O Lord, I have cried…** we sing 6 resurrectional stichera from the Octoechos, 4 Stichera from the Triodion; Glory... doxasticon from the Triodion; Both now... Dogmatic Theotokion in the tone of the week.  
**If Litiya is performed:** The sticheron of the temple.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 1 Aposticha Resurrection 1 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 2 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 3 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 4 from the Octoechos; Glory... doxasticon from the Triodion; Both now... Theotokion Aposticha in Tone 2.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:**
We sing We sing the Sunday (resurrectional) troparion in the tone of the week; Glory, both now... Theotokion Dismissal in Tone 7.  
## MATINS
After each cathisma we sing the sessional hymns from the Octoechos.  
We sing the 17th Kathisma (Psalm 118).  
After, the Evlogitaria: the Hypakoë (Tone VII), Hymns of Ascents, Prokeimenon, Let everything that has breath: in the tone of the week.  
**Matins Gospel VII**: Jn. 20: 1-10.  
After Psalm 50: instead of the usual hymns, we sing: Glory: Open to me the doors of repentance..., Both now: On the paths of salvation... and after the refrain Have mercy on me, O God, the sticheron: When I think of the many evil things I have done.  
**At the Canon (abbreviated for parish use):**  
  At each ode: Irmos of resurrection, Resurrection troparion (with response), Saint/Triodion troparion (with response), Glory... both now.  
Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Resurrection - 4 (including the irmos), Theotokos - 2, Triodion - 8.  
After Ode III: Sessional hymns from the Triodion (found after Ode III).  
After Ode VI: kontakion and ikos from the Triodion are sung.  
After Ode VIII: we sing the Magnificat.  
Holy is the Lord... Tone VII.  
**Exapostilarion** - Eothinon 7; Eothinon 7 Theotokion.  
**At the Praises:** We sing 9 stichera: 8 from the Octoechos, and 1 from the Triodion; Glory... Glory to God; Other: Now and Ever.  
After the Dismissal of Matins: Glory... both now... Gospel Sticheron VII.  
## HOURS
At all the hours: Resurrectional troparion, Glory... Trop Triodion Saint.
Kontakia: at the First, Third, Sixth and Ninth – St. John Climacus.

## NOTES & FOOTNOTES
1. The Lvov Triodion of 1906, as well as that of Mother Maria and Metropolitan Kallistos, prescribe the verse "Arise, O Lord my God...", however, this is already one of the usual verses added on Sunday, and if 9 stichera are to be sung, an additional verse is required. The verse from Psalm 111 has been provided here as an option, but is not specifically prescribed in any book.  
2. The Lvov Triodion prescribes 8 troparia in the tone of the week (Resurrectional, from the Octoechos), and does not mention any from the Triodion.  
3. The Anthology for Worship concludes with the kontakion of the saint (from the Triodion), as if it were [a festal] one of the Lord; however, concerning this, Dol'nytsky mentions that: "our service books prescribe the Theotokion, because the kontakion of the Triodion is penitential, not festal." While the kontakion of St. John is not a penitential one, we can apply the same logic here, using the pattern set forth for a polyeleos or vigil-rank feast. In common Galician practice, the Theotokion is sung in the same tone as the "Glory". Other books prescribe Undaunted patroness... in Tone VI. However, in anticipation of Akathist Saturday, during the season of the Triodion it seems appropriate to use the kontakion of the Theotokos in Tone VIII.
