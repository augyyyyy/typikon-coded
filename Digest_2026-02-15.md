# TYPICON: SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 15th, 2026.
## CHEESEFARE - FORGIVENESS SUNDAY: EXPULSION OF ADAM FROM PARADISE - TONE III
Ap. Onesimus.  
Sunday service combined with the triodion.  
The service to St. Ap. Onesimus. is transferred to the previous Friday at Compline, or another convenient time, whenever the ecclesiarch so wishes.  
**Vestment colour:** Bright [Gold or deep gold].  
## GREAT VESPERS
**At O Lord, I have cried…** we sing 6 resurrectional stichera from the Octoechos, 4 Stichera from the Triodion; Glory... doxasticon from the Triodion; Both now... Dogmatic Theotokion in the tone of the week.  
**If Litiya is performed:** The sticheron of the temple.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 1 Aposticha Resurrection 1 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 2 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 3 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 4 from the Octoechos; Glory... doxasticon from the Triodion; Both now... Theotokion Aposticha in Tone 6.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:**
We sing We sing the Sunday (resurrectional) troparion in the tone of the week; Glory, both now... Theotokion Dismissal in Tone 3.  
Or, if Vigil is served: Rejoice, O Virgin Theotokos... thrice.  
## MATINS
After each cathisma we sing the sessional hymns from the Octoechos.  
We sing the Polyeleos. Magnification: sung if prescribed.  
After, the Evlogitaria: the Hypakoë (Tone III), Hymns of Ascents, Prokeimenon, Let everything that has breath: in the tone of the week.  
**Matins Gospel III**: Mk. 16: 9-20.  
After Psalm 50: instead of the usual hymns, we sing: Glory: Open to me the doors of repentance..., Both now: On the paths of salvation... and after the refrain Have mercy on me, O God, the sticheron: When I think of the many evil things I have done.  
**At the Canon (abbreviated for parish use):**  
  At each ode, the irmos of the resurrection from the Octoechos.  
  2 troparia from the Triodion.  ℟.  Glory to You, O God, glory to You!  
  Glory... both now... Triodion.  
  Catabasia Tone IV "Isreael of old crossed the depth . . . ".  
Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Resurrection - 4 (including the irmos), Cross Resurrection - 2, Theotokos - 2, Triodion - 6.  
After Ode III: Sessional hymns from the Triodion (found after Ode III).  
After Ode VI: kontakion and ikos from the Triodion are sung.  
After Ode VIII: we sing the Magnificat.  
Holy is the Lord... Tone III.  
**Exapostilarion** - Eothinon 3; Eothinon 3 Theotokion.  
**At the Praises:** We sing 9 stichera: 5 from the Octoechos, and 4 from the Triodion; Glory... Glory to God; Other: Now and Ever.  
After the Dismissal of Matins: Glory... both now... Gospel Sticheron III.  
## HOURS
At all the hours: Resurrectional troparion.
Kontakia: at the First, Third, Sixth and Ninth – Triodion.
## DIVINE LITURGY
**Troparia and Kontakia:**
Troparion of the Resurrection in Tone 3.
Troparion of the Temple.
Kontakion of the Temple.
Glory... Kontakion of the Triodion.
Both now... Kontakion 'Steadfast Protectress of Christians'.  
**Prokimenon:**	Tone VIII: "Pray and Give Praise..."
**Epistle:**		Rom 13:11 - 14:4
**Alleluia:**		Tone VI
**Gospel:**		Mt 6:14-21
**Kinonicon:**	"Praise the Lord from the heavens, praise Him in the highest."
## NOTES & FOOTNOTES
1. The Muscovite Typicon, the Antholody for Worship, and the Triodion of Mother Maria and Metropolitan Kallistos (Ware), all conclude with the kontakion of from the Triodion after the Resurrectional troparion, as if it were [a feastal] one of the Lord; however, our service books prescribe the Theotokion, because the kontakion of the Triodion is penitential, not festal.
