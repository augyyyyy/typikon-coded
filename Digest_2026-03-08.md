# TYPICON: SUNDAY, MARCH 8th, 2026.
## SUNDAY III OF THE GREAT FAST: VENERATION OF THE PRECIOUS CROSS - TONE VI
Ven. Theophylact.  
Sunday service combined with the triodion.  
The service to Ven. Theophylact. is transferred to the previous Friday at Compline, or another convenient time, whenever the ecclesiarch so wishes.  
**Vestment colour:** Bright [Red or burgundy or purple].  
## GREAT VESPERS
**At O Lord, I have cried…** we sing 6 resurrectional stichera from the Octoechos, 4 Stichera from the Triodion; Glory... doxasticon from the Triodion; Both now... Dogmatic Theotokion in the tone of the week.  
**If Litiya is performed:** The sticheron of the temple.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 1 Aposticha Resurrection 1 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 2 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 3 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 4 from the Octoechos; Glory, both now... Aposticha Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:**
We sing We sing the Sunday (resurrectional) troparion in the tone of the week; Glory, both now... Theotokion Dismissal in Tone 6.  
Or, if Vigil is served: Rejoice, O Virgin Theotokos... twice, troparion of the Cross, once.  
## MATINS
After each cathisma we sing the sessional hymns from the Octoechos.  
We sing the 17th Kathisma (Psalm 118).  
After, the Evlogitaria: the Hypakoë (Tone VI), Hymns of Ascents, Prokeimenon, Let everything that has breath: in the tone of the week.  
**Matins Gospel VI**: Lk. 24: 36-53.  
After Psalm 50: instead of the usual hymns, we sing: Glory: Open to me the doors of repentance..., Both now: On the paths of salvation... and after the refrain Have mercy on me, O God, the sticheron: When I think of the many evil things I have done.  
**At the Canon (abbreviated for parish use):**  
  At each ode, the irmos of the resurrection from the Octoechos.  
  1 troparion: canon of the Resurrection.  ℟.  Glory to Your, holy Resurrection, O Lord!  
  1 troparion: Triodion canon I.  ℟.  Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me!  
  1 troparion: Triodion canon II.  ℟.  Venerable father John, pray to God for us!.  
  Glory... both now... Triodion.  
  Catabasia Tone IV "I shall open my mouth . . . ".  
Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Resurrection - 4 (including the irmos), Theotokos - 2, Triodion - 8.  
After Ode III: Sessional hymns from the Triodion (found after Ode III).  
After Ode VI: kontakion and ikos from the Triodion are sung.  
After Ode VIII: we sing the Magnificat.  
Holy is the Lord... Tone VI.  
**Exapostilarion** - Eothinon 6; Eothinon 6 Theotokion.  
**At the Praises:** We sing 8 stichera: 4 from the Octoechos, and 4 from the Triodion; Glory... Glory to God; Other: Now and Ever.  
After the Dismissal of Matins: Glory... both now... Gospel Sticheron VI.  
## HOURS
At all the hours: Resurrectional troparion, Glory... Trop Triodion.
Kontakia: at the First, Third, Sixth and Ninth – Triodion.
## DIVINE LITURGY OF SAINT BASIL THE GREAT
**Troparia and Kontakia:**
Troparion of the Resurrection in Tone 6.
Troparion of the Cross.
Glory, both now... Kontakion of the Cross.  
**Prokimenon:**	Tone VI: "Save Your People..."
**Epistle:**		Heb 4:14 - 5:6
**Alleluia:**		Tone II
**Gospel:**		Mk 8:34 - 9:1
**Kinonicon:**	"Praise the Lord from the heavens, praise Him in the highest."
## NOTES & FOOTNOTES
1. This instruction is given in the Lviv Typicon of 1899, which assumes that Matins will be sung separately from Vespers. If Vigil is served, then the transfer should occur before the beginning of Vespers, as indicated in the Triodion.  
2. The Lviv Typicon of 1899 prescribes 9 stichera - 5 being from the Triodion, however, the Triodion of metropolitan Kallistos provides for only 4 stichera, prescribing a total of 8.
