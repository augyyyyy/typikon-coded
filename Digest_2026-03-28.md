# TYPICON: SATURDAY, MARCH 28th, 2026.
## SATURDAY OF THE RIGHTEOUS LAZARUS - TONE VIII
Saturday service combined with the triodion.  
**Vestment colour:** Bright [Gold or bright gold].  
## GREAT VESPERS
Glory... doxasticon from the Triodion; Both now... Dogmatic Theotokion in the tone of the week.  
**If Litiya is performed:** The sticheron of the temple; Glory... Glory; Both now... Theotokion.  
**At the Aposticha:**, we sing the Aposticha; Glory, both now... Aposticha Theotokion.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:**
We sing Troparion of the Day 6; Glory, both now... Day 6.  
## MATINS
After each cathisma we sing the sessional hymns: 1st Kathisma sessional hymns. 2nd Kathisma sessional hymns.  
We sing the Polyeleos. Magnification: sung if prescribed.  
**At the Canon (abbreviated for parish use):**  
  At each ode: 2 troparia from the first canon.  ℟. Glory to You, O God, glory to You!  
  2 troparia from the second canon.  ℟. Glory to You, O God, glory to You!  
  Glory... both now... second canon.  
  Catabasia Tone VIII "Having crossed the water . . . ".  
Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Triodion - 8 (including the irmos), Triodion Ii - 6.  
After Ode III: Sessional hymns from the Triodion (found after Ode III).  
After Ode VI: kontakion and ikos from the Triodion are sung.  
After Ode VIII: we sing the Magnificat.  
**At the Praises:** We sing 8 stichera: 8 from the Triodion; Glory... Glory to God; Other: Now and Ever.  
## HOURS
At all the hours: troparion of the feast.
Kontakia: at the First, Third, Sixth and Ninth – Feast.
## DIVINE LITURGY
**Troparia and Kontakia:**
Troparion of the Triodion.
Glory, both now... Kontakion of the Triodion.  
**Prokimenon:**	Tone III: "the Lord Is My Light..."
**Epistle:**		Heb 12:28 - 13:8
**Alleluia:**		Tone V
**Gospel:**		Jn 11:1-45
**Instead of 'It is truly proper':** Instead of 'It is truly proper', we sing the Irmos of Ode 9 of the Canon.
**Kinonicon:**	"Blessed are they whom Thou hast chosen..."
