# TYPICON: TUESDAY, JUNE 9th, 2026.

SAINT OF THE DAY - TONE I.  
Tuesday service combined with that of the Triodion.  
Vestment colour: Bright (White).  

## DAILY VESPERS

At Vespers, Kathisma 18 is read.  
*At O Lord, I have cried…* we sing 6 Stichera to the Saint; Glory... Doxastikon of the Saint; Both now: usual Theotokion in the tone of the Doxastikon and of the day of the week.  
Prokeimenon: Tone V (Tuesday of the Octoechos): "Save me, O God, by Thy name, and judge me by Thy strength"  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 Aposticha Feast from the Menaion; Glory... Feast Doxastikon; Both now: Theotokion of the Feast.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing the Troparion of the Feast; Glory, Both now: Theotokion.  

## SMALL COMPLINE

Canon: Theotokos from the Octoechos.  
Troparia: Tuesday Troparion from the Octoechos, Troparion of the Temple, *"O God of our fathers..."* and the following three troparia.  

## MIDNIGHT OFFICE (DAILY)

Midnight Troparia: Troparion of the weekday, Troparion of the Temple, Troparion of the Saint (if any), daily Theotokion.  
Prayer of St. Mardarius.  

## DAILY MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing: Troparion of the feast, twice; Glory, Both now. Troparion of the feast, once.  

We read Kathismata 6 and 7.  

After the Kathismata (6 & 7): We sing the Sessional Hymns from the Octoechos.  
At the Canon: Order of the Canon: First Canon of the Octoechos with the Heirmos on 4; second Canon of the Octoechos on 4; Canon of the Saint on 6. Katavasia: Heirmos of the last canon (of the Saint) after Odes 3, 6, 8, and 9.  

Note: At the 9th Ode, the priest censes as at Great Matins.  
At Ode IX, we sing the Festal magnification and the Heirmos of Ode IX of the Feast.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 Aposticha Feast from the Menaion; Glory... Feast Doxastikon; Both now: Theotokion of the Feast.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing: Troparion of the Feast in Tone VIII; Glory, Both now: Troparion of the Feast.  

## HOURS

**At the Hours:** At all the hours: Troparion of the feast.  
Kontakia: at all the hours – Kontakion of the Eucharist.  

## DIVINE LITURGY OF ST. JOHN CHRYSOSTOM

Psalms of Typica; Beatitudes on 6: Triodion - 6.  
**Troparia and Kontakia:**  
Troparion of the Feast.  
Kontakion of the Feast.  
**Prokeimenon:** Tone VII: "The righteous shall rejoice in the Lord and shall hope in Him"  
**Epistle:** of the day (Romans 7:14-8:2)  
**Alleluia:** Tone IV, with verses of the day.  
**Gospel:** of the day (Matthew 10:9-15)  
**Communion Hymn:** of the day: "In everlasting remembrance shall the righteous be; he shall not be afraid of evil tidings."  
**Post-Communion Hymn:** "We have seen the true light, we have received the heavenly Spirit..."  
Dismissal: May Christ our true God...
