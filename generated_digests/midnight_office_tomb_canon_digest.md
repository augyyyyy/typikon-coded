# Instead of the Midnight Office (Tomb Canon)
## Sung Before the Shroud, Immediately Before Resurrection Matins
*(Ukrainian: Замість Полунощниці)*

---

> **When served:** Immediately before Resurrection Matins — *"We sing it before the Shroud, immediately before Resurrection Matins"* (Part IV, line 720).

> **Governing source:** Dolnytsky Part IV, lines 719–727.

> **Title:** Dolnytsky titles this section *"Instead of the Midnight Office"* (line 719), indicating it replaces the usual Midnight Office on this night.

---

## VESTMENTS

> **Part IV, line 720:** *"…either only in epitrachelion and phelonion, as usual in the provinces, or in all sacred vestments of violet color, as in cathedral churches."*

| Option | Vestments | Context |
|---|---|---|
| **Provincial** | Epitrachelion + phelonion | *"as usual in the provinces"* (line 720) |
| **Cathedral** | All sacred vestments of violet color | *"as in cathedral churches"* (line 720) |

The service begins in **violet** and ends with the change to **bright** vestments (line 727).

---

## POSITION

> **Part IV, line 720:** *"We sing it before the Shroud."*

The sacred ministers serve this entire office **before the Shroud** (the Tomb of God), which is still in its place from Great Friday Vespers.

---

## SERVICE ORDER

---

### I. OPENING RITE

> **Part IV, line 722:** *"From the usual beginning to 'O come, let us worship' we celebrate everything, as usual at the Midnight Office; and immediately Psalm 50 and Canon."*
> **Import chain:** Line 722 → Part I, line 124 (Midnight Office general structure).

> [!NOTE]
> Part I, line 124 describes the Midnight Office as having three forms: Daily, Saturday, and Sunday. The "usual beginning" for the Midnight Office follows the standard opening formula: *"Blessed is our God"* through *"O come, let us worship"* (as described for all services in Part I).

#### Phase A — Priestly Blessing (steps 1–7)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Priest** | **Vests** in epitrachelion and phelonion (provincial) or in all sacred vestments of violet (cathedral) — per line 720. |
| 2 | **Priest** | Stands **before the Shroud** (line 720). |
| 3 | **Priest** | **Exclaims:** *"Blessed is our God, always, now and ever and unto ages of ages."* |
| 4 | **Choir** | *"Amen."* |
| 5 | **Choir** | *"Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee," "O Heavenly King…"* |
| 6 | **Choir** | **Trisagion** through **Our Father.** |
| 7 | **Choir** | *"O come, let us worship"* (×3). |

#### Phase B — Immediately: Psalm 50 (step 8)

> **Line 722:** *"…and immediately Psalm 50 and Canon."*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | **Reader** | **Immediately**: reads **Psalm 50** — *"Have mercy on me, O God…"* (line 722). |

> [!NOTE]
> The usual Midnight Office has additional elements between "O come, let us worship" and the canon (Ps. 118 or the Triadikon Canon, depending on the day — Part I, line 124). Here, line 722 specifies that *"immediately"* after "O come, let us worship" comes Psalm 50, then the Canon — skipping those intermediate elements.

---

### II. TOMB CANON

> **Part IV, line 723:** *"The Tomb Canon of Matins of Great Saturday 'He who in ancient times hid the pursuing tyrant beneath the waves of the sea'; according to the rubric, heirmoi — twice, and troparia — on 12 (today — with heirmos on 4), with the refrain 'Glory to Thee, O Lord, for Thy holy burial,' after which Katavasia and heirmos — both choirs together."*

> **Part IV, line 724:** *"After the 3rd Ode — Sessional Hymn, and after the 6th — Kondakion-Ikos of Great Saturday; after the 9th 'It is truly meet' will not be, but only the heirmos of this ode."*

> [!NOTE]
> This is the **same Canon** that was sung at Tomb Matins earlier that day (Part IV, line 698). Line 723 explicitly identifies it: *"The Tomb Canon of Matins of Great Saturday."* However, line 723 adds a parenthetical note about current practice: *"today — with heirmos on 4,"* meaning the troparia count has been reduced from 12 to accommodate the heirmos being counted within the four.

#### Phase A — Per-Ode Cycle (repeats for each ode)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Choir** | Sings the **heirmos** of the ode — sung **twice** (line 723: *"heirmoi — twice"*). |
| 2 | **Choir** | Sings the **troparia** of the ode — **12 per ode** (line 723: *"troparia — on 12"*). |
| | | Refrain before each troparion: *"Glory to Thee, O Lord, for Thy holy burial"* (line 723). |
| 3 | **Choir** | Before the **penultimate** troparion: *"Glory."* |
| 4 | **Choir** | Before the **last** troparion: *"Both now."* |
| 5 | **Both Choirs** | Sing the **Katavasia** — heirmos of the canon, both choirs together (line 723: *"Katavasia and heirmos — both choirs together"*). |

> [!NOTE]
> **Current practice variant (line 723):** *"Today — with heirmos on 4."* This means that in contemporary practice, each ode has the heirmos counted once, followed by 3 troparia (= 4 total, "with heirmos on 4"), rather than the rubric's full 12 troparia plus the heirmos twice.

#### Phase B — After 3rd Ode: Sessional Hymn (step 6)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | **Choir** | **Sessional Hymn** (line 724: *"After the 3rd Ode — Sessional Hymn"*). |

#### Phase C — After 6th Ode: Kontakion-Ikos (steps 7–8)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | **Choir** | **Kontakion** of Great Saturday (line 724: *"after the 6th — Kondakion-Ikos of Great Saturday"*). |
| 8 | **Choir** | **Ikos** of Great Saturday. |

#### Phase D — After 9th Ode: Suppression (steps 9–10)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | | **"It is truly meet" is NOT recited** (line 724: *"after the 9th 'It is truly meet' will not be"*). |
| 10 | **Both Choirs** | Instead: the **heirmos of the 9th ode** (line 724: *"but only the heirmos of this ode"*). |

### III. TRISAGION + TROPARION

> **Part IV, line 725:** *"Trisagion and the rest with 'Our Father' and Troparion 'When Thou didst descend.'"*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Choir** | **Trisagion** through **Our Father.** |
| 2 | **Priest** | Exclaims: *"For Thine is the kingdom…"* |
| 3 | **Choir** | Troparion **"When Thou didst descend"** (line 725). |

### IV. LITANY + DISMISSAL

> **Part IV, line 726:** *"Litany 'Have mercy on us, O God,' which is at the Midnight Office, and dismissal of Matins of Great Saturday, beginning from 'Glory to Thee, O Christ God.'"*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Priest** (or Deacon) | Litany **"Have mercy on us, O God"** — *"which is at the Midnight Office"* (line 726). |
| 2 | **Choir** | Responds to each petition. |
| 3 | **Priest** | Exclamation. |
| 4 | **Priest** | **Dismissal** — of **Matins of Great Saturday**, beginning from *"Glory to Thee, O Christ God"* (line 726). |

### V. "THE NOBLE JOSEPH" + SHROUD TRANSFER + VESTING CHANGE

> **Part IV, line 727:** *"At the end: 'The noble Joseph' (3) with prostrations, as at Matins of Great Saturday, but the Shroud is kissed only by the sacred ministers, according to rank, and the bishop, taking the Shroud from the Tomb, hands it to four canons and thus all the sacred ministers return to the sanctuary and four canons hang the Shroud behind the holy table over the High Place. Then they depart to the sacristy, take off the violet vestments and vest in all bright sacred vestments of their rank, and at this time in the church all candles are lit."*

This is the climactic moment of the entire Holy Saturday liturgical day — the Shroud is removed from the Tomb, transferred to the sanctuary, and the sacred ministers change from violet to bright vestments for Resurrection Matins.

#### Phase A — "The Noble Joseph" with Prostrations (steps 1–2)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Choir** | Troparion **"The noble Joseph"** — **3 times** with **prostrations** (line 727: *"(3) with prostrations, as at Matins of Great Saturday"*). |
| 2 | **All** | Prostrations done **as at Matins of Great Saturday** — but the **Shroud is kissed only by the sacred ministers, according to rank** (line 727). |

> [!NOTE]
> Unlike at Tomb Matins (line 704) where the people also kiss the Shroud, here **only the sacred ministers** kiss it, according to rank (line 727).

#### Phase B — Shroud Transfer to the Sanctuary (steps 3–5)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Bishop** (or main priest) | **Takes the Shroud** from the Tomb (line 727). |
| 4 | **Bishop** | **Hands** the Shroud **to four canons** (line 727: *"hands it to four canons"*). |
| 5 | **All Sacred Ministers** | **Return to the sanctuary.** The four canons **hang the Shroud behind the Holy Table over the High Place** (line 727). |

#### Phase C — Final Vestment Change: Violet → Bright (steps 6–7)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | **Sacred Ministers** | **Depart** to the sacristy. **Take off the violet vestments.** **Vest in all bright sacred vestments** of their rank (line 727: *"take off the violet vestments and vest in all bright sacred vestments of their rank"*). |
| 7 | | **At this time:** in the church **all candles are lit** (line 727: *"and at this time in the church all candles are lit"*). |

> [!NOTE]
> This is the second vestment change on Holy Saturday. The first was during the Vespers-Liturgy (line 714: violet → bright after the Apostle). The sacred ministers would have returned to violet after the Liturgy for this Midnight Office (line 720 specifies violet). Now they change **permanently** to bright for Resurrection Matins and all of Bright Week.

---

## NOTE ON THE SHROUD

> **Part IV, line 729:** *"Regarding the time and place of the placing of the Shroud we do not find an ancient rubric, for this rite is later and different in different regions. According to the custom of our archcathedral church the Shroud remains hung behind the holy table, over the High Place, from Resurrection Matins to the Leavetaking of the Resurrection inclusive, that is until Wednesday before the Ascension. According to the custom of our other churches, the Shroud, taken from the Tomb before Resurrection Matins, is placed on the holy table, where it remains during the whole Bright Week, and from the Sunday of Ap. Thomas to the Leavetaking of the Resurrection — behind the holy table. According to others — it remains on the altar, the antimension with the Gospel is placed on it and the Liturgy is celebrated on it. However, it is better to place the Shroud behind the holy table, for lying on the altar, it is often significantly damaged by candles, from which wax drips on it."*
