# The Compline Matrices
## Liturgical Digest

> **When served:** Compline (Apodeipnon) concludes the active daily cycle of prayer.
> **Governing logic:** The structural shift between Small Compline and Great Compline is governed entirely by the fasting cycles dictated by Dolnytsky **Part IV (The Triodion & Pentecostarion)** and major Menaion eves.

---

## I. SMALL COMPLINE (The Standard Matrix)

Served every evening of the year with the exception of the weekdays of Great Lent and specific primary feast eves.

| # | Action | Condition / Fork |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **The Enarxis** | *"Blessed is our God..."* |
| **2** | **Psalmody & Doxology** | Psalms 50, 69, 142. The Great Doxology (Read). |
| **3** | **Nicene Creed** | Read. |
| **4** | **[NODE A: The Canon]**| *See Canon Matrix below.* |
| **5** | **[NODE B: Troparia]** | *See Troparia Matrix below.* |
| **6** | **Prayer of the Hours** | *"Thou Who at all times..."* |
| **7** | **Final Prayers** | Prayer to Theotokos, Prayer to Christ. |
| **8** | **Conclusion** | Lesser Dismissal and Rite of Forgiveness. |

### Node A: The Compline Canon
*   **Daily Rule:** Ordinarily, a Canon to the Theotokos (from the Octoechos) is chanted.
*   **Case 13 (Apodosis/Leavetaking):** The service of the Menaion Saint displaced by the Apodosis of a Feast is transferred to Compline and chanted here.

### Node B: The Compline Troparia & Kontakia
*   **Rank 6 (Simple Saint):** The Troparion and Kontakion of the Saint of the day are read.
*   **Rank 1 & 2 (Great / Vigil Feasts):** Everything is suppressed except the **Kontakion of the Feast**.

---

## II. GREAT COMPLINE (The Lenten Matrix)

Served entirely during Great Lent (Mon-Thu nights), the first three days of Holy Week, and the eves of Nativity, Theophany, and Annunciation.

| # | Action | Condition / Fork |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Part One: 6 Psalms** | Psalms 4, 6, 12, 24, 30, 90. |
| **2** | **God is With Us** | The magnificent hymn of Isaiah sung with verses. |
| **3** | **[NODE C: Troparia]** | *See Troparia Matrix below.* |
| **4** | **Part Two: Psalmody**| Psalms 50, 101, Prayer of Manasseh. |
| **5** | **Part Three: Psalmody**| Psalms 69, 142, Great Doxology. |
| **6** | **[NODE D: Canon]** | *See Canon Matrix below.* |
| **7** | **Lord of the Powers** | *"O Lord of the Powers, be with us..."* |
| **8** | **Conclusion** | St. Ephrem (If Lenten), Final Prayers, Dismissal. |

### Node C: Great Compline Troparia
*   **Lenten Matrix:** If served during Great Lent, the penitential troparia are sung (*"O invisible Lord... Have mercy on us O Lord..."*).
*   **Festal Matrix (Eves of Nativity/Theophany):** The Lenten troparia are suppressed, and the **Troparion of the Feast** is sung with great solemnity.

### Node D: The Lenten Canons
*   **Week 1 of Great Lent:** The immense **Great Canon of St. Andrew of Crete** is divided into four parts and chanted on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday of the first week at this exact node. Full earth prostrations accompany every troparion of the canon.
*   **Week 5 of Great Lent (Thursday):** The entire Great Canon is chanted in full on this evening (historically called the Matins of Thursday).
