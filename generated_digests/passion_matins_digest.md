# Great Friday Passion Matins
## The Service of the Holy and Saving Passion of Our Lord Jesus Christ
*(Strasti — Служба Страстей / Ukrainian: Страсті Господні)*

---

> **When served:** Thursday evening — *"We sing this Matins after sunset; according to the rubric — at the 2nd hour of the night"* (Part IV, line 631).

> **Governing source:** Dolnytsky Part IV, lines 626–653.

> **General structure (Part IV, line 634):** *"From the usual beginning to the end of the troparion after 'Alleluia' and from the Canon to the end of Matins everything — according to the rubric of Matins of Great Thursday, here on pp. 383–384. Between the troparion and the canon there will be five Sessional Hymns, each of which has before it three antiphons of the Passion troparia, without Kathismata and small litanies, then the Beatitudes, Prokeimenon and Psalm 50. Besides this we read 12 Passion Gospels."*

---

## PHYSICAL PREPARATION

> **Part IV, line 632:** *"Before the holy doors is placed an analogion with two candlesticks, facing the people, and on it — the Gospel."*

The Gospel Book remains on the analogion throughout the entire service — all 12 Gospels are read from this same position before the Holy Doors.

---

## VESTMENTS

**Violet** throughout (Passion Week — Part IV, line 556: *"sacred vestments of these days and of the whole Passion Week… should be of violet color"*).

The priest vests in a **violet phelonion** before each Gospel reading and removes it between readings (Part IV, line 639).

## SERVICE ORDER

---

### I. OPENING RITE — PREPARATORY BLOCK

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 636):** *"From the usual beginning to the end of the litany 'In peace let us pray to the Lord' everything — as at Matins daily."*
> **Import chain:** Line 636 → Part IV, line 204 (*"everything, just as on every day"*) → Part I, line 143.

This opening is identical to the Bridegroom Matins opening and to the standard weekday opening. Every step below is derived from Part I, line 143 unless otherwise noted.

#### Phase A — Priestly Blessing (steps 1–8)

The priest is in the sanctuary. He has not yet vested in the phelonion.

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Priest** | **Vests in epitrachelion** — blesses and kisses it at the top where it has a cross. |
| 2 | **Priest** | **Exits** through the **northern door** to the front of the **closed** Holy Doors. |
| 3 | **Priest** | **Bows low.** Exclaims: **"Blessed is our God, always, now and ever and unto ages of ages."** |
| 4 | **Choir** | *"Amen."* |
| 5 | **Choir** | *"Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee."* |
| 6 | **Choir** | *"O Heavenly King…"* |
| 7 | **Choir** | **Trisagion** through **Our Father.** *(First Trisagion-Our Father cycle.)* |
| 8 | **Priest** | Exclaims: **"For Thine is the kingdom…"** *(First exclamation.)* |

#### Phase B — Choir Response (steps 9–11)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | **Choir** | *"Amen."* |
| 10 | **Choir** | *"Lord, have mercy"* (×12). |
| 11 | **Choir** | *"O come, let us worship"* (×3). |

#### Phase C — Censing + Two Psalms (steps 12–18)

The priest enters the sanctuary and censes. The Two Psalms are read **simultaneously** with the censing (Part I, line 143).

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | **Priest** | **Bows low.** Enters through the **southern door** into the sanctuary. |
| 13 | **Priest** | Stands before the **steps of the Holy Table.** **Bows low** to the Holy Mysteries. |
| 14 | **Priest** | **Receives the censer.** Puts in incense. **Blesses it** with a short prayer. |
| 15 | **Priest** | **Censes around the Holy Table.** |
| 16 | **Priest** | **Exits** through the **northern door.** **Bows low.** |
| 17 | **Priest** | **Censes** the iconostasis, choirs, and people, **according to custom.** |
| 18 | **Choirs** | *(Simultaneously with steps 15–17)* Recite the **Two Psalms** (Pss. 19 and 20). |

#### Phase D — Troparia, Second Trisagion, Second Exclamation (steps 19–23)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 19 | **Choir** | **Troparia** *(after the Two Psalms — not named in the primary source).* |
| 20 | **Choir** | **Trisagion** through **Our Father.** *(Second Trisagion-Our Father cycle.)* |
| 21 | **Priest** | **Returns** to stand before the Holy Doors. **Bows low.** |
| 22 | **Priest** | **Censes toward the Holy Table** *(from outside the Holy Doors).* |
| 23 | **Priest** | Exclaims: **"For Thine is the kingdom."** *(Second exclamation.)* |

#### Phase E — Priest Re-enters, Litany for Sovereign (steps 24–26)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 24 | **Priest** | Enters through the **southern door** into the sanctuary. **Puts away the censer.** |
| 25 | **Priest** | **Exits** through the **northern door** to the front of the iconostasis. **Bows low.** |
| 26 | **Priest** | Upon conclusion of the troparia, recites the **litany for the Sovereign.** After its exclamation → proceeds to Six Psalms. |

### IV. TROPARION — "WHEN THE GLORIOUS DISCIPLES"

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 638):** *"Troparion 'When the glorious disciples' twice, Glory, Both now: once more the same."*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Choir** | Troparion **"When the glorious disciples"** — **first time** (line 638). |

#### Phase B — Vesting and Censing During Third Singing (steps 4–17)

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 639) — full text:** *"When the troparion is sung the third time, the priest vests in a violet phelonion, goes before the steps of the altar, bows to the Holy Mysteries and, having opened the holy doors, recites the prayer of the censer, after which, having blessed the censer: 'Blessed is our God', censes around the holy table, then, having stood before the steps of the altar and bowed low, goes out through the holy doors before the iconostasis and censes the local icons, the choirs and the people and, having returned through the holy doors to the sanctuary, gives up the censer and, having again bowed low, sings the small litany without exclamation…"*

All of the following actions take place **during and after the third singing** of the troparion:

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | **Priest** | **Vests in a violet phelonion** (line 639: *"the priest vests in a violet phelonion"*). |
| 5 | **Priest** | **Goes** before the **steps of the altar** (Holy Table). |
| 6 | **Priest** | **Bows** to the Holy Mysteries. |
| 7 | **Priest** | **Opens the Holy Doors.** |
| 8 | **Priest** | **Recites the prayer of the censer.** |
| 9 | **Priest** | **Blesses the censer:** *"Blessed is our God"* (line 639). |
| 10 | **Priest** | **Censes around the Holy Table.** |
| 11 | **Priest** | Stands before the **steps of the altar.** **Bows low.** |
| 12 | **Priest** | **Goes out** through the **Holy Doors** before the iconostasis. |
| 13 | **Priest** | **Censes** the local icons, the choirs, and the people. |
| 14 | **Priest** | **Returns** through the **Holy Doors** to the sanctuary. |
| 15 | **Priest** | **Puts away the censer.** |
| 16 | **Priest** | **Bows low** again. |

> **Full censing only at 1st Gospel (Part IV, line 640):** *"Everything should be censed only twice, that is at the 1st Gospel and at the Canon, at the heirmos of the 9th Ode."* At subsequent Gospels the censing is **only around the Holy Table** (line 640: *"only the holy table"*).

> **Part IV, line 634:** *"After the singing of each Gospel we ring all the church bells."*
>
> However, Dolnytsky himself recommends clappers: *"it is better already at Passion Matins not to ring, but to strike clappers or boards, for Passion Matins is a time of sorrow, to which the joyful sounds of bells do not correspond"* (line 634).

### ✠ GOSPEL CEREMONY — SUBSEQUENT GOSPELS (2nd–12th)

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 640):** *"All this — exactly so at the readings of the other Gospels, with the exception of the litany and censing."*

At each subsequent Gospel the priest repeats the same ceremony (vest → cense → exclamations → read → kiss → return → remove phelonion) with these modifications:

| Element | 1st Gospel | Sessional Hymn Gospels (2–6) | Other Gospels (7–12) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Small Litany** | ✅ (line 639) | ✅ — at the Sessional Hymns preceding each Gospel (line 640) | ❌ (line 640) |
| **Censing scope** | Full: Holy Table + iconostasis + choirs + people (line 639) | Holy Table only (line 640: *"the censing — only of the holy table"*) | Holy Table only (line 640) |
| **Pre-Gospel:** *"Glory to Thy Passion"* | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Post-Gospel:** *"Glory to Thy long-suffering"* | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Phelonion vest/remove** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Bells / clappers** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |

> [!NOTE]
> **Dolnytsky's own recommendation on censing (line 640):** *"We consider that it is fitting to cense not only at the Sessional Hymns, but also at all other Gospels, for the censing takes place not for the sake of the Sessional Hymns, but for the sake of the Gospels, however, only the holy table (around)… Everything should be censed only twice, that is at the 1st Gospel and at the Canon, at the heirmos of the 9th Ode."*
>
> This means: **full censing** (entire church) only at the 1st Gospel and at the Canon (9th Ode). **Holy Table only** at all other Gospels.

### V. FIVE SESSIONAL HYMNS WITH ANTIPHONS — GOSPELS 2–6

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 634):** *"Between the troparion and the canon there will be five Sessional Hymns, each of which has before it three antiphons of the Passion troparia, without Kathismata and small litanies."*
> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 644):** *"After the singing of the first Gospel follow five Sessional Hymns, each of which has before it three antiphons of the Passion troparia, and after itself — one Passion Gospel. The number of troparia in all antiphons is not the same and therefore, according to the rubric, each must be repeated, except the Theotokion, with which each antiphon concludes, with the refrain 'Glory, Both now.'"*

Each of the five Sessional Hymn blocks follows this pattern:

#### Per-Block Cycle (repeats 5 times)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Choir** | **Antiphon 1** — Passion troparia. Each troparion is **repeated** (line 644: *"each must be repeated"*). The antiphon concludes with a **Theotokion** which is NOT repeated, but sung with the refrain *"Glory, Both now"* (line 644). |
| 2 | **Choir** | **Antiphon 2** — same pattern. |
| 3 | **Choir** | **Antiphon 3** — same pattern. |
| 4 | **Choir** | **Sessional Hymn** of the Triodion for that block. |
| 5 | **Priest** | Censes (Holy Table only — line 640), vests, exclaims, reads Gospel, returns, removes phelonion — per the Gospel ceremony above. |

> [!NOTE]
> **Line 640:** *"We do not sit at these Sessional Hymns, since the priest censes the holy table, but we sing them standing."*

#### The Five Blocks

| Block | Antiphons | Gospel |
|---|---|---|
| **Sessional Hymn I** | Antiphons 1–3 | ✠ **2nd Gospel** *[from Gospel book: Matt., Section 98]* |
| **Sessional Hymn II** | Antiphons 4–6 | ✠ **3rd Gospel** *[from Gospel book: Matt., Sections 109–114]* |
| **Sessional Hymn III** | Antiphons 7–9 | ✠ **4th Gospel** *[from Gospel book: John, Sections 40–42]* |
| **Sessional Hymn IV** | Antiphons 10–12 | ✠ **5th Gospel** *[from Gospel book: Matt., Sections 110–112]* |
| **Sessional Hymn V** | Antiphons 13–15 | ✠ **6th Gospel** *[from Gospel book: Mark, Sections 67–68]* |

### VIII. CANON — TRIODION (Three-Ode)

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 647):** *"Canon — triodion, heirmoi — twice, troparia — on 12, Katavasia after each ode — its heirmos, both choirs together. After the 5th Ode — small litany and Kondakion-Ikos; at the 9th Ode we do not sing 'More honorable', but only the heirmos 'More honorable'; after the 9th we do not sing 'It is truly meet', but we sing the Katavasia; then — small litany and three times the Exaposteilarion with the refrain 'Glory, Both now', as usual, then — the ninth Gospel."*

#### Phase A — Per-Ode Cycle (repeats for each ode)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Choir** | Sings the **heirmos** of the ode — sung **twice** (line 647: *"heirmoi — twice"*). |
| 2 | **Choir** | Sings the **troparia** of the ode — **12 per ode** (line 647: *"troparia — on 12"*). |
| | | Refrain before each troparion: *"Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee"* (cf. Bridegroom Matins line 579). |
| 3 | **Choir** | Before the **penultimate** troparion: *"Glory."* |
| 4 | **Choir** | Before the **last** troparion: *"Both now."* |
| 5 | **Both Choirs** | Sing the **Katavasia** — heirmos of the canon, **once**, both choirs together (line 647: *"Katavasia after each ode — its heirmos, both choirs together"*). |

> [!NOTE]
> **Katavasia at every ode:** Unlike Bridegroom Matins (which follows the general Lenten rule of Katavasia only at Odes 3, 6, 8, 9), here the Katavasia is sung **after each ode** (line 647: *"Katavasia after each ode"*).

#### Phase B — After 5th Ode: Kondakion-Ikos (steps 6–10)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | **Priest** | Sings the **Small Litany** (line 647: *"After the 5th Ode — small litany"*). |
| 7 | **Choir** | Responds to each petition. |
| 8 | **Priest** | Exclamation. |
| 9 | **Choir** | **Kondakion** of Great Friday. |
| 10 | **Choir** | **Ikos** of Great Friday. |

> [!NOTE]
> **Kondakion after Ode 5:** Unlike Bridegroom Matins (where the Kondakion placement is ambiguous), here line 647 explicitly says *"After the 5th Ode — small litany and Kondakion-Ikos."*

#### Phase C — Ode 8: Special Refrains *(general canon rule, Part I, line 174)*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | **Choir** | Before the **penultimate** troparion of Ode 8, instead of *"Glory"*: **"Let us bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, the Lord."** |
| 12 | **Choir** | As refrain to the **Katavasia** of Ode 8: **"We praise, we bless, we worship the Lord, praising and magnifying Him unto all ages."** |

#### Phase D — Ode 9: Suppressions and Heirmos (per line 647)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | | **"More honorable" is NOT sung** in its usual form (line 647: *"at the 9th Ode we do not sing 'More honorable'"*). |
| 14 | **Choir** | Instead: the **heirmos "More honorable"** is sung (line 647: *"but only the heirmos 'More honorable'"*). |
| 15 | **Choir** | Sings the heirmos of Ode 9 **twice** + 12 troparia with refrains (same as other odes). |
| 16 | **Both Choirs** | Sing the **Katavasia** of Ode 9. |
| 17 | | **"It is truly meet" is NOT said** (line 647: *"after the 9th we do not sing 'It is truly meet'"*). |

> [!NOTE]
> **Heirmos "More honorable":** Line 647 distinguishes between the usual *"More honorable than the Cherubim"* (the Song of the Theotokos) and *"the heirmos 'More honorable.'"* The usual Song is suppressed; the heirmos of the canon that begins with these words is sung instead.

#### Phase E — Censing at Ode 9

> **Part IV, line 640:** *"Everything should be censed only twice, that is at the 1st Gospel and at the Canon, at the heirmos of the 9th Ode."*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 18 | **Priest** | **Full censing** at the heirmos of the 9th Ode — the Holy Table, iconostasis, choirs, and people (line 640: *"Everything should be censed… at the Canon, at the heirmos of the 9th Ode"*). |

### XII. LITANY OF COMPLETION — ✠ 11th GOSPEL

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 650):** *"Litany 'Let us complete' and, after the exclamation 'For Thou art a merciful God,' immediately — the eleventh Gospel."*

## BELL AND CLAPPER RUBRIC

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 634):** *"After the singing of each Gospel we ring all the church bells and, after the twelfth Gospel, we do not ring until the Resurrection itself, but instead of bells we use wooden clappers."*

| Event | Instruction |
|---|---|
| After each Gospel (1st–12th) | Ring all church bells (line 634) |
| After the 12th Gospel | Bells **cease permanently** until the Resurrection (line 634) |
| From the 12th Gospel onward | **Wooden clappers** replace bells (line 634) |

> **Dolnytsky's recommendation (line 634):** *"But it is better already at Passion Matins not to ring, but to strike clappers or boards, for Passion Matins is a time of sorrow, to which the joyful sounds of bells do not correspond. Also in Rome already at Passion Matins clappers are used."*

## DEACON RUBRIC

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 642) — full text:** *"If there be deacons, they remain from the beginning of Matins, dressed in dalmatic with orarion of violet color, in the sanctuary, at their places, until they begin to sing the third time the troparion 'When the glorious disciples'…"*

This is the most detailed deacon rubric in the entire Typikon. It governs two deacons acting throughout the service.

#### Phase A — Deacons' Initial Position (steps 1–2)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Both Deacons** | Remain from the beginning of Matins, **dressed in dalmatic with orarion of violet color**, in the sanctuary, at their places (line 642). |
| 2 | | They remain seated **until the third singing** of the troparion *"When the glorious disciples."* |

#### Phase B — At the 1st Gospel: Censing (steps 3–11)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Both Deacons** | Come out **together with the priest** (who is vested in a violet phelonion) before the **steps of the altar** (line 642). |
| 4 | **All three** | **Bow together** to the Holy Mysteries. |
| 5 | **Deacons** | **Open the Holy Doors.** |
| 6 | **Deacons** | **Cense everything as usual, as at the beginning of the Liturgy** (line 642). |
| 7 | **Deacons** | **Return** before the steps of the altar. |
| 8 | **Deacons** | **Give up the censers.** |
| 9 | **All three** | **Bow together** to the Holy Mysteries. |
| 10 | **Both Deacons** | Go out, **each through his door**, before the Holy Doors. |
| 11 | **Both Deacons** | **Bow** to the Holy Mysteries again (line 642). |

#### Phase C — At the 1st Gospel: Small Litany (steps 12–14)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | **Second Deacon** | Sings the **Small Litany** — after the singing of the troparion (line 642: *"the second deacon… sings the small litany"*). |
| 13 | **Both Deacons** | **Bow** to the Holy Mysteries. |
| 14 | **Both Deacons** | **Exchange places** and proceed to the analogion so that when the priest approaches, the **first deacon is on the right** and the **second on the left** (line 642). |

#### Phase D — At the 1st Gospel: Exclamations (steps 15–20)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 15 | **Priest** | **Bows** to the Holy Mysteries. Comes out through the **Holy Doors** to the analogion. |
| 16 | **First Deacon** | Exclaims: **"And that we may be accounted worthy."** |
| 17 | **Priest** | *"Peace be to all."* |
| 18 | **Second Deacon** | *"Wisdom. Aright. Let us listen."* |
| 19 | **Priest** | *"The Reading from [Name]."* |
| 20 | **First Deacon** | *"Let us attend."* |

#### Phase E — At the 1st Gospel: After the Reading (steps 21–27)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 21 | **Priest** | Reads the Gospel. |
| 22 | **Priest** | **Kisses the beginning of the reading** first. |
| 23 | **Deacon** (right) | **Kisses** the reading after the priest. |
| 24 | **Deacon** (left) | **Kisses** the reading after the right deacon (line 642). |
| 25 | **Priest** | Returns through the **central** (Holy) Doors. |
| 26 | **Deacons** | Return through the **side doors.** |
| 27 | **All three** | Stand before the steps of the altar. **Close the Holy Doors.** **Bow** to the Holy Mysteries. Go to their places. |

#### Phase F — At Subsequent Gospels (line 642)

> *"At the other Gospels, since there will be no small litany, the deacons, having come out before the holy doors, bow and, having exchanged places, proceed to the analogion and, when the priest stands between them, exclaim: 'And that we may be accounted worthy' and the rest, as above."*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 28 | **Both Deacons** | Come out before the Holy Doors. **Bow.** |
| 29 | **Both Deacons** | **Exchange places.** Proceed to the analogion. |
| 30 | | Exclamations and reading proceed as above (steps 16–27), **without the small litany and initial censing.** |

## SUMMARY: THE 12 GOSPELS AND THEIR POSITIONS

> [!NOTE]
> **Dolnytsky does not provide chapter/verse references** for the 12 Gospels. He identifies them by Section number or by their position in the service. The Gospel-book references below are marked as supplementary.

| Gospel | Position in Service | Section Reference from Dolnytsky |
|---|---|---|
| **1st** | After troparion *"When the glorious disciples"* | Line 639: *"reads the first Gospel"* |
| **2nd** | After Antiphons 1–3 + Sessional Hymn I | Line 644: *"after itself — one Passion Gospel"* |
| **3rd** | After Antiphons 4–6 + Sessional Hymn II | Line 644 |
| **4th** | After Antiphons 7–9 + Sessional Hymn III | Line 644 |
| **5th** | After Antiphons 10–12 + Sessional Hymn IV | Line 644 |
| **6th** | After Antiphons 13–15 + Sessional Hymn V | Line 644 |
| **7th** | After Beatitudes + Prokeimenon | Line 645: *"the 6th Gospel at the end"* |
| **8th** | After Psalm 50 | Line 646: *"Psalm 50 and the eighth Gospel"* |
| **9th** | After Exaposteilarion | Line 647: *"then — the ninth Gospel"* |
| **10th** | After Stichera of Praises | Line 648: *"then — the tenth Gospel"* |
| **11th** | After Litany of Completion | Line 650: *"immediately — the eleventh Gospel"* |
| **12th** | After Aposticha | Line 651: *"the twelfth Gospel"* |

> [!NOTE]
> **Line 645 numbering:** Line 645 says *"the 6th Gospel at the end"* of the Beatitudes section, but the Gospel at this position is actually the **7th** in the overall sequence (the "6th" would refer to the Gospel after Sessional Hymn V). The counting in line 645 appears to be an error in Dolnytsky's numbering or refers to a different counting method. The overall sequence of 12 is confirmed by line 634.
