# Great Saturday Small Compline
## With the Canon on the Burial of Christ
*(Ukrainian: Мале Повечір'я Великої Суботи)*

---

> **When served:** After the Blessing of Loaves at the Vespers-Liturgy of St. Basil (Part IV, line 716), before the Midnight Office.

> **Governing source:** Dolnytsky Part IV, lines 716–718.

> **Liturgical context (Part IV, line 717):** *"In Greek and Slavonic Triodia, except ours, Compline is not given, but from the Blessing of Loaves to the Tomb Order, which is sung immediately before Resurrection Matins, the reading of the whole book of the 'Acts of the Apostles' is prescribed, although among us today these 'Acts' are sung perhaps only in cathedral and seminary temples; and they are sung by verses by clerics two by two, alternately."*

---

## NOTES

> **Part IV, line 718:** *"This Compline should be Small. Finally, even our Triodion leaves the choice to read it or not, for it is written about it: 'if there be.'"*

This Compline is **optional** — the Triodion says *"if there be"* (line 718). Nevertheless, when celebrated, it is **Small Compline** (line 718), following the general structure from Part I, lines 96–112.

> [!NOTE]
> In the Greek and Slavonic traditions (other than the Ukrainian/Ruthenian), this Compline is not given at all; instead, the Acts of the Apostles are read from the Blessing of Loaves until the start of the "Tomb Order" (Midnight Office) that precedes Resurrection Matins (line 717). In cathedral and seminary churches, this practice may still be observed.

---

## SERVICE ORDER

> **Import chain:** Part IV, line 718 → Part I, lines 96–112 (Small Compline structure), with modifications.

---

### I. OPENING RITE

> **Part I, line 98:** *"The beginning is usual, that is 'Blessed is our God' etc., 'Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee' and all other introductory prayers up to 'Come, let us worship' (3) inclusive."*

#### Phase A — Priestly Blessing (steps 1–7)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Priest** | **Vests in epitrachelion** — blesses and kisses the cross upon it (Part I, line 111). |
| 2 | **Priest** | **Exits** to the front of the **closed Holy Doors** (Part I, line 111: *"celebrated… with closed Holy Doors"*). |
| 3 | **Priest** | **Exclaims:** *"Blessed is our God, always, now and ever and unto ages of ages."* |
| 4 | **Choir** | *"Amen."* |
| 5 | **Choir** | *"Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee," "O Heavenly King…"* |
| 6 | **Choir** | **Trisagion** through **Our Father.** |
| 7 | **Choir** | *"O come, let us worship"* (×3). |

#### Phase B — Priest Withdraws (step 8)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | **Priest** | Remains before the Holy Doors until *"O come, let us worship"* is said, then **withdraws** (Part I, line 111: *"comes out to the front of the Holy Doors at the beginning and remains there until 'Come, let us worship'"*). |

---

### II. THREE PSALMS, DOXOLOGY, CREED

> **Part I, line 99:** *"The Three Psalms, Small Doxology and Symbol of Faith just as in the Horologion."*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Reader** | Reads the **Three Psalms** (Pss. 50, 69, 142 — as in the Horologion). |
| 2 | **Reader** | Reads the **Small Doxology** (*"Glory to God in the highest"*). |
| 3 | **Reader** | Reads the **Symbol of Faith** (*"I believe in one God…"*). |

---

### III. CANON ON THE BURIAL OF CHRIST

> **Part IV, line 718:** *"In any case, in our Triodion is given the Canon on the Burial of Christ and on the Lamentation of the Most Holy Theotokos."*

This Canon replaces the usual Canon to the Theotokos of the Octoechos (Part I, line 100).

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Choir** | Sings the **Canon on the Burial of Christ and on the Lamentation of the Most Holy Theotokos** — from the Triodion (Part IV, line 718). |

> [!NOTE]
> Part I, line 100 specifies the usual Small Compline canon is without Katavasiae and without litanies, with only "Lord, have mercy" (3), Glory, Both now: Sessional Hymn after the 6th Ode. This general structure applies unless the specific canon dictates otherwise.

---

### IV. AFTER THE 9th ODE + TRISAGION + KONTAKION

> **Part IV, line 718:** *"After the 9th Ode, instead of 'It is truly meet,' will be the heirmos of this ode and, after the Trisagion, — Kondakion of Great Saturday."*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | | After the 9th Ode: **"It is truly meet" is NOT recited** — instead, the **heirmos of the 9th ode** (Part IV, line 718). |
| 2 | **Choir** | **Trisagion** through **Our Father** and *"For Thine is the kingdom"* (Part I, line 101). |
| 3 | **Choir** | **Kontakion of Great Saturday** (Part IV, line 718: *"after the Trisagion, — Kondakion of Great Saturday"*). |

> [!NOTE]
> This replaces the usual troparia at Small Compline (Part I, lines 102–107), which vary by day and occasion. Here, the single Kontakion of Great Saturday takes their place.

---

### V. CONCLUSION

> **Part I, line 108:** *"After the troparia — 'Lord, have mercy' (40) and everything else, as given in the Horologion."*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Choir** | *"Lord, have mercy"* (×40) and the rest as in the Horologion (Part I, line 108). |
| 2 | **Priest** | **Exits** to the front of the Holy Doors at *"Thou Who at all times"* and remains there until the end (Part I, line 111). |
| 3 | **Priest** | **Dismissal** — turning to the people (Part I, line 112). |
