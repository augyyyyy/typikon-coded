# Bridegroom Matins
## Holy Monday, Tuesday & Wednesday of Passion Week

> **When served:** These Matins follow the standard Lenten daily cycle: Vespers and Compline are served the preceding evening; Matins is served the following morning, separately from Vespers (Part I, line 143; Part IV, line 557: *"Lenten, as in the Forty Days"*).

> **Governing source:** Dolnytsky Part IV, lines 554–580: *"All these three days have one form of divine service, that is Lenten, as in the Forty Days, only with certain exceptions."*

---

## COMMON RUBRICS FOR ALL THREE DAYS

### Vestments
**Violet** throughout all of Passion Week, beginning from Palm Sunday evening.

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 556):** *"The sacred vestments of these days and of the whole Passion Week, beginning from Flowery Sunday evening, should be of **violet color**."*

### Form
**Lenten Matins** — the standard Lenten weekday form inherited from the Forty Days (Part IV, line 557). The defining markers are:
- **Alleluia** replaces *"God is the Lord"* (Part IV, line 205)
- **Small Doxology** read (Part IV, line 580: *"Small Doxology"*; Part I, line 204)
- **Prostrations** with the Prayer of St. Ephrem at the conclusion (Part IV, line 234)

The Passion Week exceptions to the standard Lenten pattern (Part IV, lines 577–580):
1. Troparion *"Behold the Bridegroom"* replaces the Trinity Hymns (line 577)
2. Three kathismata (line 578: *"Three regular Kathismata"*)
3. A Matins Gospel is added (line 578: *"Gospel of Matins"*)
4. Exaposteilarion *"Thy Bridal Chamber"* (line 579)

### I. OPENING RITE — PREPARATORY BLOCK

> **Primary Source (Part I, line 143):** *"If there is no Vigil, then Matins is sung in the morning, separately from Vespers, in the following manner..."*
> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 204):** *"From the beginning to the end of the litany 'In peace let us pray to the Lord,' everything, just as on every day."*

This entire block is identical to the standard weekday opening and has no Passion Week modifications (Part IV, line 204). Every step below is derived from Part I, line 143 unless otherwise noted.

#### Phase A — Priestly Blessing (steps 1–8)

The priest is in the sanctuary. He has not yet vested in the phelonion.

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Priest** | **Vests in epitrachelion** — blesses and kisses it at the top where it has a cross. |
| 2 | **Priest** | **Exits** through the **northern door** to the front of the **closed** Holy Doors. |
| 3 | **Priest** | **Bows low.** Exclaims: **"Blessed is our God, always, now and ever and unto ages of ages."** |
| 4 | **Choir** | *"Amen."* |
| 5 | **Choir** | *"Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee."* |
| 6 | **Choir** | *"O Heavenly King…"* |
| 7 | **Choir** | **Trisagion** through **Our Father.** *(First Trisagion-Our Father cycle.)* |
| 8 | **Priest** | Exclaims: **"For Thine is the kingdom…"** *(First exclamation.)* |

#### Phase B — Choir Response (steps 9–11)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | **Choir** | *"Amen."* |
| 10 | **Choir** | *"Lord, have mercy"* (×12). |
| 11 | **Choir** | *"O come, let us worship"* (×3). |

#### Phase C — Censing + Two Psalms (steps 12–18)

The priest enters the sanctuary and censes. The Two Psalms are read **simultaneously** with the censing — the choirs recite them while the priest is censing (line 143).

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | **Priest** | **Bows low.** Enters through the **southern door** into the sanctuary. |
| 13 | **Priest** | Stands before the **steps of the Holy Table.** **Bows low** to the Holy Mysteries. |
| 14 | **Priest** | **Receives the censer.** Puts in incense. **Blesses it** with a short prayer. |
| 15 | **Priest** | **Censes around the Holy Table.** |
| 16 | **Priest** | **Exits** through the **northern door.** **Bows low.** |
| 17 | **Priest** | **Censes** the iconostasis, choirs, and people, **according to custom.** |
| 18 | **Choirs** | *(Simultaneously with steps 15–17)* Recite the **Two Psalms** (Pss. 19 and 20). |

#### Phase D — Troparia, Second Trisagion, Second Exclamation (steps 19–23)

After the Two Psalms, the troparia are sung. Line 143 does not name these troparia — it references only *"the troparia."*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 19 | **Choir** | **Troparia** *(after the Two Psalms — not named in the primary source).* |
| 20 | **Choir** | **Trisagion** through **Our Father.** *(Second Trisagion-Our Father cycle.)* |
| 21 | **Priest** | **Returns** to stand before the Holy Doors. **Bows low.** |
| 22 | **Priest** | **Censes toward the Holy Table** *(from outside the Holy Doors).* |
| 23 | **Priest** | Exclaims: **"For Thine is the kingdom."** *(Second exclamation.)* |

#### Phase E — Priest Re-enters, Litany for Sovereign (steps 24–26)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 24 | **Priest** | Enters through the **southern door** into the sanctuary. **Puts away the censer.** |
| 25 | **Priest** | **Exits** through the **northern door** to the front of the iconostasis. **Bows low.** |
| 26 | **Priest** | Upon conclusion of the troparia, recites the **litany for the Sovereign.** After its exclamation → proceeds to Six Psalms. |

### Ia. BEGINNING OF MATINS PROPER — SIX PSALMS

> **Primary Source (Part I, line 144):** *"'Glory to the Holy,' etc., as at Matins with Vigil, reciting also the other prayers before the Holy Doors."*
> **Primary Source (Part I, line 142):** *"'Glory to God in the highest' (3) with small bows, and 'O Lord, open Thou my lips' (2) without bows, and also the Six Psalms."*

After the litany for the Sovereign, the priest exclaims *"Glory to the Holy"* — this marks the liturgical boundary where Matins proper begins.

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Priest** | Exclaims: **"Glory to the Holy, Consubstantial, Life-giving and Undivided Trinity, always, now and ever and unto ages of ages."** |
| 2 | **Choir** | *"Amen."* |
| 3 | **Choir** | *"Glory to God in the highest"* (×3) — **with small bows** (line 142). |
| 4 | **Choir** | *"O Lord, open Thou my lips"* (×2) — **without bows** (line 142). |
| 5 | **Reader** | Reads the **Six Psalms** (Pss. 3, 37, 62, 87, 102, 142). |
| 6 | **Priest** | *(During the Six Psalms)* Recites the **Morning Prayers** standing before the Holy Doors (line 144). |

> [!NOTE]
> **Phelonion at Six Psalms:** At the Vigil form (line 142), the priest removes the phelonion before reading the Morning Prayers. At the non-Vigil form (line 143), the priest never vested in the phelonion at the opening — he is in epitrachelion only throughout the preparatory block. He does not vest in the phelonion until the Gospel rite (Part I, line 160).

### III. ALLELUIA AND THE BRIDEGROOM TROPARION

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 205):** *"Instead of 'God is the Lord' we sing Alleluia in the sequential tone of the Octoechos."*
> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 577):** *"At Alleluia, instead of the Trinity Hymns, we sing the Troparion 'Behold the Bridegroom' twice, Glory, Both now: the same."*

On ordinary Lenten weekdays, Alleluia is followed by three Trinity Hymns of the current tone with three commemorations (Part IV, line 206). Here the Trinity Hymns are **replaced entirely** by the Bridegroom Troparion (Part IV, line 577).

#### Phase A — Alleluia (steps 1–2)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Priest** | Sings the verses at Alleluia *(same role as at "God is the Lord" — Part I, line 145).* |
| 2 | **Choir** | Sings **Alleluia** in the sequential tone of the Octoechos (line 205). |

#### Phase B — Bridegroom Troparion (steps 3–5)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Choir** | Troparion **"Behold the Bridegroom"** — **first time** (line 577). |
| 4 | **Choir** | Same troparion — **second time** (line 577: *"twice"*). |
| 5 | **Choir** | *"Glory… Both now…"* — same troparion **a third time** (line 577: *"Glory, Both now: the same"*). |

#### Phase C — Priest Withdraws (step 6)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | **Priest** | **Bows.** Withdraws through the **southern door** to his place in the sanctuary (Part I, line 145: *"having bowed, he withdraws through the southern door to his place in the sanctuary"*). |

> [!NOTE]
> The tone of Alleluia is **not specified** in line 577 for Passion Week. The general Lenten rule (line 205) prescribes *"the sequential tone of the Octoechos."* Great Thursday (line 598) explicitly specifies Tone 8 for its own service, but that specification should not be imported here.

### IV. THREE REGULAR KATHISMATA

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 578):** *"Three regular Kathismata with their Sessional Hymns."*
> **Prostrations (Part IV, line 202):** *"At 'Alleluia' of each 'Glory' of the Kathismata — 3 prostrations."*
> **Sessional Hymn sources (Part IV, line 209):** *"after the 1st Kathisma — of the Octoechos… after the 2nd and 3rd — of the Triodion."*

On ordinary Lenten weekdays there are two kathismata (Part IV, line 209). Here **three** kathismata are read (Part IV, line 578).

#### Phase A — Kathisma 1 + Sessional Hymn (steps 1–4)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Reader** | Reads **Kathisma 1** (appointed division of the Psalter). |
| 2 | **All** | At the *"Alleluia"* of each *"Glory"* division within the kathisma: **3 prostrations** (line 202). |
| 3 | | *(Kathisma 1 has three "Glory" divisions, so 3 × 3 = 9 prostrations total.)* |
| 4 | **Choir** | **Sessional Hymn 1** — from the Octoechos of the current tone (line 209: *"after the 1st Kathisma — of the Octoechos"*). |

#### Phase B — Kathisma 2 + Sessional Hymn (steps 5–7)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | **Reader** | Reads **Kathisma 2** (appointed division). |
| 6 | **All** | **3 prostrations** at each *"Glory"* division (line 202). |
| 7 | **Choir** | **Sessional Hymn 2** — from the Triodion (line 209: *"after the 2nd and 3rd — of the Triodion"*). |

#### Phase C — Kathisma 3 + Sessional Hymn (steps 8–10)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | **Reader** | Reads **Kathisma 3** (appointed division). |
| 9 | **All** | **3 prostrations** at each *"Glory"* division (line 202). |
| 10 | **Choir** | **Sessional Hymn 3** — from the Triodion (line 209). |

> [!NOTE]
> Line 578 says only *"with their Sessional Hymns"* without specifying sources. The assignment of Sessional Hymn 1 to the Octoechos and 2–3 to the Triodion is inferred from the general Lenten rule (line 209). Whether Passion Week overrides this is not explicitly stated.

> [!NOTE]
> **No small litanies between kathismata:** The Lenten rubric (Part IV, line 209) does not prescribe small litanies between kathismata during Lent. This differs from Great Matins (Part I, line 156), which prescribes them.

### V. MATINS GOSPEL

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 578):** *"…then 'And that we may be accounted worthy,' Gospel of Matins according to the sequence of Gospels, Psalm 50 and 'Save, O God.'"*
> **"Save, O God" detail (Part IV, line 210):** *"the first petition of the great litany 'Save, O God'"*

This is a unique Passion Week feature: ordinary Lenten Matins has no Gospel. Part IV, line 578 inserts a Gospel using a simplified formula distinct from the full Great Matins Gospel rite (Part I, line 160).

#### Phase A — Gospel Formula (steps 1–4)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Deacon** (or Priest) | Exclaims: **"And that we may be accounted worthy to hear the Holy Gospel…"** (line 578). |
| 2 | **Priest** | *"Peace be to all."* |
| 3 | **Deacon** (or Priest) | *"Wisdom. Aright. Let us listen."* |
| 4 | **Priest** | *"The reading from the Holy Gospel according to [Name]."* |

> [!NOTE]
> **Steps 2–4 are the standard Gospel exclamation sequence.** Part IV, line 578 prescribes only *"And that we may be accounted worthy"* + *"Gospel."* The intermediate exclamations ("Peace be to all," "Wisdom," etc.) are the fixed liturgical formula that always accompanies a Gospel reading. Part I, line 192 confirms this sequence for deacons: *"the first: 'Wisdom, Upright, let us hear the Holy Gospel,' the priest: 'The reading is from the Holy Gospel according to (Name).' The second deacon: 'Let us be attentive.'"*

#### Phase B — Gospel Reading (steps 5–6)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | **Deacon** (or Priest) | *"Let us be attentive."* |
| 6 | **Priest** | **Reads the Gospel** — *"according to the sequence of Gospels"* (line 578). |

#### Phase C — Psalm 50 + "Save, O God" (steps 7–9)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | **Reader** | **Psalm 50**: *"Have mercy on me, O God…"* (line 578). |
| 8 | **Priest** | Exclaims the **first petition only** of the great litany: *"Save, O God, Thy people…"* (line 578 + line 210). |
| 9 | **Priest** | Exclamation: *"Through the mercy and compassion…"* (line 210). |

> [!NOTE]
> **Immediately — the Canon:** Part IV, line 210 prescribes *"immediately — the Canon"* after this exclamation. There is no intervening element.

### VI. CANON — COMMON ELEMENTS (all three days)

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 579) — full text:** *"Canon only one: triodion on Monday and Wednesday, or diodion [two-ode canon] on Tuesday. Troparia — on 12, heirmoi — twice. We do not recite the [Biblical] Odes, but only the refrain to the troparia of the canon 'Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee', then the Katavasia, heirmos of the canon once both choirs together. Before the Kondakion-Ikos the priest sings the small litany; at the 9th Ode we do not sing 'More honorable', nor 'It is truly meet' after the canon, but immediately after the Katavasia — small litany and Exaposteilarion 'Thy bridal chamber' twice, Glory, Both now: three times the same."*

#### Phase A — Per-Ode Cycle (repeats for each ode of the canon)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Choir** | Sings the **heirmos** of the ode — sung **twice** (line 579: *"heirmoi — twice"*). |
| 2 | **Choir** | Sings the **troparia** of the ode — **12 per ode** (line 579: *"Troparia — on 12"*). |
| | | Refrain before each troparion: *"Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee"* (line 579). |
| 3 | **Choir** | Before the **penultimate** troparion: *"Glory."* |
| 4 | **Choir** | Before the **last** troparion: *"Both now."* |
| 5 | **Both Choirs** | Sing the **Katavasia** — heirmos of the canon, **once**, both choirs together (line 579: *"the Katavasia, heirmos of the canon once both choirs together"*). |

#### Phase B — After the Ode Before the Kondakion-Ikos: Small Litany

> Line 579: *"Before the Kondakion-Ikos the priest sings the small litany."*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 6 | **Priest** | Sings the **Small Litany** (line 579). |
| 7 | **Choir** | Responds to each petition. |
| 8 | **Priest** | Exclamation. |
| 9 | **Choir** | **Kondakion** of the day *(day-specific — see DAY-SPECIFIC DIFFERENCES below).* |
| 10 | **Choir** | **Ikos** of the day. |

#### Phase C — Ode 8: Special Refrains *(general canon rule, Part I, line 174)*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 11 | **Choir** | Before the **penultimate** troparion of Ode 8, instead of *"Glory"*: **"Let us bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, the Lord."** |
| 12 | **Choir** | As refrain to the **Katavasia** of Ode 8: **"We praise, we bless, we worship the Lord, praising and magnifying Him unto all ages."** |

> [!NOTE]
> These Ode 8 refrains (Part I, line 174) are a general rule for all canons, not a Passion-Week-specific instruction. Line 174: *"at the 8th Ode, instead of Glory — the refrain 'Let us bless the Father'…"*

#### Phase D — Ode 9: Suppressions (per line 579)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 13 | | **"More honorable" is NOT sung** before Ode 9 (line 579: *"at the 9th Ode we do not sing 'More honorable'"*). |
| 14 | **Choir** | Sings the heirmos of Ode 9 **twice** (same rule as other odes — line 579). |
| 15 | **Choir** | Sings the 12 troparia of Ode 9 with refrains (same as other odes). |
| 16 | **Both Choirs** | Sing the **Katavasia** of Ode 9. |
| 17 | | **"It is truly meet" is NOT said** after the canon (line 579: *"nor 'It is truly meet' after the canon"*). |

**Biblical Odes:** Not recited (line 579: *"We do not recite the [Biblical] Odes"*).

**Canon form and odes are day-specific** — see **DAY-SPECIFIC DIFFERENCES** below.

> [!NOTE]
> **Ode 3 litany:** Line 579 prescribes a small litany only *"before the Kondakion-Ikos"* and *"after the Katavasia"* of Ode 9. Whether a small litany is also sung after Ode 3 is not explicitly stated in line 579. The general canon rule (Part I, line 176: *"After the 3rd, 6th and 9th Ode of the canon… the Small Litany is taken"*) prescribes litanies after Odes 3, 6, and 9 — but this is a general rule not specifically confirmed for Passion Week.

> [!NOTE]
> **Kondakion placement ambiguity:** In a triodion (Odes 1, 8, 9) or diodion (Odes 8, 9), there is no 6th Ode. Line 579 says *"Before the Kondakion-Ikos the priest sings the small litany"* without specifying after which ode. The exact placement is not explicit in the primary source. The general rule (Part I, line 176) places the Kondakion-Ikos after the litany at Ode 6.
