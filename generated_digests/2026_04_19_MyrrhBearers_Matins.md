# Liturgical Digest: Sunday, April 19, 2026
## Sunday of the Myrrh-Bearing Women (3rd Sunday after Pascha)
### Commemoration: Our Venerable Father John the Ancient Hermit (Transferred to Compline)

---

**Tone:** 2  
**Matins Gospel:** 2nd (Mark 16:1-8)  
**Governing Source:** Dolnytsky Typikon, Part IV, lines 806–821 (General Easter Rubrics) and 831–847 (Sunday of the Myrrh-Bearers).

---

## GENERAL NOTES

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Saint Interaction:** St. John the Ancient Hermit (April 19) is a saint without Polyeleos. Per Dolnytsky (Part IV, 821), his service is transferred to **Compline**. The Matins service is entirely dedicated to the Resurrection and the Myrrh-Bearing Women (Triodion).

> [!TIP]
> **Paschal Elements:** Every Sunday until the Ascension, Matins begins with "Christ is risen" (3) after the initial blessing. The 9th Ode replaces "More honorable" with the Paschal refrains and "Shine, shine."

---

## PHYSICAL PREPARATION & VESTMENTS

*   **Vestments:** White/Silver (Bright/Resurrectional).
*   **Icon:** The icon of the Resurrection (Descent into Hades) is on the central analogion. At the appropriate time (the Gospel), the Gospel Book is brought out.

## SERVICE OUTLINE

### I. OPENING RITE — PREPARATORY BLOCK

> **Primary Source:** Part IV, 806; Part I, 143.

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Priest** | Vests in epitrachelion and phelonion. Stands before the Holy Table. |
| 2 | **Priest** | Blesses the censer. Censes the Holy Table in a cross-wise manner. |
| 3 | **Priest** | Exclaims: **"Blessed is our God, always, now and ever and unto ages of ages."** (Part IV, 806). |
| 4 | **Choir/All** | Sings: **"Christ is risen from the dead, trampling down death by death, and upon those in the tombs bestowing life."** (3 times). |
| 5 | **Priest** | Censes around the Holy Table, the sanctuary, and (exiting through the North door) the iconostasis and people. |
| 6 | **Reader** | **Six Psalms** (Pss. 3, 37, 62, 87, 102, 142). |
| 7 | **Priest** | Recites the Morning Prayers before the Holy Doors. |

### VI. CANONS & 9th ODE

> **Primary Source:** Part IV, 843–845.

**Structure (14 Troparia):**
*   **Resurrectional:** 4 (Part IV, 843).
*   **Theotokos of Triodion:** 2.
*   **Myrrh-bearers:** 8.
*   **Katavasia:** Paschal (irmoi of the Canon of Pascha).

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Choir** | **Ode 1–8** (Sequential according to the structure above). |
| 2 | **Priest** | After Ode 3: Small Litany; **Sessional Hymn of the Myrrh-bearers** (Triodion). |
| 3 | **Priest** | After Ode 6: Small Litany; **Kontakion & Ikos of the Myrrh-bearers** (Tone 2). |
| 4 | **Priest** | After Ode 8: Censes the Sanctuary and Icons. |
| 5 | **Priest** | **Suppress "More honorable"**; Exclaims: **"The Theotokos and Mother of Light..."** |
| 6 | **Choir** | Sings **Paschal Refrains** (The Angel Cried) and **"Shine, shine, O New Jerusalem..."** (Part IV, 815, 845). |
