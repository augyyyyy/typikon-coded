## PHYSICAL PREPARATION

> **Line 467:** *"At the time of the singing of the canon the holy doors are opened and candles are lit on the holy table. Before the holy doors in cathedral churches a rug is spread."*

| Element | Notes |
|---|---|
| Rug before Holy Doors | Cathedral practice; clergy make prostrations on it by rank |
| Two analogia in center of church | Left and right choirs stand here for the entire Great Canon |
| Holy Doors | Remain **open** throughout the entire Great Canon |
| Candles on Holy Table | Lit at the beginning of the Great Canon; remain lit |

## NOTE ON THE LIFE OF ST. MARY OF EGYPT

Dolnytsky lines 467–470 describe the **troparia** to St. Mary within the canon structure, but do not prescribe the precise moment at which the hagiographic *Life* text is read. The troparia rubric (line 468) specifies only that 2 troparia per ode are dedicated to her. The traditional placement of the full *Life* reading (following the Jerusalem practice adopted in the Ukrainian Greek Catholic tradition) is:

- **Part I** — after the Sessional Hymn following Ode 3
- **Part II** — after the Beatitudes-Ikos block following Ode 6

These are marked `📖` in the service order above as **traditional placement**, not a specific Dolnytsky line citation.
