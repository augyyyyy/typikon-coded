# Paschal Matins (The Pentecostarion Matrix)
## Liturgical Digest

> **When served:** Pascha (Easter Sunday) and every day of Bright Week.
> **Governing logic:** The Pentecostarion completely obliterates the Typikon engine. The entire monastic foundation of the rite (the reading of the Psalter) is violently expunged from the service. Nothing is read. Everything is sung in ecstatic joy. This represents the ultimate "Anomaly Matrix" of the church year.

---

## I. THE PASCHAL ENGINE OVERRIDE

If the system detects `Bright Week` via the Pentecostarion logic, almost every standard Matins node is forcibly deleted.
*   **The Six Psalms:** DELETED.
*   **The Kathismata:** DELETED.
*   **The Polyeleos:** DELETED.
*   **The Magnificat (9th Ode):** DELETED.
*   **Psalm 50:** DELETED.
*   **The Great Doxology:** DELETED.

## III. THE PASCHAL HOOKS

### NODE A: The Processional Enarxis (Christ is Risen!)
Because the Six Psalms are eliminated, the service must begin with the Resurrection itself.
*   **The Procession:** On Pascha night, the clergy and faithful process fully around the outside of the dark church. On Bright Week days, they process around the Altar or remain in the center of the nave.
*   **The Exclamation:** *"Glory to the Holy, Consubstantial, Life-Creating and Undivided Trinity..."* 
*   **The Troparion Array:** The clergy sing *"Christ is risen from the dead, trampling down death by death..."*, and the choir responds. This repeats sequentially interwoven with the verses of Psalm 67 (*"Let God arise and let His enemies be scattered"*).

### NODE B: The Paschal Canon
The massive centerpiece of the service, composed by St. John of Damascus. It completely overtakes the service.
*   **Litanies Everywhere:** Rather than only at the 3rd, 6th, and 9th Odes, a Small Litany is chanted by the Deacon after **every single Ode** of the canon.
*   **Katavasia Suppressed:** Between the odes, instead of typical Katavasiae, the choir sings *"Christ is Risen"* three times.
*   **Censing:** The Priest must cense the entire temple, pausing at every icon to exclaim *"Christ is Risen!"* to the people, running throughout the entire 45-minute chanting of the Canon.

### NODE C: The Paschal Stichera
Occurs at the end of the Praises ("Lord I Have Cried" of the morning).
*   **The Array:** The choir sings *"Let God arise"* followed immediately by the Paschal Stichera (*"A sacred Pascha has been shown forth to us today..."*). 
*   **Glory / Both Now:** *"It is the Day of Resurrection! Let us illumine ourselves..."*
*   **The Kiss of Peace:** During the singing of the Doxastikon on Pascha itself, the faithful exchange the Paschal kiss (greeting one another with *"Christ is Risen! Indeed He is Risen!"*).

### NODE D: The Paschal Conclusion 
The standard Matins dismissal is entirely rewritten for Bright Week.
*   **The Dialogue:** 
    *   Priest: *"Christ is Risen!"* (x3)
    *   Faithful: *"Indeed He is Risen!"* (x3)
*   **Conclusion Hymn:** The choir sings the final Troparion (*"Christ is Risen"*) in a massive drawn-out crescendo.
*   **Final Proclamation:** The Priest raises the cross: *"And unto us He has given eternal life. Let us worship His Resurrection on the third day!"*
