# Midnight Office (Matrix Prototype)
## Liturgical Digest

> **When served:** The Midnight Office is traditionally served in the middle of the night or immediately before Matins in the early morning. It represents the church keeping the vigil for the Second Coming of Christ (the "Bridegroom").
> **Governing logic:** The service operates independently of the 20 Dolnytsky general cases, branching uniquely based entirely on the **Day of the Week** (Sunday, Saturday, or Mon-Fri).

---

## I. THE INVARIABLE SPINE
The service remains largely fixed, never deploying stichera from the Menaion or Octoechos.

| # | Action | Condition / Fork |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **The Enarxis** | Priest exclaims *"Blessed is our God"* from before the closed doors. |
| **2** | **Initial Prayers** | *"Glory to Thee... O Heavenly King..."*, Trisagion. |
| **3** | **Psalm 50** | *"Have mercy on me, O God"* (Read). |
| **4** | **[NODE A: Kathisma]** | *See Day of Week Matrix below.* |
| **5** | **Nicene Creed** | *"I believe in one God"* (Read). |
| **6** | **Trisagion** | "Our Father". |
| **7** | **[NODE B: Troparia & Canons]**| *See Day of Week Matrix below.* |
| **8** | **Prayer of the Hours** | *"Thou Who at all times..."* |
| **9** | **St. Mardarije** | *"O God Master, Father Almighty..."* |
| **10** | **Conclusion** | Lesser Dismissal. Rite of Forgiveness. |

---

## II. THE MATRIX (DAY OF THE WEEK)

### The Sunday Matrix
*(Preparing the church for the celebration of the Resurrection)*
*   **[Node A - Kathisma]:** The Kathisma is completely **suppressed**.
*   **[Node B - Troparia & Canons]:** The **Canon to the Holy Trinity** from the Octoechos (in the tone of the week) is chanted. After the 9th Ode, the Trinitarian Megalynaria (*"It is truly meet to glorify the Supreme Word..."*) and the Hypakoe of the Tone.

### The Saturday Matrix
*(Saturday is uniquely dedicated to praying for the departed, recognizing the day Christ rested in the Tomb)*
*   **[Node A - Kathisma]:** The **9th Kathisma** is read.
*   **[Node B - Troparia & Canons]:** Sings the Troparia for the Departed: *"O Thou Who by the depth of Thy wisdom dost provide all things out of love for mankind..."*

### The Weekday Matrix (Monday through Friday)
*(This form anticipates the sudden Second Coming)*
*   **[Node A - Kathisma]:** The **17th Kathisma** (Psalm 118, the massive *"Blessed are the undefiled in the way..."*) is chanted.
*   **[Node B - Troparia & Canons]:** Sings the Bridegroom Troparia: *"Behold, the Bridegroom cometh at midnight, and blessed is the servant whom He shall find watching..."*

---

## III. FASTING INTERSECTIONS

If the Midnight Office is celebrated during **Great Lent**:
1.  **Node B Override:** Following the troparia, the massive **Prayer of St. Ephrem** is inserted with full earth prostrations.
2.  *(Note: The Midnight Office is sometimes suppressed entirely on weekdays of the First Week of Great Lent in monastic practice, absorbed by the expanded Matins and Hours schedule).*
