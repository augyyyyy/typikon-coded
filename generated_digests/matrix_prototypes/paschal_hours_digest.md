# The Paschal Hours (The Pentecostarion Matrix)
## Liturgical Digest

> **When served:** Pascha (Easter Sunday) and every day of Bright Week.
> **Governing logic:** The Pentecostarion completely obliterates the minor hours. The 1st, 3rd, 6th, and 9th hours are completely stripped of their psalmic anchor points. Nothing is read. The service is fundamentally a repeated loop of resurrectional hymns sung enthusiastically.

---

## I. THE PASCHAL ENGINE OVERRIDE

If the system detects `Bright Week` via the Pentecostarion logic, almost every standard Minor Hours node is forcibly deleted.
*   **The Three Psalms:** DELETED.
*   **The Trisagion to Our Father:** DELETED. (Replaced by "Christ is Risen").
*   **Psalm 50 (if 3rd hour):** DELETED.
*   **Lord Have Mercy x40:** DELETED.
*   **The Prayer of the Hours (*"Thou Who at all times..."*):** DELETED.
*   **The Final Prayers of the Hours:** DELETED.

---

## II. THE INVARIABLE PASCHAL SPINE
Because the hours are completely leveled, the 1st, 3rd, 6th, and 9th Hours are **absolutely identical** during Bright Week. There is no distinction between them.

| # | Action | Condition / Fork |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **The Enarxis** | Priest: *"Blessed is our God..."* |
| **2** | **Christ is Risen** | Sung three times. |
| **3** | **Having Beheld the Resurrection** | *"Having beheld the Resurrection of Christ..."* (Sung three times). |
| **4** | **The Hypakoe** | *"Before the dawn Mary and the women..."* (Sung once). |
| **5** | **The Kontakion** | *"Thou didst descend into the tomb, O Immortal..."* (Sung once). |
| **6** | **The Troparia Stack** | *"In the tomb with the body..."*, Glory: *"As bearing life..."*, Both Now: *"Rejoice, O holy and divine abode..."* |
| **7** | **Lord Have Mercy** | EXACTLY forty times. (The only read element preserved from the regular hours). |
| **8** | **Conclusion** | *"Glory... Both now... More honorable... In the name of the Lord, Father bless!"* |
| **9** | **Blessing** | Priest: *"Through the prayers of our Holy Fathers..."* |
| **10** | **Final Sequence**| *"Christ is risen"* (x3), *"Glory... Both now..."*, *"Lord have mercy"* (x3) and the final dismissal. |

---

## III. USAGE (TYPIKA FUSION)

If the **Typika (Obidnitsia)** is required during Bright Week (for example, if a Liturgy cannot be served and a reader's service must replace it):
*   **The Override:** The Typika itself is also completely unraveled.
*   **The Implementation:** To serve the "Typika" during Bright Week, the Reader simply recites the **Paschal Hours** a final time, substituting the final blessing with a more pronounced conclusion attributing the grace to the Resurrection.
