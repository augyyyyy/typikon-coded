# Holy Friday Matins (The Twelve Passion Gospels)
## Liturgical Digest

> **When served:** Thursday evening of Holy Week (serving as the Matins of Holy Friday).
> **Governing logic:** This is one of the most uniquely structured services of the liturgical year. The entire service is suspended upon the chanting of Twelve distinct Gospel pericopes recounting the Passion of Christ, interspersed with massive blocks of antiphons and sessional hymns. It is governed rigorously by Dolnytsky Part IV, lines 641-653.

---

## COMMON RUBRICS
1. **The Vestments:** The clergy are vested in dark violet. 
2. **The Censing:** The temple is not censed entirely at every Gospel. According to Dolnytsky (Part IV, Line 640): *"Everything should be censed only twice, that is at the 1st Gospel and at the Canon... At the other Gospels, only the Gospel and the standing people."*
3. **The Centerpiece:** The Gospel is not read from the Altar, but from an analogion placed in the center of the nave (Part IV, Line 642).
