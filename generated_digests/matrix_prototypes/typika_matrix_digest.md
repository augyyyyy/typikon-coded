# The Typika (Obidnitsia) Matrix Prototype
## Liturgical Digest

> **When served:** The Typika (or "Obidnitsia") is served when the Divine Liturgy is not celebrated. This occurs most frequently on "Aliturgical" days (weekdays of Great Lent where Presanctified is not prescribed, like Monday/Tuesday/Thursday), or when a priest is absent (a "Reader's service").
> **Governing logic:** It functions as a "dry Liturgy," substituting the Eucharistic components with psalms and prayers. It exists in two primary states: **Standalone** or **Aggregated** (attached to the 9th Hour).

## II. THE ANOMALY MATRICES

### NODE A: The Typical Psalms Hook
*   **Standalone Form (Reader's Service on Sunday/Weekday):** The service begins with Psalm 102 (*"Bless the Lord O my soul"*), Psalm 145 (*"Praise the Lord O my soul"*), and the hymn *"Only Begotten Son"*.
*   **Aggregated Form (Attached to the 9th Hour):** If the Typika is served immediately following the 9th Hour (as is strictly required during Great Lent and Royal Hours), Psalms 102 and 145 are **suppressed**. The service snaps immediately into the Beatitudes.

### NODE B: The Kontakia Stack
Because there are no Troparia in the Typika, only Kontakia are read. The hierarchy of Kontakia depends heavily on whether it is a fasting day or a festal day.
*   **General Rule (If no feast):** Kontakion of the Transfiguration -> Kontakion of the Day -> Glory: Martyricon -> Both Now: Theotokion.
*   **Festal Intervention (Cases 03, 04, 10, 11, 12, 13):** If a Great Feast or Afterfeast happens to fall on an Aliturgical day (e.g. Feast of the Cross falling on a Lenten Thursday), Only the Kontakion of the Feast is read.
*   **Lenten Exception:** If the Temple Patron is a Saint, the Saint's Kontakion is read before the Glory.

### NODE C: The Lenten Conclusion Override
If the Typika is served during Great Lent (Mon-Fri) or as the conclusion to the Royal Hours:
*   **Standard:** The service proceeds to Psalm 33 and Dismissal.
*   **Lenten Override:** Before Psalm 33, the massive **Prayer of St. Ephrem the Syrian** is inserted, accompanied by 3 (or 16) earth prostrations. The dismissal incorporates *"Lord have mercy"* (x40) and the Prayer of the Heavenly King.
