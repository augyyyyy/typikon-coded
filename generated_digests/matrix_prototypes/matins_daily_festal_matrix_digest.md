# Daily & Festal Matins (Matrix Prototype)
## Liturgical Digest

> **When served:** Every non-Sunday morning outside of Great Lent.
> **Governing logic:** Without the overwhelming presence of the Resurrectional cycle, the Menaion (Saint/Feast of the day) dictates the structure. This document governs **Cases 05, 07, and 10-18** of Dolnytsky Part II, showcasing how a weekday scales from "Simple" to "Festal Vigil".

## II. THE VARIABLE MATRICES (DAILY & FESTAL CASES)

### NODE A: Troparia Stack (Following "God is the Lord")
*   **Case 05 (Simple Weekday Rank 4/5/6):** Saint Troparion (x2). Glory/Both Now: Theotokion.
*   **Case 07 (Polyeleos/Vigil Saint):** Saint Troparion (x2). Glory/Both Now: Festal Theotokion of the tone.
*   **Cases 10, 11, 12, 13 (Great Feasts & Apodoses):** Festal Troparion (x2 or x3). Theotokia are suppressed.
*   **Cases 14-16 (Forefeasts/Afterfeasts):** Festal Troparion -> Glory -> Saint Troparion -> Both Now -> Festal Troparion.

### NODE B: Kathismata & Polyeleos Hooks
*   **Cases 05, 14, 15, 16 (Simple & Feasts without Vigil):** 2 Kathismata are read. The Sedalens are drawn from the Octoechos. The Polyeleos is **suppressed**.
*   **Cases 07, 10, 18 (Vigils & Polyeleos Feasts):** 2 Kathismata with Sedalens. The **Polyeleos** (Psalms 134/135 *"Praise ye the name of the Lord"*) is sung festively. The Sessional Hymn of the Saint/Feast follows.

### NODE C: The Matins Gospel
*   **Cases 05, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16:** **Suppressed.** The Priest exclaims *"O God save thy people..."* immediately after the Sedalens.
*   **Cases 07, 10, 18 (Vigils & Polyeleos Feasts):** The Gospel of the Saint or Feast is read from the analogion. 

### NODE D: The Canons (14 Troparia Stack)
*   **Case 05 (Simple Weekday):** 14 Troparia heavily favoring the Octoechos daily themes (e.g., Angels on Monday, Forerunner on Tuesday, Cross on Wed/Fri) combined with the Menaion Saint.
*   **Cases 07, 10 (Vigils & Feasts):** The Octoechos is suppressed. The Canon is entirely Festal/Menaion (e.g. 6 Festal, 8 Menaion).
*   *(Note: On Case 05, Katavasiae are omitted, the choir simply sings the Heirmos of the 3rd, 6th, and 9th ode. On Festal Cases, the full Katavasia of the seasonal cycle is sung).*

### NODE E: The Praises Stack (Psalms 148-150)
*   **Cases 05, 14, 15, 16 (Simple Days):** **Read.** No stichera are sung at the Praises. The structural flow drops directly from reading Psalm 150 into the Small Doxology, and then into the Aposticha.
*   **Cases 07, 10, 18 (Vigils & Feasts):** **Sung.** 4 or 6 Stichera of the Feast/Saint. Glory: Feast/Saint. Both Now: Theotokion.

### NODE F: Doxology & Conclusion
*   **Case 05 (Simple Weekdays):** The **Small Doxology** is strictly read. The service hooks into the **Aposticha of Matins**, followed by Litanies and Dismissal.
*   **Cases 07, 10, 18 (Vigils & Feasts):** The **Great Doxology** is sung festively. The Aposticha is suppressed entirely. Concludes directly with the Festal/Saint Troparion, Litanies, and Dismissal.
