# Great Vespers (Matrix Prototype)
## Liturgical Digest

> **When served:** Great Vespers is celebrated on the eve of Sundays and Great Feasts (Ranks 1, 2, and 3). 
> **Governing logic:** Because Great Vespers stretches across multiple paradigm cases depending on the rank of the Menaion Saint and the liturgical period (Octoechos vs. Triodion vs. Pentecostarion vs. Pre/Post-Feast), the variable hubs ("Lord I Have Cried", "Aposticha", and "Troparia") strictly follow the **20 General Cases of Dolnytsky Part II**.

---

## I. THE INVARIABLE SPINE
The following structural flow is common to all Great Vespers, branching only at designated *Variable Nodes*.

| # | Action | Condition / Fork |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **The Enarxis (Opening)** | **If Vigil:** Censing in silence, *"Glory to the Holy, Consubstantial..."*<br>**If Standalone:** *"Blessed is our God..."* |
| **2** | **Psalm 103** | Sung festively. |
| **3** | **Great Litany** | Read by Deacon. |
| **4** | **[NODE A: Kathisma]** | *See Kathisma Matrix below.* |
| **5** | **[NODE B: Lord, I Have Cried]**| *See distribution matrix below.* |
| **6** | **The Entrance** | Made with the censer (or Gospel if there is a reading). |
| **7** | **"O Joyful Light"** | Sung festively. |
| **8** | **Daily Prokeimenon** | Sung. If a Great Feast, Paremiya (OT Readings) are read here. |
| **9** | **Litanies & Supplications** | *"Let us all say..."*, *"Vouchsafe O Lord..."*, *"Let us complete..."* |
| **10** | **[NODE C: The Litiya]** | **If Vigil:** Procession to Narthex, massive intercessions.<br>**If Standalone:** Suppressed. |
| **11** | **[NODE D: Aposticha]** | *See distribution matrix below.* |
| **12** | **Prayer of St. Simeon** | *"Now lettest thou thy servant..."*, Trisagion. |
| **13** | **[NODE E: Troparia]** | *See distribution matrix below.* |
| **14** | **The Conclusion** | **If Vigil:** Blessing of Loaves (Artoklasia), transition to Matins.<br>**If Standalone:** Great Dismissal. |

---

## II. THE CASE MATRIX

The following defines exactly how the Variable Nodes (A, B, D, E) resolve based on the overarching Paradigm Case (Dolnytsky Part II).

### Case 01: Simple Sunday 
*(Ordinary Sunday with a Rank 4/6 Saint in the Menaion)*
*   **[Node A - Kathisma]:** 1st Kathisma (*"Blessed is the man"*).
*   **[Node B - Lord I Have Cried (10)]:** 7 Resurrection (Octoechos), 3 Saint (Menaion). Glory: Saint (if present). Both Now: Dogmatikon of the Tone.
*   **[Node D - Aposticha]:** 4 Resurrection. Glory: Saint (if present). Both Now: Theotokion.
*   **[Node E - Troparia]:** *"Rejoice O Virgin Theotokos"* (x3) (If Vigil). If Standalone: Resurrection Troparion. Glory/Both Now: Theotokion.

### Case 02: Sunday with a Saint (Rank 5 on 6)
*   **[Node B - Lord I Have Cried (10)]:** 6 Resurrection, 4 Saint. Glory: Saint. Both Now: Dogmatikon.
*   *(Nodes A, D, E remain identical to Case 01).*

### Case 03: Sunday with a Great Feast of the Lord
*(E.g., Elevation of the Cross falling on a Sunday)*
*   **[Node A - Kathisma]:** 1st Kathisma.
*   **[Node B - Lord I Have Cried (10)]:** 4 Resurrection, 6 Feast. Glory: Feast. Both Now: Dogmatikon.
*   **[Node C - Litiya]:** Mandatory. Stichera of the Feast.
*   **[Node D - Aposticha]:** 4 Resurrection. Glory/Both Now: Feast.
*   **[Node E - Troparia]:** Resurrection Troparion (x2). Festal Troparion (x1).

### Case 04: Sunday with a Great Feast of the Theotokos
*(E.g., Dormition falling on a Sunday)*
*   **[Node B - Lord I Have Cried (10)]:** 4 Resurrection, 6 Feast. Glory/Both Now: Feast.
*   **[Node D - Aposticha]:** 4 Resurrection. Glory/Both Now: Feast.
*   **[Node E - Troparia]:** Resurrection Troparion (x1). Festal Troparion (x2).

### Case 06: Sunday with a Polyeleos Saint (Rank 3)
*   **[Node B - Lord I Have Cried (10)]:** 4 Resurrection, 6 Saint. Glory: Saint. Both Now: Dogmatikon.
*   **[Node D - Aposticha]:** 4 Resurrection. Glory: Saint. Both Now: Theotokion.
*   **[Node E - Troparia (If Vigil)]:** *"Rejoice O Virgin Theotokos"* (x2). Troparion of the Saint (x1).

### Case 07: Weekday Vigil (Feast or Polyeleos Saint)
*(E.g., St. Nicholas falling on a Tuesday)*
*   **[Node A - Kathisma]:** Kathisma of the Day. (If Monday eve, 1st Kathisma).
*   **[Node B - Lord I Have Cried (8)]:** 8 Saint / Feast. Glory: Saint/Feast. Both Now: Dogmatikon of the Tone (or Festal Theotokion).
*   **[Node D - Aposticha]:** 4 Saint / Feast Aposticha. Glory: Saint/Feast. Both Now: Theotokion.
*   **[Node E - Troparia (If Vigil)]:** Saint/Festal Troparion (x2). *"Rejoice O Virgin Theotokos"* (x1).

### Case 08 & 09: Sunday Forefeast (With/Without Saint)
*(The Sunday immediately preceding a Great Feast)*
*   **[Node B - Lord I Have Cried (10)]:** 4 Resurrection, 3 Forefeast, 3 Saint. Glory: Forefeast or Saint. Both Now: Dogmatikon.
*   **[Node D - Aposticha]:** 4 Resurrection. Glory: Saint/Forefeast. Both Now: Forefeast.
*   **[Node E - Troparia]:** Resurrection (If Standalone) -> Glory: Saint -> Both Now: Forefeast.

### Case 10: Weekday Forefeast
*(Ordinary weekday preceding a Great Feast)*
*   **[Node B - Lord I Have Cried (6)]:** 3 Forefeast, 3 Saint. Glory: Forefeast/Saint. Both Now: Forefeast.
*   **[Node D - Aposticha]:** Forefeast Aposticha. Glory/Both Now: Forefeast.

### Case 11 & 12: Sunday Afterfeast (With/Without Saint)
*(The Sundays following Pascha, Pentecost, or Great Feasts)*
*   **[Node B - Lord I Have Cried (10)]:** 4 Resurrection, 6 Feast (from the Menaion/Pentecostarion). Glory: Feast. Both Now: Dogmatikon.
*   **[Node D - Aposticha]:** 4 Resurrection. Glory: Feast/Saint. Both Now: Feast.
*   **[Node E - Troparia]:** Resurrection (x2). Festal Troparion (x1).

### Case 13: Apodosis (Leavetaking) on a Sunday
*(The final day of a Festal cycle intersecting with Sunday)*
*   **[Node B - Lord I Have Cried (10)]:** 4 Resurrection, 6 Feast. Glory/Both Now: Feast.
*   **[Node D - Aposticha]:** 4 Resurrection. Glory/Both Now: Feast.
*   **[Node E - Troparia]:** Resurrection Troparion. Glory/Both Now: Festal Troparion.

## III. FASTING INTERSECTIONS (PART IV CASES)

If Great Vespers is celebrated during a fasting period (e.g., Annunciation falling on a weekday in Great Lent):
1. **Lenten Tone:** The service retains the Festal stichera, but the melodies and remaining fixed hymns shift to the Lenten tone.
2. **"Alleluia" Displacements:** If the day is a strict fast, the Liturgy is generally Presanctified, meaning this Great Vespers merges *into* the Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts, effectively clipping the service after the Entrance and Readings, transitioning directly into the *"Let my prayer arise"* of the Presanctified.
