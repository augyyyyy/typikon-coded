# The Divine Liturgy (Matrix Prototype)
## Liturgical Digest

> **When served:** The Divine Liturgy of St. John Chrysostom is the central Eucharistic service of the Byzantine Rite, celebrated on almost all Sundays and weekdays of the year (except during Great Lent).
> **Governing logic:** The core texts (Anaphoras, Litanies) are completely rigid. The structural variations rely exclusively on the **Hymn Stacking Algorithm (Temple Rules A, B, C)** and the **20 Paradigm Cases (Dolnytsky Part II)** injecting the proper Antiphons, Troparia, Readings, and Communion Hymns.

## II. THE MATRICES (DOLNYTSKY 20 CASES)

### NODE A: The Antiphon Matrix
*   **The Typical Psalms (Sundays & Vigils):** Cases 01, 02, 03, 04, 06, 07, 08, 11, 17, 18. (Psalms 102, 145, and the Beatitudes).
*   **The Everyday Antiphons (Ordinary Weekdays):** Cases 05, 12, 14, 15, 16. (Psalms 9, 92, 94).
*   **The Festal Antiphons (Great Feasts of the Lord):** Cases 10, 13 (and 03 if a Lord's Feast on Sunday). Specific Psalm verses with refrains (*"Through the prayers of the Theotokos...", "Save us O Son of God..."*).

### NODE B: The Hymn Stacking Matrix (Troparia & Kontakia)
This resolves the "Little Entrance" puzzle. The hierarchy is: Resurrection -> Feast -> Temple Patron -> Menaion Saint. The specific sequence is dictated by the Physical Temple dedication (Rule A: Temple of the Lord, Rule B: Temple of the Theotokos, Rule C: Temple of a Saint).

*   **Case 01 (Simple Sunday):** 
    *   *Troparia:* Resurrection, Temple Patron, Saint of the Day.
    *   *Kontakia:* Glory: Saint. Both Now: Resurrection (or Temple).
*   **Case 03 (Feast of the Lord on Sunday):** 
    *   *Troparia/Kontakia:* The Patron/Temple is entirely suppressed. Only the Resurrection and the Feast are sung.
*   **Case 05 (Simple Weekday):** 
    *   *Troparia:* Temple Patron, Day of the Week, Menaion Saint.
*   **Cases 10, 11 (Great Feasts):** 
    *   *Troparia/Kontakia:* The Feast completely dominates. No other Troparia are sung (Except Resurrection if Case 11).

### NODE C: The Readings Matrix (Prokeimena, Epistles, Gospels)
*   **Cases 01, 02 (Ordinary Sundays):** The Resurrection (Octoechos) Prokeimenon, Epistle, Alleluia, and Gospel.
*   **Cases 03, 04 (Sunday Feasts):** The Resurrection and the Festal readings are *combined* (read sequentially). The Prokeimenon of the Feast takes precedence.
*   **Case 05 (Ordinary Weekdays):** The Day of the Week readings. If the Saint has readings assigned, the Day is read first, then the Saint.
*   **Cases 10, 13 (Great Feasts):** The daily readings are suppressed; only the Festal readings are proclaimed.

### NODE D: The Megalynarion Matrix (Hymn to the Theotokos)
*   **Standard Rule:** *"It is truly meet to bless thee, O Theotokos..."* (Axion Estin) is sung for Cases 01, 02, 05, 06, 07, 08, 14, 15, 16.
*   **Festal Override (The Zadostoinik):** For Great Feasts and their Afterfeasts (Cases 03, 04, 10, 11, 12, 13, 17, 18), the Irmos of the 9th Ode of the specific Festal Canon is sung instead.

### NODE E: The Koinonikon Matrix (Communion Hymn)
*   **Sundays:** *"Praise the Lord from the heavens..."* (Psalm 148).
*   **Tuesdays:** *"The righteous shall be in everlasting remembrance..."* (Psalm 111).
*   **Feasts/Saints:** Sourced directly from the Menaion (e.g. *"I will take the cup of salvation"* on Marian feasts).
*   *(Note: If a Sunday intersects with a Saint (Case 01), both Communion hymns are sung).*

## III. FASTING INTERSECTIONS (PART IV)
The Liturgy of St. John Chrysostom is rarely celebrated during Great Lent.

1.  **Saturdays of Lent:** Chrysostom is celebrated, but heavily focused on the Memorial of the Departed. The Communion Hymn is *"Blessed are they whom Thou hast chosen..."*
2.  **Sundays of Lent:** The Liturgy of St. John Chrysostom is entirely **suppressed** and replaced by the longer **Divine Liturgy of St. Basil the Great** (which features massive Anaphoral prayers and *"All of Creation rejoices in thee"* instead of the Axion Estin).
3.  **Weekdays of Lent:** Chrysostom is **forbidden**. The Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts is served instead (an evening Vespers service combined with the distribution of previously consecrated communion).
