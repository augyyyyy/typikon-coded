# Sunday Matins (Matrix Prototype)
## Liturgical Digest

> **When served:** Every Sunday morning.
> **Governing logic:** The structural engine resolves the conflict between the Resurrectional cycle (Octoechos dictates) and the Menaion/Festal cycle. This document governs strictly **Cases 01, 02, 03, 04, and 06** of Dolnytsky Part II.

## II. THE VARIABLE MATRICES (SUNDAY CASES)

### NODE A: Troparia Stack (Following "God is the Lord")
*   **Case 01 (Simple Sunday):** 2x Resurrection Troparion. Glory: Saint Troparion (if it exists). Both Now: Theotokion.
*   **Case 03 (Feast of the Lord):** 2x Resurrection Troparion. Glory/Both Now: Festal Troparion. 
*   **Case 04 (Feast of the Theotokos):** 1x Resurrection Troparion. Glory/Both Now: 2x Festal Troparion.
*   **Case 06 (Polyeleos Saint):** 2x Resurrection Troparion. Glory: Saint. Both Now: Theotokion.

### NODE B: Kathismata & Polyeleos Hooks
*   **Cases 01, 02 (Ordinary Sundays):** 2 Kathismata are read with their respective Sedalens. The **Evlogitaria of the Resurrection** (*"Blessed art thou O Lord..."*) follow immediately.
*   **Cases 03, 04, 06 (Sundays with Great Feasts or Polyeleos):** 2 Kathismata with Sedalens. The **Polyeleos** (Psalms 134/135 *"Praise ye the name of the Lord"*) is sung festively with censing. The Evlogitaria of the Resurrection are sung *after* the Polyeleos.

### NODE C: The Matins Gospel
*   **Cases 01, 02 (Ordinary Sundays):** One of the **11 Resurrection Gospels** (Eothina) is read from the Altar.
*   **Cases 03, 04 (Sundays with Feasts):** The Resurrection Gospel is suppressed. The **Festal Gospel** is read.
*   **Case 06 (Polyeleos Saint):** The Gospel of the Saint is read. The Resurrection Gospel is suppressed.

### NODE D: The Canons (14 Troparia Stack)
*   **Case 01/06 (Typical Sunday Array):** 4 Resurrection (with Heirmoi), 3 Cross-Resurrection, 3 Theotokos, 4 Menaion Saint.
*   **Case 03/04 (Sunday Feasts Array):** Heavily favors the Feast. Usually 8 Octoechos (Resurrection/Cross), 6 Festal.
*   *(Note: The Katavasiae sung after every Ode are drawn from the Festal cycle of the calendar).*

### NODE E: The Praises Stack (Psalms 148-150)
All Sunday Matins sing the Praises festively. 
*   **Cases 01, 02 (Ordinary Sundays):** 8 Stichera. (4 Resurrection, 4 Menaion). Glory: **Gospel Doxastikon (Eothinon)**. Both Now: *"You are most blessed, O Virgin..."*
*   **Cases 03, 04 (Sunday Feasts):** 8 Stichera. (4 Resurrection, 4 Feast). Glory: Feast. Both Now: *"You are most blessed..."*
*   **Case 06 (Polyeleos Saint):** 8 Stichera. (4 Resurrection, 4 Saint). Glory: Saint/Gospel depending on Typikon intersections. Both Now: *"You are most blessed..."*
