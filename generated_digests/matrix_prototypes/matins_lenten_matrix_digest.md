# Lenten Matins (Matrix Prototype)
## Liturgical Digest

> **When served:** Monday through Friday mornings during Great Lent (unless displaced by a Great Feast like the Annunciation).
> **Governing logic:** The Octoechos and Menaion are brutally superseded by the **Triodion**. The structure moves entirely away from the 20 Dolnytsky Cases into the strict Fasting Logic of Part IV. This document governs all weekday Lenten logic.

## II. THE VARIABLE MATRICES (PART IV FASTING RULES)

### NODE A: The Alleluia Hook (Replaces "God is the Lord")
*   **Action:** The Deacon intones **"Alleluia, Alleluia, Alleluia"** 4 times with specific Lenten verses from Isaiah 26.
*   **Troparia Stack:** The Menaion Saints are ignored here. Instead, the choir sings the three **Triadica (Trinity Hymns)** of the weekly Octoechos tone.

### NODE B: Kathismata Expansion
During Great Lent, the Psalter must be read twice a week, massive increasing the daily reading requirement.
*   **Array Requirement:** **Three** full Kathismata are read instead of two.
*   **Sedalen Injection:** After each Kathisma, the Little Litany is suppressed (except after the 3rd). Instead, the Reader chants the specific **Sessional Hymns of the Triodion** appended to that day and tone.

### NODE C: The Biblical Odes Canon
The standard Canon is heavily augmented by the ancient Biblical Canticles.
*   **Logic Rule:** The 9 Biblical Odes (Song of Moses, Song of Hannah, etc.) are chanted in full. This operates on a highly complex rotation depending on the day of the week (e.g. Tuesday features Ode 2, Wednesday features Ode 3). 
*   **Stack Integration:** The troparia of the Triodion and Menaion are woven directly into these Biblical Canticle verses. The Triodion completely dominates the Odes assigned to that specific weekday.

### NODE D: The Praises & Triodion Aposticha Stack
This is where the Lenten structure diverges sharply from all other Matins services.
*   **The Praises (Psalms 148-150):** The Praises are never sung. They are strictly read by the Reader.
*   **Praises Stichera Stack:** **Empty.** No stichera are appended to the Praises.
*   **The Doxology:** Small Doxology (Read).
*   **The Aposticha Stack:** Immediately following the Doxology, the choir sings the massive **Aposticha of the Triodion** for that specific day, ending with a Martyricon/Theotokion.

### NODE E: Prostrations Hook
*   **Litanies Suppressed:** The typical morning litanies (*"Have mercy on us O God", "Let us complete"*) are entirely skipped.
*   **Array Segment:** *"It is good to give thanks to the Lord"* -> Trisagion to Our Father -> Lenten concluding Troparia (*"Standing in the temple of Thy glory"*).
*   **St. Ephrem:** 40 *"Lord Have Mercys"*. Then the Priest recites the **Prayer of St. Ephrem the Syrian** aloud, accompanied by 3 full earth prostrations. The Reader immediately initiates the First Hour.
