# The Hours (Matrix Prototype)
## Liturgical Digest

> **When served:** The Hours punctuate the liturgical day (1st Hour at Dawn, 3rd at mid-morning, 6th at noon, 9th at mid-afternoon). 
> **Governing logic:** The core of the service (Psalms and Litanies) is structurally rigid. The variations rely entirely on the 20 paradigm cases injecting the proper Troparia and Kontakia into the middle of the service, or Part IV fasting logic transforming them into Lenten or Royal Hours.

---

## I. THE INVARIABLE SPINE
This applies to all ordinary Minor Hours (1st, 3rd, 6th, and 9th).

| # | Action | Condition / Fork |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **The Enarxis** | *"Blessed is our God"* (or *"O come let us worship"* if tied continuously to Matins/Liturgy). |
| **2** | **Three Psalms** | Dependent on the specific Hour (e.g., 5, 89, 100 for the 1st Hour). |
| **3** | **[NODE A: Troparia]**| *See Case Matrix below.* |
| **4** | **Theotokion of the Hour** | Fixed rigidly to each hour. |
| **5** | **Trisagion** | "Our Father". |
| **6** | **[NODE B: Kontakion]** | *See Case Matrix below.* |
| **7** | **Conclusion** | 40x *"Lord have mercy"*, Prayer of the Hours, Final prayer of the specific Hour, Dismissal (if 1st or 9th). |

---

## II. THE TROPARIA/KONTAKIA MATRICES (DOLNYTSKY 20 CASES)

### NODE A \& B: The Daily Matrix (Cases 05, 14, 15, 16)
*(Ordinary Weekdays)*
*   **[Node A - Troparia]:** The Troparion of the Menaion Saint. If two saints (e.g. Case 05b), the first saint's troparion is sung, *"Glory"*, then the second saint's troparion, *"Both now"*, Theotokion.
*   **[Node B - Kontakion]:** The Kontakion of the Menaion Saint. If missing, the Kontakion of the Octoechos (Martyricon or Theotokion) depending on the day of the week.

### NODE A \& B: The Sunday Matrix (Cases 01, 02, 06, 08, 11)
*(Sundays without Great Feasts)*
*   **[Node A - Troparia]:** The Resurrection Troparion (Octoechos), *"Glory"*, the Troparion of the Menaion Saint. *"Both now"*, Theotokion of the Hour.
*   **[Node B - Kontakion]:** The Typikon regulates an alternating cycle if multiple Kontakia exist (e.g. at the 1st/6th hour, read the Resurrection Kontakion; at the 3rd/9th hour, read the Saint's Kontakion).

### NODE A \& B: The Festal Matrices (Cases 03, 04, 07, 10, 12, 13)
*(Great Feasts, Apodoses, and their overlaps)*
*   **[Node A - Troparia]:** The Festal Troparion (from the Menaion or Triodion/Pentecostarion). If a Sunday Feast (Case 03/04), Resurrection Troparion -> Glory -> Festal Troparion. 
*   **[Node B - Kontakion]:** On absolute Feasts (Cases 10, 13), *only* the Kontakion of the Feast is read at all hours. On Sunday Feasts (Cases 03/04), an alternating cycle between the Resurrection and Festal Kontakion is employed across the four hours.

## III. THE FASTING MATRIX (PART IV)

If celebrated on a **Strict Fast Day** (Mon-Fri of Great Lent), the Minor Hours become "**Lenten Hours**".
1.  **Node A Override (Lenten Troparia):** The Saint's Troparia are entirely suppressed. Instead, the specific **Troparion of the Hour** (e.g., *"Thou Who at the third hour didst send down Thy Holy Spirit..."*) is sung three times.
2.  **Prostration Injection:** During the singing of the Troparia of the Hour, the clergy and faithful perform full earth prostrations.
3.  **Sixth Hour Node:** At the 6th Hour only, a Reading from the prophecy of Isaiah is inserted after the Theotokion.
4.  **Conclusion Override:** The massive **Prayer of St. Ephrem** is recited at the conclusion of every Hour with full prostrations.

---

## IV. THE ROYAL HOURS MATRIX

Served exclusively on the Eves of Nativity, Theophany, and Great Friday.

1.  **Consecutive Rendering:** The 1st, 3rd, 6th, and 9th Hours are read as a single, massive, continuous block. The clergy are vested. The Gospel is censed on an analogion in the center of the nave.
2.  **Psalm Deviation:** Two of the three ordinary psalms are replaced by specific prophetic psalms closely tied to the feast (e.g., Psalm 21 *"My God, my God"* on Great Friday).
3.  **Stichera Override:** Node A is replaced entirely; specialized hymnic Stichera are sung instead of standard troparia.
4.  **Readings Injection:** After the Stichera, an Old Testament **Prophecy**, an **Epistle**, and a **Gospel** reading are chanted in *every* hour.
5.  **Typika Fusion:** At the conclusion of the 9th Hour, the service bleeds directly into the Typika (Obidnitsia).
