# The Liturgy of the Presanctified Gifts (Matrix Prototype)
## Liturgical Digest

> **When served:** Wednesdays and Fridays of Great Lent, Thursday of the 5th Week (Great Canon), and Monday-Wednesday of Holy Week. A variant is served on the Feast of the Annunciation if it falls on these days.
> **Governing logic:** It relies strictly on Part IV logic. Structurally, it is the fusing of Lenten Great Vespers with a communion service that distributes the Eucharist consecrated on the previous Sunday. Because there is no Anaphora (Eucharistic Prayer), it is technically a Vespers service, not a true Liturgy.

## II. THE ANOMALY MATRIX (PART IV OVERRIDES)

### NODE A: Kathisma & Transfer of the Lamb
The Eucharist is moved from the Altar to the Prothesis Table during the singing of the Psalter.
*   **Action:** The **18th Kathisma** (Psalms 119-133) is read exclusively, divided into three "Stases".
*   **Stasis 1:** Priest places the Presanctified Lamb on the Diskos.
*   **Stasis 2:** Priest censes the Lamb.
*   **Stasis 3:** Priest carries the Lamb to the Prothesis table in quiet solemnity.
*   *Note: Between each Stasis, a Small Litany is intoned.*

### NODE B: Lord I Have Cried Stack (10 Stichera Base)
Because it functions as the Great Vespers of a fasting day, it features a heavy 10-stichera stack.
*   **Standard Lenten Stack (Wed/Fri):** Usually `6 Triodion (Day) + 4 Menaion (Saint)`. Glory: Saint (if present). Both Now: Theotokion.
*   **Holy Week Hook:** The Menaion is entirely suppressed. `10 Triodion`. 

### NODE C: The Readings Hook
*   **Prokeimenon 1:** Sung.
*   **First Paremiya:** Reading from Genesis.
*   **Prokeimenon 2:** Sung.
*   **"The Light of Christ":** The Priest turns to the faithful with a lit candle and censer, exclaiming: **"The Light of Christ illumines all!"** The congregation performs a full earth prostration.
*   **Second Paremiya:** Reading from Proverbs.
*   ***"Let My Prayer Arise"***: The choir sings Psalm 140 (*"Let my prayer arise in Thy sight as incense..."*) kneeling, while the Priest censes the Holy Table.

## III. THE COMMUNION SPINE (PART 2)

Once Vespers concludes, the service hooks immediately into the communion rites.

| # | Action | Condition / Fork |
|---|---|---|
| **9** | **St. Ephrem** | The Prayer of St. Ephrem the Syrian (3 prostrations). |
| **10** | **Litanies** | Fervent Supplication, Catechumens, and (after Mid-Lent) *For those preparing for Holy Illumination*. |
| **11** | **[NODE D: The Great Entrance]** | *See Fasting Matrix below.* |
| **12** | **"Our Father"** | Sung. |
| **13** | **Holy Things** | Priest exclaims: *"Presanctified Holy Things for the Holy!"* |
| **14** | **[NODE E: Communion Hymn]** | *See Fasting Matrix below.* |
| **15** | **Conclusion** | Post-Communion Prayers, Ambon Prayer, Dismissal. |

### NODE D: The Great Entrance (Now the Powers of Heaven)
*   **Cherubic Hymn Suppressed:** The standard Cherubic hymn is replaced entirely by the Lenten hymn: **"Now the Powers of Heaven do serve invisibly with us..."**
*   **Absolute Silence:** Because the bread and wine are *already* the Body and Blood of Christ, no prayers of intercession are spoken during the procession. The clergy process in total, terrifying silence while the congregation performs full face-to-the-earth prostrations.

### NODE E: The Koinonikon (Communion Hymn)
*   **Standard Lenten Friday/Wednesday:** Sings *"O Taste and See that the Lord is good. Alleluia."*
*   *(Note: Rather than singing "We have seen the True Light", the choir sings "I will bless the Lord at all times" after communion).*
