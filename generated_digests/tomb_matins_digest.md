# Great Saturday Matins (Tomb Matins)
## Matins with the Encomia (Praises) at the Blameless
*(Ukrainian: Утреня Великої Суботи з Похвалами)*

---

> **When served:** Friday evening — *"This Matins we sing usually on Friday evening, after sunset, although the rubrics prescribe it at the 7th hour of the night"* (Part IV, line 689).

> **Governing source:** Dolnytsky Part IV, lines 687–704.

---

## PHYSICAL PREPARATION

> **Part IV, line 690:** *"The sacred ministers remain during Matins before the Tomb of God, where seats and a table with candlesticks and books are prepared for them. Sitting, they sing Matins from there."*

The Tomb of God (placed at Great Friday Vespers — line 655) stands before the Holy Doors with the Shroud attached to it. The sacred ministers serve the first part of Matins **seated** at the Tomb.

---

## VESTMENTS

> **Part IV, line 690:** *"…the priests — in epitrachelia and phelonia, and the deacons — in dalmatics of violet color."*

**Violet** throughout the first part of Matins (Passion Week). At the Entrance (line 702), the priest vests in **all sacred vestments of his rank** — this is the full hierarchical vesting for the Gospel/readings block.

## SERVICE ORDER

---

### I. OPENING RITE — PREPARATORY BLOCK

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 692):** *"From 'Blessed is our God' to 'O come, let us worship' exclusively everything — as usual at Matins."*
> **Import chain:** Line 692 → Part I, line 143.

This opening follows the standard non-Vigil Matins opening from Part I, line 143 — identical to Bridegroom Matins and Passion Matins, with one modification noted in Phase E below.

#### Phase A — Priestly Blessing (steps 1–8)

The priest is seated before the Tomb of God. He rises to begin.

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Priest** | **Vests in epitrachelion** — blesses and kisses it at the top where it has a cross (Part I, line 143). |
| 2 | **Priest** | **Exits** through the **northern door** to the front of the **closed** Holy Doors. |
| 3 | **Priest** | **Bows low.** Exclaims: **"Blessed is our God, always, now and ever and unto ages of ages."** |
| 4 | **Choir** | *"Amen."* |
| 5 | **Choir** | *"Glory to Thee, our God, glory to Thee."* |
| 6 | **Choir** | *"O Heavenly King…"* |
| 7 | **Choir** | **Trisagion** through **Our Father.** *(First Trisagion-Our Father cycle.)* |
| 8 | **Priest** | Exclaims: **"For Thine is the kingdom…"** *(First exclamation.)* |

#### Phase B — Choir Response (steps 9–11)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | **Choir** | *"Amen."* |
| 10 | **Choir** | *"Lord, have mercy"* (×12). |
| 11 | | *(No "O come, let us worship" — see Phase E.)* |

#### Phase C — Censing + Two Psalms (steps 12–18)

The priest enters the sanctuary and censes. The Two Psalms are read **simultaneously** with the censing (Part I, line 143).

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 12 | **Priest** | **Bows low.** Enters through the **southern door** into the sanctuary. |
| 13 | **Priest** | Stands before the **steps of the Holy Table.** **Bows low** to the Holy Mysteries. |

| 17 | **Priest** | **Censes** the iconostasis, choirs, and people, **according to custom.** |
| 18 | **Choirs** | *(Simultaneously with steps 15–17)* Recite the **Two Psalms** (Pss. 19 and 20). |

#### Phase D — Troparia, Second Trisagion, Second Exclamation (steps 19–23)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 19 | **Choir** | **Troparia** *(after the Two Psalms — not named in the primary source).* |
| 20 | **Choir** | **Trisagion** through **Our Father.** *(Second Trisagion-Our Father cycle.)* |
| 21 | **Priest** | **Returns** to stand before the Holy Doors. **Bows low.** |

| 23 | **Priest** | Exclaims: **"For Thine is the kingdom."** *(Second exclamation.)* |

#### Phase E — Modification: Skip "O Come, Let Us Worship" (steps 24–26)

> **Part IV, line 692:** *"…after 'Lord, have mercy' (12), without 'O come, let us worship' the priest immediately: 'Glory to the Holy' and the Six Psalms."*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 24 | **Priest** | Enters through the **southern door** into the sanctuary. **Puts away the censer.** |
| 25 | **Priest** | **Exits** through the **northern door** to the front of the iconostasis. **Bows low.** |
| 26 | **Priest** | Upon conclusion of the troparia, recites the **litany for the Sovereign.** After its exclamation → proceeds **immediately** to "Glory to the Holy" (line 692). **"O come, let us worship" is skipped.** |

> [!NOTE]
> This is a unique modification for Tomb Matins: after "Lord, have mercy" (12), "O come, let us worship" is **not** said. The priest proceeds directly from the litany exclamation to "Glory to the Holy" (line 692).

### IV. 17th KATHISMA ("THE BLAMELESS") WITH THE ENCOMIA — THREE STATIONS

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 694):** *"17th Kathisma ('The Blameless') in three stations with the Encomia (Praises), with censing at the beginning of each station and a small litany after each station."*

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 695):** *"Regarding the censing, we suppose that it is sufficient to cense only the Shroud (together with the Holy Mysteries, if they be exposed on the tomb), the choirs and the people."*

> **Primary Source (Part IV, line 696):** *"About the Encomia (Praises), in Greek Triodia called Engkomia (praise), let it be known that before each of them one verse of the Kathisma is sung, until all are finished; only the first troparion of each station (in the editions of Pochayiv Triodia also the second troparion of the second station) is sung without a verse both at the beginning and at the end of each station, with the exception of the third, after which the first troparion is not sung and the small litany is not recited, but immediately — 'The choir of angels' and, after the singing of it, — small litany."*

This is the heart of Tomb Matins — the Encomia at the Blameless. The 17th Kathisma (Psalm 118) is divided into three stations. At each station, the **verses of the psalm** alternate with the **Encomia troparia** from the Triodion.

#### Station I — First Station of the Blameless

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Priest** | **Censes** the Shroud (together with the Holy Mysteries, if exposed on the Tomb), the choirs, and the people (lines 694–695). |
| 2 | **Choir** | Sings the **first troparion** of Station I **without a verse** — at the beginning (line 696: *"the first troparion of each station… is sung without a verse… at the beginning"*). |
| 3 | **Choir** | Alternates: one **verse of the Kathisma** is sung, then one **Encomion (troparion)**, until all are finished (line 696: *"before each of them one verse of the Kathisma is sung, until all are finished"*). |
| 4 | **Choir** | At the end: the **first troparion** of Station I is sung again **without a verse** (line 696: *"…and at the end of each station"*). |
| 5 | **Priest** | Sings the **Small Litany** (line 694: *"a small litany after each station"*). |
| 6 | **Choir** | Responds to each petition. |
| 7 | **Priest** | Exclamation. |

#### Station II — Second Station of the Blameless

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | **Priest** | **Censes** the Shroud, choirs, and people (line 694). |
| 9 | **Choir** | Sings the **first troparion** of Station II **without a verse** — at the beginning (line 696). |
| 10 | **Choir** | Alternates: verse of the Kathisma + Encomion, until all are finished. |
| 11 | **Choir** | At the end: the **first troparion** of Station II is sung again **without a verse** (line 696). |
| 12 | **Priest** | Sings the **Small Litany.** Exclamation. |

> [!NOTE]
> **Pochayiv edition variant (line 696):** *"In the editions of Pochayiv Triodia also the second troparion of the second station is sung without a verse."*
