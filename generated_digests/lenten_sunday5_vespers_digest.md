# Sunday Evening Lenten Vespers — 5th Sunday of Great Lent
## Opening the Monday of the 6th Week of the Great Fast
**Authority: Dolnytsky Typikon Part IV (Triodion)**

---

> **What this service is:**
> The Daily (Lenten) Vespers sung on **Sunday afternoon/evening** of the 5th week, which liturgically *opens Monday March 23, 2026*. This is entirely distinct from the Great Vespers of the All-Night Vigil served the previous Saturday evening (which opened Sunday). The Menaion saint is therefore for March 23 (the day being opened), not March 22.

> **Governing source chain:**
> - Part IV, line 498: *"Everything according to the rubric of the 2nd Sunday."*
> - 2nd Sunday Evening Vespers rubric = **Part IV, lines 175–189** (unified template for all five Lenten Sunday evenings).
> - 5th Sunday is an **ODD Sunday** → Entry made, Prokeimenon "Thou hast given an inheritance."

## SERVICE ORDER

### I. OPENING RITE

| # | Element | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Blessing** | Priest: *"Blessed is our God, always, now and ever, and unto ages of ages."* |
| 2 | **"Come, let us worship"** (3×) | Reader, with small bows |
| 3 | **Psalm 103** *(Prooimiac Psalm)* | Sung by chanters. Priest reads the Prayers of Vespers silently before closed Holy Doors. |
| 4 | **Great Litany** *(Ectenia Velika)* | Deacon or priest from before the Holy Doors |

---

### II. KATHISMA — **OMITTED**

> **Primary Source, line 180:** *"No Kathisma."*
> Entirely absent at Sunday Evening Lenten Vespers — a rule tracing to the former practice of a weekly All-Night Vigil.

---

### III. "LORD, I HAVE CRIED" *(Kyrie Ekekraxa)*

**Psalms sung:** 140, 141, 129, 116 — with stichera on the final verses.

**Censing:** Priest censes the altar, iconostasis, chanters, and people during the stichera.

#### Stichera Distribution — **Total: 10** (Odd Sunday rule)

> **Primary Source, line 181:** *"10 stichera: 4 Penitential of the Octoechos of the current tone, 2 given at the Aposticha of Vespers and 2 at the Aposticha of Matins (which are taken from the Octoechos and given at the end of the Triodion), 3 of the Triodion and 3 of the Menaion of the saint."*

| # | Count | Source | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | **2** | Octoechos **Tone 8** — Sunday Vespers Aposticha | 2 penitential stichera of Tone 8 from Sunday Vespers Aposticha section |
| 3–4 | **2** | Octoechos **Tone 8** — Monday Matins Aposticha | 2 penitential stichera of Tone 8 from the Monday Matins Aposticha section (printed at end of Triodion) |
| 5–7 | **3** | Triodion | 3 idiomela assigned to the 5th Sunday evening |
| 8–10 | **3** | Menaion — **Martyr Nicon** | 3 stichera for Martyr Nicon and his disciples from the Menaion for March 23 |

> **If Mary of Egypt's service is taken:** Her stichera displace or combine with Martyr Nicon's per the Two-Saints Rule (line 182): one saint at Vespers (3 stichera), the other at Matins Praises (4 stichera).

#### Glory / Both Now

| Element | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Glory…** | *No Doxastikon* — Martyr Nicon is rank [4 NO]; no Glory sticheron is given | — |
| **Both Now…** | **Theotokion from the Menaion** (March 23) | Menaion |

> Per line 181: *"if none [Doxastikon], then Glory, Both now: Theotokion from the Menaion."* Since [4 NO] saints do not have a Doxastikon, this rule applies.

### IV. ENTRY WITH THURIBLE *(5th Sunday = Odd → Entry IS made)*

> **Primary Source, line 184:** *"Entrance, on account of the Great Prokeimenon."*

Priest vests in phelonion. Deacon carries thurible. Entry prayer, blessing of the Entry, deacon: *"Wisdom! Upright!"* Priest and deacon enter sanctuary.

**"O Gladsome Light"** *(Svite Tykhy / Phos Hilaron)* is sung as the Entry is completed.

---

### V. GREAT PROKEIMENON — "THOU HAST GIVEN AN INHERITANCE"

> **Primary Source, line 185:** *"Instead of the Prokeimenon of the day we sing the Great Prokeimenon of the Triodion (on the 1st, 3rd and 5th: 'Thou hast given an inheritance'; on the 2nd and 4th: 'God is our refuge')."*

**Great Prokeimenon, Tone 4:**

> *"Thou hast given an inheritance to those who fear Thy name."*
> *(Psalm 60/61:6)*

**Verses** (sung antiphonally, prokeimenon repeated after each):
1. *"Hear my cry, O God; attend unto my prayer."* (Ps. 60:1)
2. *"From the end of the earth have I cried unto Thee, when my heart was overwhelmed."* (Ps. 60:2)
3. *"I will dwell in Thy tabernacle forever; I will trust in the covert of Thy wings."* (Ps. 60:4)
4. Final full repetition of the prokeimenon.

---

### VI. POST-PROKEIMENON — PRIEST DIVESTS

> **Primary Source, line 186:** *"After the Prokeimenon there will be no litany; the priest takes off the phelonion and closes the holy doors."*

- No litany after the Prokeimenon
- Priest removes phelonion → service transitions into Lenten dismissal sequence
- Holy Doors are closed

---

### VII. "VOUCHSAFE, O LORD" *(Udostoi, Hospodi)*

Read by the priest from before the closed Holy Doors.

**→ 3 small prostrations (metaniae)** made immediately after.
*Source: line 179 + line 186*

---

### VIII. LITANY OF COMPLETION

*"Let us complete our evening prayer unto the Lord…"*
Priest exclaims: *"For Thou art good and lovest mankind…"*

---

### IX. APOSTICHA

> **Primary Source, line 187:** *"Aposticha of the Triodion with the usual verses of the Horologion."*

The Aposticha stichera come entirely from the **Triodion** for the 5th Sunday evening.

**Verses (from Horologion):**
- Verse 1: *"To Thee, O Lord, I have lifted up my soul."* (Ps. 24:1)
- Verse 2: *"Look upon my humility and my toil and forgive all my sins."* (Ps. 24:18)

| Element | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Sticheron 1** (with verse) | Triodion idiomelon for 5th Sunday evening | Triodion |
| **Sticheron 2** (with verse) | Triodion sticheron | Triodion |
| **Glory…** | *No Doxastikon* — Martyr Nicon [4 NO] has none | — |
| **Both Now…** | Theotokion from the Menaion (March 23) | Menaion |

> *"if there be Glory: of the saint, then the Theotokion will be the usual one, in the tone of the Doxastikon."* (line 187) — Since no Glory, Theotokion from the Menaion is used instead.

---

### X. "NOW LETTEST THOU DEPART" *(Nunc Dimittis)*

The Canticle of Simeon (Luke 2:29–32), chanted or read.

---

### XI. TRISAGION

*"Holy God, Holy Mighty, Holy Immortal, have mercy on us."* (×3)

*"Glory… Both Now… All-Holy Trinity… Lord have mercy (×3)… Our Father…"*
Priest: *"For Thine is the kingdom…"*

**→ 3 small prostrations (metaniae)** are made at the Trisagion.
*Source: line 179*

---

### XII. LENTEN TROPARIA — WITH GREAT PROSTRATIONS

> **Primary Source, line 188:** *"We sing 3 troparia with prostrations, that is 'Virgin Theotokos' and a prostration; Glory: 'O Baptizer of Christ' and a prostration; Both now: 'Intercede for us' and a prostration; and then 'Beneath thy tender mercy' without a prostration."*

| Glory… | *"O Baptizer of Christ, remember us all who venerate thee with love…"* | **→ 1 great prostration** |
| Both Now… | *"Intercede for us, O holy apostles and all saints, that we may be saved…"* | **→ 1 great prostration** |
| — | *"Beneath thy tender mercy we flee for refuge, O Theotokos…"* | No prostration |

---

### XIII. "LORD HAVE MERCY" (×40)

Read by the reader. *(Psalm 33 is omitted — see line 189)*

---

### XIV. PRAYER OF ST. EPHREM THE SYRIAN *(Reduced Sunday Form)*

> **Primary Source, line 189:** *"Only up to the end of the 3rd great prostration of the prayer of St. Ephrem, we do not make other small prostrations."*

On Sunday evenings only **3 great prostrations** are made — NOT the full weekday sequence of 3 great + 12 small + 1 great:

| Part | Text | Prostration |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | *"O Lord and Master of my life, a spirit of idleness, despondency, ambition, and idle talking give me not."* | **→ 1 great prostration** |
| 2nd | *"But a spirit of chastity, humble-mindedness, patience, and love bestow upon me, Thy servant."* | **→ 1 great prostration** |
| 3rd | *"Yea, O Lord and King, grant me to see my own faults, and not to condemn my brother; for blessed art Thou unto ages of ages. Amen."* | **→ 1 great prostration** |

*(The 12 small prostrations and the final full repetition are OMITTED on Sunday evenings)*

---

### XV. DISMISSAL

Priest: *"Wisdom!"* · Choir: *"Bless."*
Priest: *"Glory be to Thee, O Christ our God, our hope, glory to Thee."*
Choir: *"Glory… Both now… Lord have mercy (×3)… Give the blessing."*

**Dismissal formula:**
> *"Christ our true God, who rose from the dead, through the prayers of His most pure Mother, of our holy fathers, of the holy glorious and all-laudable apostles, of the holy Martyr Nicon and his disciples, and of all the saints, have mercy on us and save us, for He is good and loves mankind."*

*(Psalm 33 is **omitted** on Sunday Lenten evenings, per line 189)*

## COMPLINE THAT FOLLOWS

> **Primary Source, line 191:** *"We sing Small Compline with the Lenten prostrations, that is 3 small prostrations at 'Glory to God in the highest', at the Trisagion following 'It is truly meet' and after the prayer of St. Ephrem, and 16 usual ones at the prayer of St. Ephrem. We do not omit the Canon of the Theotokos, since there was no All-Night Vigil."*

| Component | Content |
|---|---|
| Form | **Small Compline** (not Great Compline) |
| Canon of Theotokos | **Included** (no vigil was served tonight) |
| Canon of Triodion | Triodion three-ode canon for this day |
| After Trisagion | Kondakion of the Triodion **only** |
| Prostrations at St. Ephrem | Full **16**: 4 great + 12 small (Compline retains full count) |
