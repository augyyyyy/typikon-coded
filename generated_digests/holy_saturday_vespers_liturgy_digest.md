# Holy Saturday Vespers with the Liturgy of St. Basil the Great
## Great Vespers Combined with the Divine Liturgy
*(Ukrainian: Вечірня з Літургією св. Василія Великого)*

---

> **When served:** Great Saturday morning — *"According to the custom of the archcathedral church, this Vespers begins at 9 o'clock in the day"* (Part IV, line 711).

> **Governing source:** Dolnytsky Part IV, lines 710–715, importing from Part III, lines 566–581 (Vespers with the Service of Basil on the Eve of the Nativity of Christ).

> **Import chain:** Part IV, line 713 → Part III, lines 566–581 (Nativity Eve Vespers with Basil), with specific modifications for Holy Saturday.

---

## NOTES

> **Part IV, line 712:** *"On this day there will be only one Vespers, that is Great with the Liturgy of St. Basil and without All-Night Vigil, and after the Prayer behind the Ambo, or (as the rubrics prescribe) after the dismissal of the Liturgy, there will be the Blessing of Loaves, as usual at the All-Night Vigil."*

> **Part IV, line 715:** *"Throughout the whole Bright Week, beginning from the Vespers of this day, the Resurrection service is sung and, besides the stichera of the Triodion, are sung also the Resurrection stichera of the Octoechos, each day — in a different tone, that is on Sunday — in Tone 1, on Monday — in Tone 2, on Tuesday — in Tone 3, on Wednesday — in Tone 4, on Thursday — in Tone 5, on Friday — in Tone 6, on Saturday — in Tone 8, omitting Tone 7."*

---

## VESTMENTS

> **Part IV, line 714:** *"…from the beginning of Vespers we use violet vestments, and afterwards change them after the conclusion of the Apostle, at the verses instead of 'Alleluia,' in order to read the Gospel already in bright vestments."*

| Phase | Vestment Color | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Opening → through Apostle reading | **Violet** | Start of Vespers (line 714) |
| "Alleluia" verses → end of Liturgy | **Bright** (white/gold) | After conclusion of Apostle, at the verses instead of "Alleluia" (line 714) |

---

## SERVICE ORDER

---

### I. PROTHESIS (PROSKOMEDIA) + OPENING

> **Import chain:** Part IV, line 713 → Part III, line 568.
> **Part III, line 568:** *"From the beginning to 'Blessed is the Kingdom' everything is exactly as usual at the Service without Vespers."*

The Proskomedia is completed at the Table of Oblation **before** the public service begins, as at any Divine Liturgy.

#### Phase A — "Blessed is the Kingdom" (steps 1–5)

> **Part III, line 569:** *"After 'Blessed is the Kingdom,' immediately after 'Amen' — 'O Heavenly King' and the rest with the Trisagion, as usual, up to 'O come, let us worship' inclusive."*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Priest** | **Exclaims:** *"Blessed is the Kingdom of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit, now and ever and unto ages of ages."* (Part III, line 569). |
| 2 | **Choir** | *"Amen."* |
| 3 | **Choir** | *"O Heavenly King…"* |
| 4 | **Choir** | **Trisagion** through **Our Father** — as usual (Part III, line 569). |
| 5 | **Choir** | *"O come, let us worship"* (×3) (Part III, line 569). |

> [!NOTE]
> This opening differs from an ordinary Vespers opening (which begins "Blessed is our God"). Because Vespers is combined with the Liturgy, it begins with the Liturgy exclamation *"Blessed is the Kingdom"* (Part III, line 569).

---

### II. INTRODUCTORY PSALM + GREAT LITANY

> **Part III, line 570:** *"At the introductory Psalm the priest, standing near the Holy Table, reads with uncovered head the usual evening prayers and afterwards from there exclaims the litany 'In peace let us pray to the Lord.' If there be also a deacon, then he goes out at this litany before the Holy Doors."*

#### Phase A — Introductory Psalm with Evening Prayers (steps 1–2)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Choir** | Sings the **Introductory Psalm** (Ps. 103) (Part III, line 570). |
| 2 | **Priest** | *(During the Introductory Psalm)* Standing near the **Holy Table**, reads with **uncovered head** the usual **evening prayers** (Part III, line 570). |

#### Phase B — Great Litany (steps 3–5)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 3 | **Priest** (or Deacon) | Exclaims the Great Litany: *"In peace let us pray to the Lord…"* (Part III, line 570). |
| 4 | **Choir** | Responds *"Lord, have mercy"* to each petition. |
| 5 | **Priest** | Exclamation: *"For to Thee belongs…"* |

> [!NOTE]
> If a deacon is present, he goes out before the Holy Doors for this litany (Part III, line 570).

---

### III. "LORD, I HAVE CRIED" + STICHERA + ENTRANCE WITH GOSPEL

> **Part IV, line 713:** *"…the same as the Vespers on the Eve of the Nativity… without 'Blessed is the man.'"*
> **Part III, line 571:** *"The Kathisma 'Blessed is the man' will not be… nor will there be a Small Litany, but immediately — 'Lord, I have cried' with censing, as usual."*

#### Phase A — "Lord, I Have Cried" with Censing (steps 1–4)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Choir** | Sings *"Lord, I have cried"* according to the tone of the first stichera (Part III, line 571). |
| 2 | **Priest / Deacons** | **Cense** as usual during *"Lord, I have cried"* (Part III, line 571: *"with censing, as usual"*). |
| 3 | **Choir** | **Stichera** — on 8, Glory, Both now: from the Irmologion (Part III, line 572: *"8 stichera, Glory, Both now: Stichera from the Irmologion"*). |

> [!NOTE]
> No Kathisma "Blessed is the man" and no Small Litany — this is explicitly stated in both line 713 (*"without 'Blessed is the man'"*) and the imported Part III, line 571.

#### Phase B — Entrance with the Gospel (steps 4–8)

> **Part III, line 573:** *"Entrance with the Gospel, with the usual prayer of the Entrance. If there be deacons, then the first of them, who is to the right of the priest, carries the Gospel, and the second goes before him with the censer; and they do in this everything as at the Entrance with the Gospel at the Liturgy."*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | **Priest** | Says the usual **Prayer of the Entrance** (Part III, line 573). |
| 5 | **First Deacon** | (If present) **Carries the Gospel** — positioned to the **right** of the priest (Part III, line 573). |
| 6 | **Second Deacon** | (If present) Goes **before** the first deacon **with the censer** (Part III, line 573). |
| 7 | **All** | **Make the Entrance** — *"as at the Entrance with the Gospel at the Liturgy"* (Part III, line 573). |
| 8 | **First Deacon** (or Priest) | Exclaims: **"Wisdom! Upright!"** |

---

### IV. "O GLADSOME LIGHT" — MODIFICATION FOR HOLY SATURDAY

> **Part IV, line 713:** *"…only here, after 'O Gladsome Light,' there will be no Prokeimenon, but immediately 15 readings."*

> **Part III, line 574:** At the Nativity Eve, after "O Gladsome Light" there is a **Prokeimenon of the day** and then the readings. On Holy Saturday, this Prokeimenon is **suppressed** — the 15 readings begin immediately.

#### Phase A — "O Gladsome Light" (steps 1–3)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Choir** | Sings *"O Gladsome Light"* (Part III, line 574, imported via Part IV, line 713). |
| 2 | | **No Prokeimenon** after *"O Gladsome Light"* (Part IV, line 713: *"there will be no Prokeimenon"*). |
| 3 | **Priest** | *(After "O Gladsome Light")* **Withdraws** behind the Holy Table and sits during the readings (Part III, line 574: *"the priest withdraws behind the Holy Table and sits there during the readings in his place"*). |

> [!NOTE]
> The Nativity Eve Vespers has a Prokeimenon of the day here (Part III, line 574). Holy Saturday **suppresses** it — the readings begin immediately after "O Gladsome Light" (Part IV, line 713).

---

### V. THE 15 OLD TESTAMENT READINGS

> **Part IV, line 713:** *"…immediately 15 readings, between which there will be no troparia, and at the 6th reading all verses of the Song of Moses are read with the refrain 'For gloriously has He been glorified,' and at the 15th reading the verses of the Song of the Three Youths are read with the refrain 'Praise the Lord and exalt Him above all for ever.'"*

> **Contrast with Nativity Eve (Part III, line 575):** At the Nativity Eve Vespers, between the readings there are **troparia with their verses** after the first three and second three readings. On Holy Saturday, there are **no troparia** between the readings.

#### Phase A — Readings 1–5 (without troparia)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Deacons** | (If present, from the Holy Doors) Exclaim *"Wisdom"* and *"Let us attend"* before each reading (Part III, line 574). |
| 2 | **Reader** | Reads **Reading 1** (of the Prophecy). |
| 3 | **Reader** | Reads **Reading 2.** |
| 4 | **Reader** | Reads **Reading 3.** |
| 5 | **Reader** | Reads **Reading 4.** |
| 6 | **Reader** | Reads **Reading 5.** |

> [!NOTE]
> No troparia between any readings (Part IV, line 713: *"between which there will be no troparia"*). This is a key difference from the Nativity Eve structure.

#### Phase B — Reading 6: Song of Moses (steps 7–8)

> **Part IV, line 713:** *"…at the 6th reading all verses of the Song of Moses are read with the refrain 'For gloriously has He been glorified.'"*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | **Reader** | Reads **Reading 6** — with the **Song of Moses.** |
| 8 | **Choir** | After each verse of the Song of Moses, sings the refrain: **"For gloriously has He been glorified"** (Part IV, line 713). |

#### Phase C — Readings 7–14 (without troparia)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | **Reader** | Reads **Reading 7.** |
| 10 | **Reader** | Reads **Reading 8.** |
| 11 | **Reader** | Reads **Reading 9.** |
| 12 | **Reader** | Reads **Reading 10.** |
| 13 | **Reader** | Reads **Reading 11.** |
| 14 | **Reader** | Reads **Reading 12.** |
| 15 | **Reader** | Reads **Reading 13.** |
| 16 | **Reader** | Reads **Reading 14.** |

#### Phase D — Reading 15: Song of the Three Youths (steps 17–18)

> **Part IV, line 713:** *"…and at the 15th reading the verses of the Song of the Three Youths are read with the refrain 'Praise the Lord and exalt Him above all for ever.'"*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 17 | **Reader** | Reads **Reading 15** — with the **Song of the Three Youths.** |
| 18 | **Choir** | After each verse, sings the refrain: **"Praise the Lord and exalt Him above all for ever"** (Part IV, line 713). |

### VI. SMALL LITANY + TRISAGION (TRANSITION TO LITURGY)

> **Import chain:** Part IV, line 713 → Part III, line 576.
> **Part III, line 576:** *"At the end of the readings — Small Litany (which the deacon, if there be one, sings before the Holy Doors) with the exclamation of the Trisagion and immediately the Trisagion; and further according to the order of the Liturgy of Basil."*

#### Phase A — Small Litany (steps 1–3)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Priest** | *(After the 15th reading)* Returns before the **steps of the Holy Table** (Part III, line 576). |
| 2 | **Deacon** (or Priest) | Sings the **Small Litany** — from before the Holy Doors, if a deacon (Part III, line 576). |
| 3 | **Priest** | Exclamation of the **Trisagion** (Part III, line 576: *"the exclamation of the Trisagion"*). |

#### Phase B — Trisagion + Liturgy of Basil begins (steps 4–5)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | **Choir** | Sings the **Trisagion** — immediately after the exclamation (Part III, line 576: *"immediately the Trisagion"*). |
| 5 | | **The Liturgy of St. Basil the Great continues** from here according to its usual order (Part III, line 576: *"further according to the order of the Liturgy of Basil"*). |

---

### VII. APOSTLE READING + VESTMENT CHANGE

> **Part IV, line 714:** *"…from the beginning of Vespers we use violet vestments, and afterwards change them after the conclusion of the Apostle, at the verses instead of 'Alleluia,' in order to read the Gospel already in bright vestments."*

#### Phase A — Apostle (steps 1–3)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | | **Prokeimenon** of the day. |
| 2 | **Reader** | **Apostle reading** (in violet vestments — Part IV, line 714). |
| 3 | | **Conclusion of the Apostle.** |

#### Phase B — Vestment Change: Violet → Bright (steps 4–6)

> **Part IV, line 713:** *"Instead of 'Alleluia' we sing the last verse of the 81st Psalm with its other verses, in the form of a Prokeimenon."*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | **Choir** | Instead of **"Alleluia"**: sings the **last verse of the 81st Psalm** with its other verses, **in the form of a Prokeimenon** (Part IV, line 713). |
| 5 | **Sacred Ministers** | *(During the singing of the verses of Ps. 81)* **Change** from **violet vestments** to **bright vestments** (Part IV, line 714: *"change them after the conclusion of the Apostle, at the verses instead of 'Alleluia'"*). |
| 6 | | *(The vestment change is complete.)* All subsequent actions are in **bright vestments.** |

> [!NOTE]
> This is one of the most dramatic moments in the entire liturgical year: the transition from the liturgical mourning of Holy Week (violet) to the joy of Pascha (bright). The special Psalm 81 verses replace the usual "Alleluia" to provide time for the vestment change and to mark the liturgical threshold. Line 714 explicitly states the reason: *"in order to read the Gospel already in bright vestments."*

---

### VIII. GOSPEL + CONTINUATION OF LITURGY

#### Phase A — Gospel (steps 1–4)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **First Deacon** | Exclaims: **"Wisdom. Aright! Let us hear the Holy Gospel."** |
| 2 | **Priest** | *"The Reading from the Holy Gospel according to…"* — **in bright vestments** (Part IV, line 714). |
| 3 | **Second Deacon** | *"Let us attend."* |
| 4 | **Priest** | **Reads the Gospel** — in bright vestments. |

> [!NOTE]
> The specific Apostle and Gospel readings for Holy Saturday are not named in lines 710–715. The deacon exclamation sequence is imported from the general structure of the Nativity Eve Vespers (Part III, line 573, lines 576–577). The Gospel exclamation form follows that of Part III, line 592.

#### Phase B — Liturgy of St. Basil continues (steps 5–8)

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | | The Liturgy of St. Basil the Great continues in its usual order — **Litanies, Cherubic Hymn, Great Entrance, Anaphora, Communion**, etc. |
| 6 | **Choir** | Instead of *"It is truly meet"*: sings **"In thee rejoiceth"** or the irmos of the 9th ode (Part III, line 578: *"Instead of 'It is truly meet' we sing 'In thee rejoiceth' or, according to the later rule of our service books, the irmos of the 9th ode of the canon given for Compline of the Eve"*). |
| 7 | | **Communion Hymn:** *"Praise the Lord"* (Part III, line 579). |
| 8 | | **Prayer behind the Ambo** (Part III, line 580). |

---

### IX. BLESSING OF LOAVES

> **Part IV, line 712:** *"…after the Prayer behind the Ambo, or (as the rubrics prescribe) after the dismissal of the Liturgy, there will be the Blessing of Loaves, as usual at the All-Night Vigil."*

| # | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | | *(After the Prayer behind the Ambo, or after the Dismissal)* **Blessing of Loaves** — as usual at the All-Night Vigil (Part IV, line 712). |

> [!NOTE]
> The Blessing of Loaves normally occurs at Vespers with the All-Night Vigil (Part I, lines 36–39), but here there is no All-Night Vigil — line 712 explicitly states the Vespers is *"without All-Night Vigil."* The Blessing of Loaves is placed after the Prayer behind the Ambo (or after the Dismissal, per the rubrics) as a special provision.

## KEY MODIFICATIONS: HOLY SATURDAY VS. NATIVITY EVE

The following table summarizes where Part IV, line 713 explicitly **modifies** the imported Nativity Eve structure:

| Element | Nativity Eve (Part III) | Holy Saturday (Part IV, line 713) |
|---|---|---|
| "Blessed is the man" | Not taken (line 571) | Not taken — confirmed by line 713 (*"without 'Blessed is the man'"*) |
| Prokeimenon after "O Gladsome Light" | Prokeimenon of the day (line 574) | **No Prokeimenon** — immediately 15 readings |
| Number of readings | 8 (line 575) | **15** |
| Troparia between readings | After first three and second three (line 575) | **No troparia** between any readings |
| Song of Moses refrain (6th reading) | *(not applicable — only 8 readings)* | *"For gloriously has He been glorified"* |
| Song of Three Youths (15th reading) | *(not applicable)* | *"Praise the Lord and exalt Him above all for ever"* |
| "Alleluia" / Prokeimenon | Standard "Alleluia" | Last verse of **81st Psalm** with its verses, in the form of a Prokeimenon |
| Vestment change | *(not specified)* | **Violet → Bright** after Apostle, during Ps. 81 verses |
| Dismissal | Of the Feast | **Of Matins of Saturday** |
