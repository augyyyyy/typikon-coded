# TYPICON: SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 1st, 2026.

FOREFEAST OF THE MEETING - TONE I.  
Sunday service combined with that of the Triodion, and that of the forefeast.  
The service to Martyr Tryphon is transferred to the previous Friday at Compline, or another convenient time, whenever the ecclesiarch so wishes.  
Vestment colour: Bright (Gold or blue for the forefeast).  

## GREAT VESPERS

Fasting Rule: Fast-free week, no fasting restrictions.

## SMALL COMPLINE

Canon: Canon Forefeast from the Menaion.  
Troparia: Sunday Troparion from the Octoechos, Troparion of the Temple, *"O God of our fathers..."* and the following three troparia.  

## MIDNIGHT OFFICE (SUNDAY)

Triadic Canon: Canon Trinity in Tone 1.  
Midnight Troparia: Sunday Hypakoe in Tone 1.  
Prayer: Prayer Holy Trinity All Creating.  

## SUNDAY MATINS

*At The Lord is God…* we sing: the Sunday (resurrectional) troparion, twice; Glory, Both now. Troparion of the forefeast, once.  

After the Kathismata (2 & 3): We sing: after the 1st (2): Small Litany, then we sing the resurrectional Sessional Hymns in the tone of the week, from the Octoechos; after the 2nd (3): Small Litany, then we sing the resurrectional Sessional Hymns in the tone of the week, from the Octoechos.  
We sing the Polyeleos, and we add Psalm 136 (By the waters of Babylon).  
Hypakoe: Hypakoe Tone 1.  
Gradual (Hymns of Ascents): Gradual Tone 1.  
Prokeimenon: Arise, Lord, help us, and redeem us for Thy mercy's sake (Tone I).  
Matins Gospel I: (116) Matthew 28:16-20.  
Psalm 50 and Post-Gospel Stichera: Open to me the doors of repentance, Prepare for Me the Ways of Salvation, If I Think Upon the Multitude.  
At the Canon: Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Octoechos - 4 (including the heirmos), Octoechos - 2, Triodion - 4, Menaion - 4.  

**Katavasia:** "Seasonal Katavasia" (Tone IV).  
At Ode 8 (Glory): "Let us bless the Father, and the Son, and the Holy Spirit, the Lord."  
At Ode IX, we sing the Magnification ('My soul magnifies the Lord...') and the refrains ('More honorable than the Cherubim...').  
Zadostoinyk (Feast Heirmos).  
Exapostilarion: Holy Is the Lord in Tone 1; Gospel Exapostilarion I; Theotokion of Gospel Exapostilarion I.  
At the Praises, we sing 8 Stichera: 8 from the Octoechos; Glory... ; Other: Now and ever....  
Doxology: Sunday Resurrection (Sung).  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:** We sing: Sunday Dismissal Troparion 'Today salvation has come to the world' in Tone I; Glory, Both now: Today Salvation.  
We sing: Glory... Gospel Sticheron I in Tone 5; Both now: Most Blessed Art Thou.  

## HOURS

**At the Hours:** At all the hours: Resurrectional troparion, Glory... Troparion of the forefeast.  
Kontakia: at the First and Sixth Hours – Kontakion of the Forefeast of the Meeting; at the Third and Ninth Hours – Resurrectional kontakion.
