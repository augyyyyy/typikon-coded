# TYPICON: SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 1st, 2026.
## FOREFEAST OF THE ENCOUNTER - SUNDAY OF THE PRODIGAL SON - TONE I
Martyr Tryphon.  
Sunday service combined with the triodion, and that of the forefeast.  
The service to St. Martyr Tryphon. is transferred to the previous Friday at Compline, or another convenient time, whenever the ecclesiarch so wishes.  
**Vestment colour:** Bright [Gold or blue for the forefeast].  
## GREAT VESPERS
**At O Lord, I have cried…** we sing 4 resurrectional stichera from the Octoechos, 3 Stichera from the Triodion, 3 Forefeast from the Menaion; Glory... doxasticon from the Triodion; Both now... Dogmatic Theotokion in the tone of the week.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 1 Aposticha Resurrection 1 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 2 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 3 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 4 from the Octoechos; Glory... doxasticon from the Triodion; Both now... doxasticon of the forefeast.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:**
We sing We sing the Sunday (resurrectional) troparion in the tone of the week; Glory, both now... Theotokion Dismissal in Tone 1.  
Or, if Vigil is served: Rejoice, O Virgin Theotokos... twice; troparion of the forefeast, once.  
## MATINS
After each cathisma we sing the sessional hymns from the Octoechos.  
We sing the Polyeleos, and we add Psalm 136 (By the waters of Babylon). Magnification: sung if prescribed.  
After, the Evlogitaria: the Hypakoë (Tone I), Hymns of Ascents, Prokeimenon, Let everything that has breath: in the tone of the week.  
**Matins Gospel I**: Mt 28: 16-20.  
After Psalm 50: instead of the usual hymns, we sing: Glory: Open to me the doors of repentance..., Both now: On the paths of salvation... and after the refrain Have mercy on me, O God, the sticheron: When I think of the many evil things I have done.  
**At the Canon (abbreviated for parish use):**  
  At each ode, the irmos of the resurrection from the Octoechos.  
  1 troparion of the resurrection.  ℟. Glory to Your holy resurrection, O Lord!  
  1 troparion from the Triodion.  ℟.  Have mercy on me, O God, have mercy on me!  
  Glory... both now... feast (from the Menaion).  
  Catabasia of the Encounter.  
Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Resurrection - 4 (including the irmos), Theotokos - 2, Triodion - 4, Menaion - 4.  Catabasia of the Encounter.  
After Ode III: kontakion and ikos of the feast, and the sessional hymns from the Triodion (found after Ode III), Glory, both now... feast.  
After Ode VI: kontakion and ikos from the Triodion are sung.  
After Ode VIII: we sing the Magnificat.  
Holy is the Lord... Tone I.  
**Exapostilarion** - Eothinon 1; Eothinon 1 Theotokion.  
**At the Praises:** We sing 8 stichera: 5 from the Octoechos, and 3 from the Triodion; Glory... Glory to God; Other: Now and Ever.  
After the Dismissal of Matins: Glory... both now... Gospel Sticheron I.  
## HOURS
At all the hours: Resurrectional troparion, Glory... troparion of the forefeast.
Kontakia: at the First and Sixth – Triodion; at the Third and Ninth – Feast.
## DIVINE LITURGY
Psalms of Typica; Beatitudes on 12: Octoechos - 4; Triodion - 4; forefeast - 4.  
**Troparia and Kontakia:**
Troparion of the Resurrection in Tone 1.
Troparion of the Forefeast.
Glory... Kontakion of the Triodion.
Both now... Kontakion of the Forefeast.  
**Prokimenon:**	Tone I: "Let Your Mercy, O Lord..."
**Epistle:**		1 Cor 6:12-20
**Alleluia:**		Tone I
**Gospel:**		Lk 15:11-32
**Kinonicon:**	"Praise the Lord from the heavens, praise Him in the highest."
