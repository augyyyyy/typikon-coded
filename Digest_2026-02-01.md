# TYPICON: SUNDAY, FEBRUARY 1st, 2026.

SUNDAY OF THE PUBLICAN AND THE PHARISEE - TONE I  
St. Forefeast of the Meeting  
Sunday service combined with that of the Triodion, and that of the forefeast.  
The service to St. Martyr Tryphon. is transferred to the previous Friday at Compline, or another convenient time, whenever the ecclesiarch so wishes.  
Vestment colour: Bright (Gold).  

## GREAT VESPERS

*RUBRIC: Vesting and Censing — [Ordo §29–§30]*  
Vestments (priest): epitrachelion, phelonion - Phelonion blessed and kissed before Entrance. (Ordo §22–§24).  
Fasting Rule: Fast-free week, no fasting restrictions (Dolnytsky Appendix — Fast-free week).  
Censing: Priest censes entire church during Psalm 103 (Dolnytsky I:16 — Great censing at Psalm 103).  
*RUBRIC: The Proemial Psalm — [Ordo §31]*  
Censing: Great censing of the entire church (Dolnytsky I:20 — At 'Lord I have cried').  
**At O Lord, I have cried…** we sing 7 Stichera from the Octoechos, 3 Stichera from the Triodion; Glory, both now... Dogmatic Theotokion in the tone of the week.  
Censing: Small censing at the entrance (Dolnytsky I — At the Entrance).  
Royal Doors: OPEN -  (Ordo §19b).  
*RUBRIC: [Ordo §34]*  
Prokimenon: The Lord is King (Tone VI).  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing the resurrectional aposticha in the tone of the week, from the Octoechos; Glory, both now... Aposticha Theotokion in Tone 1.  

## SMALL COMPLINE

Canon: Theotokos from the Octoechos.  
Troparia: Sunday Troparion from the Octoechos, Troparion of the Temple, *"O God of our fathers..."* and the following three troparia.  

## MIDNIGHT OFFICE (SUNDAY)

*RUBRIC: Triadic Canon — Sing the Triadic Canon in the Tone of the Week.*  
Triadic Canon: Canon Trinity in Tone 1.  
Midnight Troparia: Sunday Hypakoe in Tone 1.  
Prayer: Prayer Holy Trinity All Creating.  

## SUNDAY MATINS

*RUBRIC: Beginning with Vigil*  
*RUBRIC: God is the Lord — [Ordo §77]*  
**At The Lord is God…** we sing: the Sunday (resurrectional) troparion, twice; Glory, both now. Theotokion, once.  
After the Kathismata (1 & 2): We sing: after the 1st (1): the sessional resurrection set 1 in tone 1; after the 2nd (2): the sessional resurrection set 2 in tone 1.  
We sing the Polyeleos.  
*RUBRIC: Hypakoe and Anabathmoi — [Ordo §78]*  
Hypakoe: Hypakoe in Tone 1.  
Anabathmoi (Hymns of Ascents): Anabathmoi in Tone 1.  
Gradual (Anabathmoi): Anabathmoi in Tone 1.  
Prokimenon: Arise, Lord, help us, and redeem us for Thy mercy's sake (Tone I).  
Matins Gospel: Matins Gospel 1 (Eothinon).  
Psalm 50 and Post-Gospel Stichera: Open to me the doors of repentance, Prepare for Me the Ways of Salvation, If I Think Upon the Multitude.  
*RUBRIC: The Canon — Combined from Octoechos, Menaion, and Triodion according to rank.*  
At the Canon: Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Octoechos - 4 (including the irmos), Octoechos - 3, Octoechos - 3, Menaion - 4.  
**Katavasia:** *Katavasia Meeting* (Tone III).  
*RUBRIC: Eighth Ode - Special Refrains — Instead of 'Glory' → 'Let us bless the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit'. Katavasia intro: 'We praise, we bless, we worship the Lord'*  
At Ode 8 (Glory): Glory in Tone 1.  
At Ode IX, we sing the Magnificat ('My soul magnifies the Lord...') and the refrains ('More honorable than the Cherubim...').  
*RUBRIC: Post-Ode 9 Hymn — Non-Sunday: 'It is truly meet'. Sunday: 'Holy is the Lord our God' (3x)*  
Holy is the Lord our God (3x).  
Exapostilarion: Holy Is the Lord in Tone 1; Gospel Exapostilarion I; Theotokion of Gospel Exapostilarion I.  
At the Praises, we sing 39 stichera: 39 from the Octoechos; Glory... Praises Glory Gospel 1; Both now... Praises Both Now in Tone 1.  
*RUBRIC: Great Doxology Logic — [Ordo §82]*  
Doxology: Sunday Resurrection (Sung).  
*RUBRIC: Post-Doxology Rite — For Cross Veneration etc.*  
At the Dismissal Troparion, we sing: Sunday Dismissal Troparion 'Today salvation has come to the world' in Tone I; Troparion of Forefeast of the Meeting.  
*RUBRIC: After the dismissal: Glory, Gospel Sticheron; Both now, 'Most Blessed art Thou'*  
We sing: Glory... Gospel Sticheron I in Tone 5; Both now... Most Blessed Art Thou.  

## HOURS

**At the Hours:** At the First and Sixth Hours: Resurrectional troparion, Glory... troparion of the forefeast; At the Third and Ninth Hours: Resurrectional troparion, Glory... troparion of Forefeast of the Meeting.  
Kontakia: at the First and Sixth Hours – Feast; at the Third and Ninth Hours – Resurrection.
