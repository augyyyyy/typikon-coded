# § INLINE RUBRICS

Small Vespers is an abbreviated form of Vespers served only before an All-Night Vigil (meaning it is typically served on Saturday afternoons or the eves of Great Feasts). It anticipates the longer Great Vespers that will follow shortly after.

---

## 1. The Opening
**JSON node:** `beginning_small`

The priest, vested in the epitrachelion, stands before the closed holy doors and exclaims *"Blessed is our God..."*
Following the introductory prayers (*"O Heavenly King,"* Trisagion, etc.) and *"Come, let us worship"*, Psalm 103 is read simply by the reader. 
*(The Great Litany and the Kathisma are completely omitted).*

---

## 2. Lord, I Have Cried
**JSON node:** `lord_i_have_cried_4`

"Lord, I have cried" is chanted with only **four (4) stichera**.

- *On Saturday evening:* 4 Resurrection stichera of the tone of the week. ☨ Glory... Now: The Dogmatikon of the tone.
- *On the eve of a Feast:* 4 Festal stichera. ☨ Glory... Now: The Festal doxastikon.

---

## 3. O Joyful Light (No Entrance)
**JSON node:** `entrance_suppression`

**There is never an Entrance at Small Vespers.**
The hymn *"O Joyful Light"* is read.

---

## 4. The Prokeimenon
**JSON node:** `prokeimenon_small`

The daily Prokeimenon of the day of the week is sung.

---

## 5. Vouchsafe, O Lord
**JSON node:** `vouchsafe_read`

*"Vouchsafe, O Lord, to keep us this evening without sin..."* is read by the reader.

---

## 6. The Aposticha
**JSON node:** `aposticha`

The stichera of the Aposticha are sung.
- *On Saturday evening:* The Resurrectional Aposticha of the tone. ☨ Glory... Now: Theotokion.
- *On the eve of a Feast:* The Festal Aposticha. ☨ Glory... Now: Festal doxastikon.

---

## 7. Conclusion
**JSON nodes:** `nunc_dimittis_read`, `trisagion`, `troparia`, `dismissal`

*"Now lettest Thou Thy servant depart in peace"* is read.
Following the Trisagion and *"Our Father"*, the Troparia are sung.
- *On Saturday evening:* Resurrection Troparion. ☨ Glory... Now: Theotokion.
- *On the eve of a Feast:* Festal Troparion. ☨ Glory... Now: Festal Theotokion.

The Small Litany (*"Have mercy on us, O God..."*) is intoned.
The Small Dismissal is given.

## Appendix A: Serving Context

Small Vespers is a monastic allowance for days when an All-Night Vigil will be celebrated. Because an All-Night Vigil amalgamates Great Vespers, Matins, and the First Hour into one continuous service—and historically lasted throughout the night—the Typikon prescribes Small Vespers to be served in the late afternoon. This fulfills the requirement for the daily cycle of Vespers before the monks retire for a rest prior to the Vigil.

In modern parish practice, Small Vespers is almost never served, as the All-Night Vigil (or standalone Great Vespers) is typically celebrated earlier in the evening. However, the rubrical structures for Small Vespers remain an integrated part of the full Typikon framework.
