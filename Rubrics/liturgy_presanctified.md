## 1. The Vespers Portion
**JSON node:** `vespers_portion`

The Presanctified Liturgy is structurally joined to Lenten Vespers.
- The service begins with the opening exclamation of the Divine Liturgy: *"Blessed is the Kingdom..."* instead of the Vespers blessing.
- During the Kathisma (*"Unto the Lord in my affliction"*), the priest transfers the Presanctified Lamb from the holy table to the prothesis table in silence.
- Following *"O Joyful Light"* and the 1st Prokeimenon, the reader reads the reading from Genesis.
- **The Blessing:** The priest takes a lighted candle and the censer, blesses the people, and exclaims: *"The Light of Christ illumines all!"* The people make a prostration.
- The reader reads the reading from Proverbs.

## 2. "Let my prayer arise"
**JSON node:** *(End of Vespers Portion)*

The priest stands before the holy doors and sings *"Let my prayer arise in Thy sight as incense..."*
The choir repeats the refrain after each of the following four verses, during which the people kneel or make prostrations.
Following the final singing, three great prostrations are made.

---

## 3. The Litanies
**JSON node:** `litanies_catechumens_photizomenoi`

The Litany of Fervent Supplication is intoned.
This is followed by the Litany for the Catechumens.
*From Wednesday of the 4th Week of Lent onwards*, a special additional Litany for those Preparing for Illumination (the Photizomenoi) is intoned.

---

## 4. The Great Entrance
**JSON node:** `great_entrance_presanctified`

Instead of the Cherubic Hymn, the choir sings *"Now the Powers of Heaven do serve invisibly with us..."*

The Great Entrance is made. Because the Holy Gifts are already consecrated (Presanctified), the transfer is made **in absolute silence**. 
- The priest carries the Holy Gifts on his head, or elevated in his hands.
- The deacon precedes with the censer, censing backwards toward the Gifts.
- **Rubric:** They exit via the northern door and proceed directly to the holy doors. They do NOT stop in the center of the church to make commemorations.
- **Rubric:** The people must kneel, touching their foreheads to the floor in profound adoration during the silent transfer. (It is strictly forbidden to stand or bow simply during this entrance).

Once the priest places the Gifts on the altar, he says nothing, but simply covers them and censes them. The choir concludes the hymn: *"Let us draw near with faith and love..."*
Three prostrations are then made.

---

## 5. The Lord's Prayer and Communion
**JSON nodes:** `lords_prayer`, `communion_presanctified`

Following the Litany of Supplication, *"Our Father"* is sung.
The priest exclaims *"Presanctified Holy Things for the Holy!"*

The Communion Hymn (Kinonikon) is sung: *"Taste and see that the Lord is good. Alleluia."*

The priest consumes the Presanctified Gifts (no breaking of the Lamb is required unless communicating the faithful). *If the priest serves alone, he does not drink from the chalice at this exact moment, as the wine is completely unconsecrated.*

---

## 6. Post-Communion and Dismissal
**JSON node:** `dismissal_presanctified`

Instead of *"We have seen the true light,"* the choir sings *"I will bless the Lord at all times..."*

The Ambo Prayer of the Presanctified (*"O Almighty Master, Who hast made all creation..."*) is read.
The choir sings *"Blessed be the name of the Lord" (3x)* and Psalm 33 is read.

The priest gives the Dismissal.

## Appendix A: The Typikon of the Gifts

The defining feature of the Presanctified Liturgy is that there is **no Anaphora (Eucharistic Prayer)**. The bread and wine are not consecrated during this service.

### 1. Preparation of the Lamb
The Lamb used for the Presanctified Liturgy is consecrated on the preceding Sunday at the Liturgy of St. Basil (or St. John Chrysostom). During that Sunday Liturgy, the priest prepares an extra Lamb (or two), intinction it with the Precious Blood, and reserves it in an artophorion (tabernacle) on the holy table.

### 2. The Chalice at the Presanctified
During the proskomedia of the Presanctified Liturgy, the deacon pours wine and water into the chalice. However, **this wine is never consecrated**. It remains ordinary wine and water, serving only to facilitate the swallowing of the Presanctified Body.

Therefore, if a priest celebrates the Presanctified Liturgy without a deacon, he communions himself of the Body, but does not drink the wine from the chalice until the ablutions (the clearing of the chalice at the end of the service).
