# TYPICON: SUNDAY, MARCH 1st, 2026.
## SUNDAY II OF THE GREAT FAST: ST. GREGORY PALAMAS - TONE V
Monk-Martyr Eudokia.  
Sunday service combined with the triodion.  
The service to St. Monk-Martyr Eudokia. is transferred to the previous Friday at Compline, or another convenient time, whenever the ecclesiarch so wishes.  
**Vestment colour:** Bright [deep gold, or, in some places, gold with purple].  
## GREAT VESPERS
**At O Lord, I have cried…** we sing 6 resurrectional stichera from the Octoechos, 4 Stichera from the Triodion; Glory... doxasticon from the Triodion; Both now... Dogmatic Theotokion in the tone of the week.  
**If Litiya is performed:** The sticheron of the temple.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 1 Aposticha Resurrection 1 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 2 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 3 from the Octoechos, 1 Aposticha Resurrection 4 from the Octoechos; Glory... doxasticon from the Triodion; Both now... Theotokion Aposticha in Tone 8.  
**At the Dismissal Troparia:**
We sing We sing the Sunday (resurrectional) troparion in the tone of the week; Glory, both now... Theotokion Dismissal in Tone 5.  
Or, if Vigil is served: Rejoice, O Virgin Theotokos... twice, troparion of the Saint, once.  
## MATINS
After each cathisma we sing the sessional hymns from the Octoechos.  
We sing the 17th Kathisma (Psalm 118).  
After, the Evlogitaria: the Hypakoë (Tone V), Hymns of Ascents, Prokeimenon, Let everything that has breath: in the tone of the week.  
**Matins Gospel V**: Lk: 24:12-35.  
After Psalm 50: instead of the usual hymns, we sing: Glory: Open to me the doors of repentance..., Both now: On the paths of salvation... and after the refrain Have mercy on me, O God, the sticheron: When I think of the many evil things I have done.  
**At the Canon (abbreviated for parish use):**  
  At each ode, the irmos of the resurrection from the Octoechos.  
  3 troparia from canon of the Triodion.  ℟.  Glory to Your precious Cross, O Lord!  
  Glory... both now... Triodion.  
  Catabasia Tone I "Moses the servant of God . . . "  
Full order of the canon (according to the typicon): Resurrection - 4 (including the irmos), Triodion - 4, Triodion Saint - 6.  
After Ode III: Sessional hymns from the Triodion (found after Ode III).  
After Ode VI: kontakion and ikos of the Cross, from the Triodion, in Tone VII.  
After Ode VIII: we sing the Magnificat.  
Holy is the Lord... Tone V.  
**Exapostilarion** - Eothinon 5; Eothinon 5 Theotokion.  
**At the Praises:** We sing 9 stichera: 5 from the Octoechos, and 4 from the Triodion; Glory... Glory to God; Other: Now and Ever.  
After the Dismissal of Matins: Glory... both now... Gospel Sticheron V.  
## HOURS
At all the hours: Resurrectional troparion, Glory... Trop Triodion Saint.
Kontakia: at the First and Sixth – Triodion; at the Third and Ninth – St. Gregory Palamas.
