# TYPICON: TUESDAY, JUNE 9th, 2026.

ST. CYRIL, ARCHBISHOP OF ALEXANDRIA - TONE VIII  
St. Cyril  
Tuesday service combined with that of St. Cyril.  
Vestment colour: Bright (Green).  

## DAILY VESPERS

At Vespers, Kathisma 18 is read.  
**At O Lord, I have cried…** we sing 6 stichera to the Saint; Glory... doxasticon of the Saint; Both now... usual Theotokion in the tone of the doxasticon and of the day of the week.  
Prokimenon: Tone I: "Thy mercy, O Lord, shall follow me all the days of my life"  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 daily aposticha from the Octoechos; Glory, both now... Aposticha Theotokion.  
At the Dismissal Troparia: We sing the troparion of the Saint; Glory, both now... Dismissal Theotokion in the tone of the Saint's troparion and of the day of the week.  

## SMALL COMPLINE

Canon: Theotokos from the Octoechos.  
Troparia: Tuesday Troparion from the Octoechos, Troparion of the Temple, Troparion 'O God of our fathers' block.  

## MIDNIGHT OFFICE (DAILY)

Midnight Troparia: Troparion of the weekday, Troparion of the Temple, Troparion of the Saint (if any), daily Theotokion.  
Prayer: Prayer Mardarius.  

## DAILY MATINS

At The Lord is God... we sing the troparion of St. Cyril, twice; Glory, both now... Dismissal Theotokion in the tone of the Saint's troparion and of the day of the week.  
After the Kathismata (1 & 2): We sing the sessional hymns from the octoechos.  
At the Canon: Order of the Canon: First Canon of the Octoechos with the Heirmos on 4; second Canon of the Octoechos on 4; Canon of the Saint on 6. Katavasia: Heirmos of the last canon (of the Saint) after Odes 3, 6, 8, and 9.  
Note: At the 9th Ode, the priest censes as at Great Matins.  
**At the Aposticha:** We sing 3 daily aposticha from the Octoechos; Glory, both now... Aposticha Theotokion.  
At the Dismissal Troparia: Troparion of St. Cyril; Glory, both now... Dismissal Theotokion for Tuesday in Tone I.  

## HOURS

Troparia: First Hour – Day; Third & Ninth Hours – St. Cyril; Sixth Hour – Temple. Kontakia: First Hour – Day; Third & Ninth Hours – St. Cyril; Sixth Hour – Temple.  

## DIVINE LITURGY OF ST. JOHN CHRYSOSTOM

Psalms of Typica; Beatitudes on 6: 3 from the Octoechos, 3 from Ode III of the Saint.  
Troparia and Kontakia:  
Troparion of the Forerunner.  
Troparion of St. Cyril.  
Kontakion of the Forerunner.  
Glory... Kontakion of St. Cyril.  
Both now... Kontakion of the Temple.  
**Prokimenon:** Tone 7: "The righteous shall rejoice in the Lord and shall hope in Him"  
**Epistle:** Romans 7:14-8:2  
**Alleluia:** Tone 4, with verses of the day.  
**Gospel:** Matthew 10:9-15  
**Communion Hymn:** "In everlasting remembrance shall the righteous be"  
Post-Communion Hymn: 'We have seen the true light, we have received the heavenly Spirit...' (We Have Seen True Light)  
Dismissal: May Christ our true God
