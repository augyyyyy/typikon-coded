# Canonical Maximalist Typikon Digest Standard (26 General Formats Edition)

> **Authority**: 2010 Lviv Typikon; Dolnytsky Typikon Parts I–V (1899); Ordo Celebrationis (1944/1996).  
> **Classification**: The 26 General Liturgical Formats (20 Non-Triodion + 6 Triodia General Paradigms).  
> **Goal**: 100% consistent data presentation across every card of any day, where every card deterministically evaluates to `PASS`.

---

## 1. Hierarchy of Canonical Authority (Rule 2)
1. **Ordo Celebrationis** (Rome, 1944 / 1996) — Primary standard for service choreography, entrances, litanies, and hierarchy.
2. **Dolnytsky Typikon Parts II–V** (Lviv, 1899) — Specific rubrics for Daily Cycle (Pt. II), Menaion (Pt. III), Triodion (Pt. IV), and Pentecostarion (Pt. V).
3. **2010 Lviv Typikon** — The authoritative modern UGCC codification.
4. **Liturgicon & Horologion** — Verbatim prayers, litanies, and ordos.

## 2. Universal 6-Tier Data Presentation Schema for Every Card

Every service card generated in the **Service Rubrics Digest** MUST render in this exact 6-tier uniform structure:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ [TIER 1] CARD HEADER & BADGES                                               │
│ • Service Title (e.g. "DAILY VESPERS", "GREAT MATINS (ALL-NIGHT VIGIL)")    │
│ • Appointed Vestment Color Badge & Fasting Rule Badge (if service-specific) │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [TIER 2] OPENING & ENTRANCE CHOREOGRAPHY                                    │
│ • Opening Blessing & Psalmody (Psalm 103, Hexapsalmos, or Royal Office)     │
│ • Sanctuary / Door State & Entrance Type (No Entrance, Censer, Gospel Book)  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [TIER 3] PSALMODY & KATHISMA DETERMINATION                                  │
│ • Appointed Kathismata numbers & Sessional Hymns (with Tone and Theotokion) │
│ • Kathisma Omission rules (e.g. post-Vigil omission, Lenten 3-Kathisma rule)│
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [TIER 4] CORE HYMN STACK & PROPORTIONAL RATIOS                              │
│ • Exact Hymn counts and distribution (Octoechos vs. Menaion vs. Triodion)   │
│ • Doxastikon (Glory) & Theotokion / Dogmatikon (Both now) with Tone Matching│
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [TIER 5] SCRIPTURE READINGS & LITANIES                                      │
│ • Prokeimena (Tone, verses, refrains) & Paremias / Epistle / Gospel pericopes│
│ • Litanies sequence (Great, Small, Fervent, Supplication, Special petitions)│
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ [TIER 6] DISMISSAL & CONCLUDING APODOSIS                                    │
│ • Dismissal Troparia chain (Resurrection -> Feast -> Patron -> Saint)       │
│ • Dismissal Theotokion in the appointed tone                                │
│ • Priest's Final Benediction naming all commemorated saints                 │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Part II: The 6 Triodia General Formats (Dolnytsky Parts IV & V)
| Format ID | Title & Scope | Vespers Stichera | Matins Canon & Katavasia | Matins Doxology | Liturgy Ordo |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **FORMAT 21** | **Weekdays (Mon–Fri) of the Triodion & Great Fast** | 6 (3 Triodion + 3 Menaion) [or Presanctified 10 (6 Triodion + 4 Menaion)] | 3-Ode Triodia Canons (Odes appointed for day); Katavasia: Irmos of 3rd Canon | Small Doxology (Read) + Ephrem prostrations | Presanctified Liturgy on Wed/Fri; No Liturgy Mon/Tue/Thu |
| **FORMAT 22** | **Weekday during Great Fast with Polyeleos, Vigil, or Patronal Saint** | 8 (4 Triodion + 4 Saint) [or Presanctified 10 (4 Triodion + 6 Saint)] | 12 (4 Theotokos + 8 Saint); Katavasia: Season / Saint | Great Doxology (Chanted) if Feast allows | Presanctified Liturgy (or Chrysostom Liturgy if Annunciation) |
| **FORMAT 23** | **Sundays during the Triodion (Publican & Pharisee to 5th Sunday of Lent)** | 10 (6/4 Octoechos + 4/6 Triodion; Glory: Triodion; Both: Dogmatikon) | 14 (4 Res + 2 Theotokos + 8 Triodion); Katavasia: Seasonal Lenten Table | Great Doxology (Chanted) + Sunday Dismissal Troparion | Liturgy of St. Basil the Great (Lent 1–5); Chrysostom on Pre-Lent |
| **FORMAT 24** | **Weekdays during the Paschal Season** | 6 (3 Paschal/Octoechos + 3 Menaion; Glory/Both: Paschal/Theotokion) | 12 (6 Paschal/Feast + 6 Saint); Katavasia: Paschal Canon | Small Doxology (Read) | Paschal Divine Liturgy (Antiphons, Paschal Troparion, *"As many as have been baptized"*) |
| **FORMAT 25** | **Sundays during the Paschal Season (Pascha to Ascension)** | 10 (4/3 Octoechos + 3/4 Pentecostarion + 3 Saint); Dogmatikon | 14 (4 Res + 2 Theotokos + 8 Pentecostarion); Katavasia: Pascha | Great Doxology (Chanted) | Paschal Divine Liturgy; Ode 9 Paschal Refrains replace Magnificat |
| **FORMAT 26** | **Polyeleos Saint, Vigil Saint, or Patronal Saint on a Weekday in Paschal Season** | 8 (4 Paschal + 4 Saint; Glory: Saint; Both: Paschal Dogmatikon) | 12 (4 Paschal + 8 Saint); Katavasia: Paschal Canon | Great Doxology (Chanted) | Divine Liturgy with Paschal Prokeimena & Saint Readings |
